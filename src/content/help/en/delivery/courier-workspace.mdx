Admin → Orders → Courier mode, or assigned courier workspace

<span id="itinerary" />

## Open the assigned itinerary

Open the assigned route and check its date, stop sequence and delivery windows. Use only orders assigned to your authorized courier context.

<span id="navigate" />

## Navigate and contact

Open an offered navigation app for the stop and review customer contact/delivery instructions. Check a corrected address with dispatch before changing the delivery plan.

<span id="complete" />

## Confirm the actual result

Use the offered stop/order status action after the actual handover. Record required information and verify the saved result; a delivery confirmation does not by itself collect an unpaid balance.
