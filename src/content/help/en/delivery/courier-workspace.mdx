Admin → Orders → Courier mode, or assigned courier workspace

<span id="itinerary" />

## Open the assigned itinerary

Open the assigned route and check its date, stop sequence and delivery windows. Use only orders assigned to your authorized courier context.

<span id="navigate" />

## Navigate and contact

Open an offered navigation app for the stop and review customer contact/delivery instructions. Check a corrected address with dispatch before changing the delivery plan.

<span id="complete" />

## Confirm the actual result

Use the offered stop/order status action after the actual handover. Record required information and verify the saved result; a delivery confirmation does not by itself collect an unpaid balance.

<span id="departure-settings" />

## Edit endpoints and start the route

Before departure, open Edit on your assigned route. Set departure time, departure address and final destination; use the address suggestions or enter a complete address manually. Set the customer window and handover duration separately, then save and review the recalculated sequence. Changing an endpoint resets manual ordering. Start route confirms that the assigned unfinished deliveries are leaving and changes accepted, preparing and ready deliveries together to Out for delivery. Finished/cancelled deliveries are not reopened. A held kitchen course or an unsettled weight order must be resolved first. If departure fails, no partial status change is saved. A courier can have only one active route; finish it before starting another. Before departure, departure and final addresses remain editable after allocation. Include the house number, street and city; the number may appear before or after the street name, and the displayed address is retained. If Address not found appears, check the number and city, then select another suggestion or correct the full address. A temporary location-service failure requires trying again later. The saved route remains unchanged on either failure. When Google is active in Maps and routes, departure and final addresses can use a precise Google match even when Geoapify cannot locate them. Save the route settings before departure and review the recalculated final leg. If Google is unavailable or its protected quota is exhausted and the other provider cannot locate an endpoint, saving or recalculation is blocked and the previous plan is retained; the final destination is never silently omitted. Google-only points are shown on the Google map and hidden when the map switches provider. Select a precisely located alternative or try again when Google becomes available. Enable Respect order time windows only when saved order times are agreed customer commitments. Disabled by default, this option allows stop optimization before deriving customer arrival ranges. Changing it recalculates even a previous manual sequence; review the result before departure.

<span id="routing-service-limits" />

## Respond to a mapping service limit

An exhausted-quota or unavailable-service warning does not mean the customer address is wrong. Known Google positions remain on the Google map while their cache is valid; unchecked positions are marked temporarily unavailable. Paths and arrivals that cannot be calculated stay hidden. Ask dispatch to check Usage and quotas and wait for service availability, then refresh and recalculate if needed. Do not remove a delivery to dismiss the warning. Orders and addresses are retained. Review recalculated times before contacting customers.
