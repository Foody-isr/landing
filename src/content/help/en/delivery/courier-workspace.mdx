Admin → Orders → Courier mode, or assigned courier workspace

<span id="itinerary" />

## Open the assigned itinerary

In Foody Admin courier mode, choose your assigned route from the top list. The active route is selected on opening. Before departure the list shows order numbers and any calculated times. Customer addresses, contact details, instructions and map pins stay hidden. Finish time, distance and duration are plan estimates. Open Total duration for the available breakdown.

Courier mode follows the itinerary saved by the restaurant. The order amount appears for the open stop when the payment is in cash and the amount is known.

<span id="navigate" />

## Navigate and contact

After departure, select a stop by order number and choose Open this stop. Foody records the current stop and shows its address, pin when available, phone number and instructions. You can call the customer, open WhatsApp or choose Waze or Google Maps from the action bar. If Foody warns about an unverified address, check it with the restaurant before confirming the search.

When you return from navigation, the open stop remains available, including after a reload. Save Mark delivered or Report a problem to close this stop and open the next one.

<span id="complete" />

## Confirm the actual result

When the order has been handed over, check the stop number and address in the fixed bar and choose Mark delivered. If you cannot deliver it, choose Report a problem, select a reason and save. The restaurant can review the order. The next stop becomes available after either result is saved. The route closes when every stop has a result. Mark arrived is optional, and a delivery confirmation does not collect an unpaid balance. If Foody refuses to save because the route or assignment changed, refresh and check the current assignment before retrying.

<span id="departure-settings" />

## Check the plan and start the route

Before leaving, check the itinerary saved by the restaurant. Courier mode no longer offers Refine, route settings, Reoptimize, address correction or stop reordering. Planned departure, start and end points, handover times, customer windows and route composition can only be changed by an authorized dispatcher in Orders → Deliveries. This also applies to an owner or superadmin viewing courier mode; return to Deliveries to plan. An old interface cannot save these changes through courier actions.

Start route confirms the actual departure of unfinished assigned deliveries and moves accepted, preparing or ready orders together to Out for delivery. Finished or cancelled orders are not reopened. A preparation hold or an unfinalized weighted order must be resolved first. Failure makes no partial change. A courier can have only one active route; finish it before starting another. If a destination could not be verified, ask the dispatcher to check its address or GPS point before confirming departure.

At departure choose Start and open Waze to record departure, open the first stop and launch Waze. If you choose Start, addresses stay hidden until you choose Open this stop.

<span id="routing-service-limits" />

## Respond to a mapping service limit

A quota or service-unavailable warning does not mean the customer address is wrong. The open stop’s known point remains on the Google map while its cache is valid; unverified positions are flagged as temporarily unavailable. Unavailable geometry and arrival calculations stay hidden. Ask the dispatcher to review Usage and quotas, then refresh when service returns. Retry in courier mode reloads information without recalculating or changing the itinerary. Any necessary recalculation is performed by the dispatcher in Deliveries. Orders and their addresses are preserved. Check updated times before contacting customers.

<span id="map-timeline-panel" />

## Adjust the map and find a stop

On mobile, drag the handle above the list to choose a compact, balanced or expanded view. Keyboard Up/Down and Home/End provide the same adjustment. The list scrolls independently of the fixed actions. Search for hidden stops by order number. Address and contact search applies to the open stop. Select its pin to find it in the list. Fit the whole route reframes the map; + and − control zoom. Viewing another route does not stop active-route location sharing when the phone permits it.

<span id="accidental-departure" />

## Correct a test departure

If you start a route accidentally, tell a manager before recording any arrival or delivery. In Orders → Deliveries, a user with order-management permission can choose Undo departure while every stop is pending, then unassign and reallocate the route. Out for delivery orders return to In preparation and the replacement courier can start after reassignment. Courier mode does not offer this management correction. Previously sent messages remain in the history. Undo departure is also unavailable once navigation to a stop has been recorded.

<span id="superadmin-courier-selection" />

## View another courier’s workspace

With compatible API and Admin versions, sign in as the relevant restaurant’s owner or as a superadmin, then open Orders → Courier mode. Under View as courier, select a courier from that restaurant; the first in the list is selected initially. You see the same map, unfinished routes and stops as that courier. Change the selection to display the new courier’s routes; the previous selected stop, search and panels are reset. Displayed actions, including Start route and Mark delivered, really change that courier’s deliveries: use them only to record work that has taken place. The signed-in account keeps its identity and its GPS position is not reported as the courier’s. Owning another restaurant does not grant this selector here; other accounts keep their own routes. If no couriers are configured, check the team roles. If loading fails, choose Retry; a removed membership or a courier from another restaurant is rejected by the server. If the owner has no selector, have both compatible versions checked, deploying API before Admin. This workspace remains for execution: itinerary settings are unavailable even for an owner or superadmin. Return to Orders → Deliveries to change the plan.

<span id="live-location" />

## Check the courier’s live position

The courier must open Foody on their phone with their own account, start their route and allow location access. Keep Foody visible with an Internet connection. GPS readings are sent to Foody at most once every ten seconds, subject to browser updates; this frequency is not guaranteed. The marker titled Courier position shows the last received location in Deliveries and Courier mode. The green triangle remains the planned departure point. The displayed time is when the position was received; after more than one minute, it is labelled old and the marker fades. A message identifies a missing position. Courier mode distinguishes waiting for GPS, denied permission, unavailable GPS and transmission failure. Correct permissions or connectivity, then choose Retry. These states never confirm a delivery. Opening Waze, switching apps or locking the phone can interrupt web tracking: return to Foody to resume. View as courier displays the selected courier’s received position and never sends the manager’s location. Sharing GPS and moving the marker make no Google calls; map background loads and route recalculations retain their own usage.

The courier is represented by a round avatar above delivery pins, with an always-visible label. An old position retains its avatar and the Old courier position label. Initial framing includes the last received position; in Courier mode, Fit the whole route brings it back into view with the open stop. GPS updates move the avatar without automatically recentering the map.

<span id="route-reminders" />

## Enable route reminders

In your courier workspace menu, open Route reminders and choose Enable notifications on the phone you use to deliver. On iPhone, open Foody from its Home Screen icon before enabling. If permission is blocked, allow notifications in your device settings. A reminder from your restaurant opens the delivery workspace so you can check the actual result.
