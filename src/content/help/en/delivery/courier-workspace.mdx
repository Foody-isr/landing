Admin → Orders → Courier mode, or assigned courier workspace

<span id="itinerary" />

## Open the assigned itinerary

Open the assigned route and check its date, stop sequence and delivery windows. Use only orders assigned to your authorized courier context.

<span id="navigate" />

## Navigate and contact

Open an offered navigation app for the stop and review customer contact/delivery instructions. Check a corrected address with dispatch before changing the delivery plan.

<span id="complete" />

## Confirm the actual result

Use the offered stop/order status action after the actual handover. Record required information and verify the saved result; a delivery confirmation does not by itself collect an unpaid balance.

<span id="departure-settings" />

## Edit endpoints and start the route

Before departure, open Edit on your assigned route. Set departure time, departure address and final destination; use the address suggestions or enter a complete address manually. Set the customer window and handover duration separately, then save and review the recalculated sequence. Changing an endpoint resets manual ordering. Start route confirms that the assigned unfinished deliveries are leaving and changes accepted, preparing and ready deliveries together to Out for delivery. Finished/cancelled deliveries are not reopened. A held kitchen course or an unsettled weight order must be resolved first. If departure fails, no partial status change is saved. A courier can have only one active route; finish it before starting another.
