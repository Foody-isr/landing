<span id="verifone-admin-money-operations" />

## Refund or void a Verifone online payment

The Admin order payment section supports total or partial matched refunds and total VOID for an eligible single Verifone online payment, for owners/managers with payments.manage. Provider state, captured amount and verified history govern eligibility, not a fixed 24-hour rule. This does not cancel preparation or fulfillment and does not add a POS terminal refund button. Unknown results stay reserved and must be checked, never resubmitted. Invoice4U credit/cancellation documents require a separate check. See [the online payment procedure](/en/help/payments/how-payments-work#verifone-admin-refund-void).

A captured ABS sale still AUTHORISED/AUTHORIZED or SETTLEMENT_REQUESTED is not settled: Admin shows Available to void and only Void entire payment, with no prior refund. Refund payment and Available to refund require an explicit settled state or verified partially-refunded history. Generic APPROVED/COMPLETED, contradictory states or missing evidence offer neither action. A state change before confirmation rejects the stale action; it never switches VOID to REFUND automatically. Request rejected by Verifone means no return of funds is confirmed, not necessarily an issuer decline.

<span id="weight-hold-margin" />

## Review the by-weight authorization ceiling

For online by-weight orders, open Settings → Payments & VAT with settings.edit. Set Weight hold buffer % from 0 to 100 and save; the default for new restaurants is 20. A failed save keeps the draft. Zero still authorizes the estimate. Only the weight-priced subtotal gets a margin: 100 weighted + 50 fixed at 20% authorizes 170; delivery has no extra margin. Checkout shows the estimate, temporary ceiling and Confirm and authorize, in the restaurant theme. A changed ceiling requires customer review and another confirmation before payment; unknown policy blocks authorization. Existing reviewed checkouts and holds keep their ceiling. Staff confirm measured weights before capture and progression; excess beyond the hold is flagged for a separate payment, never automatically charged. Cash/pay-later makes no hold promise. Requires a configured authorize/capture provider, with partial capture, release and expiry validated before production. This neither changes the catering deposit/balance policy nor enables new-card saving with a CST weighted hold.

<span id="date" />

## Select deliveries for one date

Use the date or arrows to choose the service day. Available orders follow the calendar for their address. Check the allowed slot again after an address change. Postal groups with the same fee and options can combine delivery days. See [postal day groups](/en/help/delivery/zones-and-schedules#postal-day-groups).

In the sidebar, open Deliveries. Dispatch shows orders waiting to be planned, planned departures, routes on the road and delivered orders. Search the queue by customer, city or order. Select all acts on visible results. Search the routes and history by courier. Open Map to locate stops and List to return to assignments.

<span id="dispatch-workspace" />

## Read the dispatch board

Choose the service day. **Route** lists remaining stops, **Unassigned** shows available orders and **Addresses to review** lists entrances that need attention. These stops stay assigned to their drivers. Search an order number, customer, street or city. The table shows each stop’s position and arrival window. Select its address or its map marker for access instructions and **Correct address**. Hiding the map keeps your filter and selection. Open a stop with missing coordinates from its table row.

<span id="route-history" />

## Find a past route

Open Deliveries and choose **History**. Routes appear newest first across all service dates. A past route whose courier has not confirmed departure keeps its pending departure status. Routes completed today are also listed. Select a courier, then a route to see its stops and map. **Show older routes** continues with the same filter. In the detail, use the route selector or **Back to history**. In the active view, when several routes share a day, choose the courier and route number above the table to jump directly to its stops.

<span id="assign" />

## Assign couriers and stops

Select the intended courier, depot and orders, then review stop addresses, service times and promised windows. Correct an address before optimization.

<span id="insert-planned-delivery" />

## Add a late order to a route

In **Dispatch**, choose the day and **Add a delivery**. When several orders are available, choose **Add to a route** on the order you need. Choose a planned route and a position at the start, after a stop or at the end. Check the adjacent stops in the preview and confirm. The order appears in that position and the other stops keep their order. If the location is missing or Google is slow, check the calculation status. If the response was interrupted, reopen the plan to see the assignment. If the driver has started, check the updated route before choosing another one.

<span id="optimize" />

## Review a calculated route

Calculate the route or fleet allocation, then review the route before departure. By default, saved order slots do not constrain stop order: they may have been assigned automatically. Enable Respect order time windows only when those times have been agreed with customers. When enabled, couriers can receive different stop counts and planning can serve the earliest deadline first; each complete route must satisfy the enabled windows. Distinguish driving, waiting and handover. A rejected plan can concern departure, enabled windows or road access. Review these settings before retrying; recalculations and unsuccessful attempts can consume provider usage.

<span id="order-time-windows" />

## Choose the route time constraints

In Deliveries → Dispatch, open allocation or Edit on a draft route. Respect order time windows is off by default: the route optimizes stop order, then calculates arrivals and the customer communication range. Enable it only for saved times actually agreed with customers. The choice is saved per route; changing it and saving recalculates stop order even if it was manual. Existing routes keep their sequence until an explicit recalculation: use Recalculate to correct an existing route. Delivery dates remain enforced and saved order slots are not erased. Review the new estimates and reopen reminder previews before sending. An already sent message is not sent again automatically. For new same-day orders, the receiving estimate now follows the matched zone’s saved same-day days, time mode and transport allowance, within restaurant availability and product readiness. An address in an advance-only zone cannot request delivery today. Review the frozen order estimate and confirm a valid time after checking the courier. Editing zone rules does not recalculate an existing order promise or dispatch a WhatsApp message.

<span id="dispatch" />

## Save and track progress

Save the checked route, then dispatch it. Follow the courier and orders and explicitly update an obsolete plan with the required management access. Courier mode provides a map, timeline and route selector. The fixed dock offers Start, then navigation and delivery confirmation. Courier mode cannot change settings, reoptimize, correct an address or change stop order or route composition. Dispatchers make these changes in Deliveries → Dispatch. Selecting a pin or searching for a stop does not change the saved order.

In the courier view, the order amount is shown only for cash payments. Card payments, other payment methods and orders with an unknown payment method do not show an amount.

The fixed action bar shows the selected stop or, once navigation has started, the stop awaiting a result. An obsolete assignment cannot be confirmed. Refresh to check the current route.

<span id="correct-delivery-address" />

## Correct a stop’s delivery address

In Deliveries → Dispatch, open the stop and choose **Correct address**. Select the result in the intended city. If Google identifies only the street, you can save it and keep or enter the house number in the form. You can also type a numbered address as “street and number, city”. Saving updates the order and queues the route calculation. For a delivery to someone else, clear the customer profile checkbox. If you know the precise entrance, you can save its coordinates and allow reuse for this building.

<span id="endpoints" />

## Set each courier’s departure and final address

Before departure, edit the route in Deliveries → Dispatch, or define each courier’s endpoints during allocation. Type at least three characters in Departure address or Final destination and select a suggestion. A city alone, such as Raanana, uses its center; choose a complete address for a precise point. The selected label is retained without adding the city again. Street-only suggestions still require a house number for a precise address. If suggestions are unavailable, type a city or full address manually. Save, then review the recalculated final leg and arrival times. Changing an endpoint resets manual stop order; saved order windows apply only when Respect order time windows is enabled. A city endpoint does not replace precise customer addresses. An unresolved street is never substituted with the city center. If an address cannot be found, select a city suggestion or correct the street, number and city. Failed lookups preserve the saved route. The final destination is never silently omitted. Google-only points appear on the Google map and are hidden when the map switches provider. Select a located alternative or retry when Google is available. Expand Route settings below its stops to edit the journey.

<span id="customer-window" />

## Set the customer delivery range

In allocation, use Customer delivery window beside Time at each stop. New plans propose 90 minutes. Enter 1:30, 1h30 or 90 for an hour and a half; the allowed range is 1–720 minutes. The range begins at the calculated arrival, rounded down to its displayed minute: 08:30 plus 90 minutes gives 08:30–10:00. It does not extend the handover time or change the order’s original scheduling constraints. The setting is saved per route and can be edited before departure. Delivery WhatsApp links and manual/approved reminders use this range. Reopen a reminder after recalculation to review its updated text. Historic routes without this setting retain their saved reminder slot. If a configured route cannot provide a valid arrival, recalculate or correct its address before sending; no guessed range is substituted. This range is calculated after optimization and never becomes a constraint on a later recalculation. Only Respect order time windows enables the slots already saved on orders as arrival constraints. Planning changes are reserved for authorized dispatchers in Deliveries; they are unavailable to courier accounts and in an owner’s or superadmin’s courier workspace.

<span id="whatsapp-media" />

## Attach a file to a WhatsApp reminder

With direct Meta notifications enabled, configure the attachment in Settings → Communications → WhatsApp or on the Order workflow stage: an approved photo/PDF header template, an authorized fixed file or that order’s PDF receipt. This selection changes neither the route nor the configured sending time. Review the file in the manual preview and the attachment filename in Customer messages; a filename does not prove delivery.

<span id="internal-combo-substitution" />

## Make a one-order combo exception

For a one-order combo exception, an authorized Admin user opens the combo step and selects Choose another item. The replacement is included without a surcharge and is saved on that order only. Fulfillment, scheduling and delivery rules remain unchanged. Review the selected dish and portion in the order details. Guest and POS combo pickers retain the configured composition; arrange an exception through the internal Admin order editor. See the combo guide for quantities, eligibility and recovery.

<span id="carte-series-stock" />

## Keep stock provenance when moving fulfillment

Predefined item stocks now have a journal under Items & menus → Stock history and on each product. A rotating carte can prepare initial allowances per series and publish them without restoring already committed units. Previous remaining counts are not carried forward. Order changes and pre-production releases use the original frozen pool; a produced item remains consumed. Ingredient/preparation quantities and recipe costs remain separate. See [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) for initial imports, corrections and history filters.

<span id="routing-service-limits" />

## Understand a pending calculation

A saved correction or insertion may show a pending route calculation. The orders remain visible. If a single stop shows an unknown position, open it and check its street, house number and city. If the mapping service is temporarily unavailable, Foody retries the pending calculation. Review the updated arrival windows before contacting customers.

<span id="related-service-controls" />

## Access the new controls

Customer sign-in invitations are configured separately in Website → Order page → Fulfillment → Show the sign-in invitation on arrival; off retains a discreet Sign in link. This does not change courier assignment or delivery rules.

<span id="delivery-whatsapp-dispatch" />

## Preview and send the selected date’s reminders

In Deliveries → Dispatch, select a date and open WhatsApp reminders to review pending customers across all displayed routes. WhatsApp customer opens only that customer’s reminder. The read-only preview uses the approved reminder template, saved recipient, assigned courier and calculated route arrival window. It can be reviewed early; manual sending remains blocked until the day before delivery in the restaurant’s timezone. Use Send this reminder for one customer, or Send reminders and confirm the number of separate messages for the entire date. No WhatsApp group is created. Previously submitted, in-progress, uncertain and unavailable reminders are excluded. Check blocked customers; a preparation failure does not prevent other eligible customers. A changed route or message stops remaining sends: refresh, review and confirm again. A lost send response remains uncertain and is never retried automatically. Acceptance does not prove delivery; check Customer messages on the order. The current preview does not reconstruct an earlier message body. For automatic sending, open Order workflow and configure the reminder template and Day before timing on the intended stage, for example Ready. Opening previews enables no automation. Automatic, grouped and individual modes share protection against a second reminder for the same order, including after template or provider changes. The list groups reminders by route and courier.

<span id="courier-profile-destination" />

## Use the courier’s saved final destination

Staff → employee → Edit contact details stores the courier’s default final destination, with an option to use the current home address. The planning drawer pre-fills it for a new route, and the API also applies it if a client omits an override. An explicit route destination, including an explicitly empty field, takes precedence. Editing the departure address retains the proposed final destination. Existing routes with deliveries retain their saved settings when a profile changes; empty courier placeholders use the latest default when first planned. Route endpoint validation and recalculation rules still apply. No staff profile edit starts or recalculates an existing route.

<span id="staff-activation-pos-code" />

## POS code at staff activation

Staff activation checks the permissions assigned in this restaurant, including custom roles: orders.manage or payments.manage requires a private POS code. Read-only couriers and kitchen-only roles do not need one. Existing unused invitation links apply the same current rule. Changing a role label, courier address or route destination does not grant POS permissions. See [activation and recovery](/en/help/getting-started/first-login#activate).

<span id="google-account-guide" />

## Prepare a business-owned Google account

In FoodyAdmin’s Help and support panel, open **Google account and API keys guide** or choose “How do I prepare my Google account and API keys for Foody?”. The [step-by-step Google guide](/en/help/delivery/google-account-and-api-keys) covers accounts, projects, billing, APIs, restrictions and secure handoff to Foody. The same translations feed search and assistant sources. Never paste a key or secret file into the conversation. The assistant explains preparation; it does not collect credentials or activate individual onboarding.

<span id="undo-departure" />

## Undo an accidental departure

Dispatch opens on the restaurant’s current day. Choose Next planned day or a calendar date to see another route. In the courier’s card choose Undo departure. For a mistakenly reported issue, review each order number and reason before selecting Correct reports and undo departure. Order-management permission is required. The route returns to draft with the same date, courier and stop order. Out for delivery orders return to In preparation. Navigation closes and the original report, correction and sent messages remain in history. A recorded arrival or delivery blocks the reset.

<span id="superadmin-courier-mode" />

## View another courier’s workspace

With compatible API and Admin versions, the restaurant owner can also use this selector, like a superadmin. Open Deliveries → Courier mode and select a restaurant courier under View as courier. The map and routes belong to that courier; actions record real changes and the signed-in account’s GPS is not shared for them. See [the courier-mode procedure](/en/help/delivery/courier-workspace#superadmin-courier-selection), including switching couriers and recovering from errors. Planning changes are reserved for authorized dispatchers in Deliveries; they are unavailable to courier accounts and in an owner’s or superadmin’s courier workspace. For optional same-day orders, inspect the frozen arrival estimate in Order details, check the courier’s availability and confirm a valid delivery range. The estimate does not reserve courier capacity. Route optimization, courier assignments and day-before WhatsApp reminders keep their existing workflows; enabling same-day rules does not create a new reminder trigger.

<span id="courier-position" />

## Read the last received position

For an En route run, check the location notice under the courier: no position received, position received at the displayed time, or old position after more than one minute. The Courier position marker moves with new received positions; the green triangle is the planned departure. An active route does not guarantee that the phone is sending GPS. Check that Foody is visible, location access is allowed and the courier’s phone is connected. Opening Courier mode on the manager’s phone does not share the courier’s GPS. See [courier tracking and recovery](../delivery/courier-workspace#live-location).

The courier is represented by a round avatar above delivery pins, with an always-visible label. An old position retains its avatar and the Old courier position label. Initial framing includes the last received position; in Courier mode, Fit the whole route brings it back into view with the stops. GPS updates move the avatar without automatically recentering the map.

<span id="independent-lifecycle-confirmations" />

## Review independent confirmations

A partial handoff preserves remaining units. Completing one required KDS station does not complete all preparation. Courier handoff and customer receipt are separate confirmations; a route departure does not prove a customer receipt time. See [preparation and handoff](/en/help/orders/payment-preparation-and-handoff).

<span id="ordering-simulation-clock" />

## Test when an order is placed

In Orders & availability → Simulate an order, select Order date and time in the displayed timezone, then the receiving date and basket. Now reloads the server time. Preparation and same-day rules are evaluated at this reference using current saved settings and ready stock. A past date does not reconstruct history; this test neither reserves a courier nor creates an order. Customer orders retain the real server clock. See [the simulation procedure](/en/help/orders/preorders-and-batches#simulate-order).

<span id="courier-results-and-reminders" />

## Follow up on departures and stop results

In Deliveries → Dispatch, select the route date. Departure pending identifies a route that has yet to start. Result pending identifies a stop where the courier opened navigation and still needs to save a result. Choose Remind courier on an open route to send a notification to that courier’s registered devices. Acceptance confirms submission, and the courier must have enabled notifications. Open a reported problem’s order to review it. Verify the result and use the correction or handover actions available for the order. In Correct status, enter an explanatory note.

<span id="retired-sales-links" />

## What happens to an old sales tour link?

Dedicated sales tour links are retired. To order, open the restaurant’s regular website and choose from its published menu. The update preserves ordinary carts. Courier routes are available in Deliveries.
