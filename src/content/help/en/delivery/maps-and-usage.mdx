Deliveries and the delivery checkout in the website editor.

<span id="storefront" />

## Set checkout suggestions

In the website editor, open the delivery checkout. Turn address autocomplete on or off, then publish the website. When enabled, guests can select a Google suggestion. They can also confirm their building and city manually. The restaurant’s delivery zones, prices and schedules apply. Choosing a numbered address is enough to check delivery. If Google returns only the street, the selection keeps the house number.

<span id="address" />

## Correct a stop

In Deliveries, open the stop and edit its address. Choose the Google suggestion for the intended city. If Google returns only the street, review the house number before saving. An address can be saved while its precise building location is still pending. An unlocated stop keeps its assignment and is identified in the route.

<span id="route" />

## Locate stops on the map

Open the route map in Deliveries to see its stops and road itinerary. If a street is recognized but the building is missing, check the number and city. A route calculation can resume once the building or a confirmed entrance is located.
