Admin → Settings → Delivery → Maps

<span id="provider" />

## Review the configured map provider

Read the active provider and available usage information. Keep provider credentials in the designated secure configuration; never paste them into support chat.

<span id="limits" />

## Respect usage limits

Check map, address-search and route/optimization limits before large planning sessions. A commercial offer does not automatically activate an extra quota or bypass a provider cap.

<span id="own-google-project" />

## Prepare your own Google project

To prepare your account, billing and address, map and route keys, follow the [step-by-step Google guide](/en/help/delivery/google-account-and-api-keys). Individual onboarding requires confirmation from Foody: this screen has no form for business-owned keys. First contact Foody without secrets to agree the scope and a secure handoff channel.
