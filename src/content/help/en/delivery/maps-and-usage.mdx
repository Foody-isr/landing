Deliveries and the delivery checkout in the website editor.

<span id="storefront" />

## Set checkout suggestions

In the website editor, open the delivery order form, turn Google address autocomplete on or off, then publish. Guests can type a street, house number and city and tap the matching address. If suggestions are unavailable, they can use **Retry** in the same field. A numbered address can also be chosen with **Use the address you entered**. Coverage, fees and delivery times are checked before ordering.

<span id="address" />

## Correct a stop

In Deliveries, open the stop and edit its address. Choose the Google suggestion for the intended city. If Google returns only the street, review the house number before saving. An address can be saved while its precise building location is still pending. An unlocated stop keeps its assignment and is identified in the route.

<span id="route" />

## Locate stops on the map

Open the route map in Deliveries to see its stops and road itinerary. If a street is recognized but the building is missing, check the number and city. A route calculation can resume once the building or a confirmed entrance is located. “Estimated building location” means Google estimated the house number along the street. The driver sees this indication on the stop.
