Items & menus → Item library → saved item → Stock & availability.

<span id="section-1" />

## Open Stock & availability

Open a saved item from Items & menus → Item library and scroll to Stock & availability. The table shows the item, quantity and status. Editing requires menu editing permission. This section is part of the continuous editor, not a panel under a Recipe tab.

<span id="section-2" />

## Choose the stock basis

Click the quantity or use Actions → Stock tracking. Track one shared item quantity or quantities by size, depending on its variants. Compatible items offer portions, g or kg. Without predefined item stock, calculation can use recipe ingredients and preparations. No recipe does not guarantee unlimited availability: also check predefined stock, variants, rules and manual status.

<span id="section-3" />

## Choose and manage a rule

In stock tracking settings, choose an availability rule or the default rule. Actions → Manage rules opens Settings → Stock → Availability. Rules define tracking, low-stock threshold, out-of-stock behavior and remaining-count display. Check assignments before editing a rule used by multiple items.

<span id="section-4" />

## Override status and save

Click status and choose Follow a rule, Always available or Force sold out. Click Apply, then Save in the item header to persist it. Apply alone stages the change in the editor. The override wins over calculation; return to Follow a rule and save to resume automatic tracking.

<span id="section-5" />

## Check calculation and size quantities

Actions → View availability calculation opens capacity details: possible sales, calculation basis and limiting ingredient when present. When tracking sizes separately, check each quantity. Item stock differs from recipe consumption quantities and the Active/Inactive filter in the Items library. An unavailable calculation is an error to investigate, not zero stock.

<span id="section-6" />

## Verify after saving

After Save, reopen the item and check quantity, rule and status, then the relevant web or POS channel. Visibility also depends on groups, channels and hours. If rules fail to load, retry before saving; do not substitute an assumed configuration.
