Compatible activated iPad → More → Kitchen Companion

## Availability and access

Native iPad/tablet layout and kitchen permissions are required; availability depends on activation.

<span id="production" />

## Prepare production objectives

Review the selected service and proposed production quantities. Adjust allowed objectives and record actual preparation quantities using the corresponding controls.

<span id="service" />

## Review service priorities

Open the service workspace to review availability and priorities. Treat simulation/projection results as planning aids and verify actual stock before making a production decision.

<span id="closing" />

## Review sales and close the day

Review sales links, counts, variance and preparation results. Save the review and use closing only after checking the figures; do not repeat a confirmed stock-changing closure.

<span id="siri" />

## Use compatible Siri access

On a supported activated iPad with the required system version and permissions, use the configured Siri/App Intents. If unavailable, use the app workspace; this is not Android voice support.
