## Overview

The **Kitchen → Stock** section in FoodyAdmin lets you track your raw ingredient inventory. It is separate from your menu items — it tracks what's in your storage, not what's on the menu.

---

## Adding an Ingredient

1. In FoodyAdmin, go to **Kitchen → Stock**.
2. Click **Add Ingredient**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** — e.g., "Olive Oil", "Chicken Breast"
   - **Unit** — kg, g, l, ml, unit, pack, box, bag, dose, or other
   - **Current quantity**
   - **Reorder threshold** — the quantity at which a low-stock alert is triggered
4. Click **Save**.

---

## Low-Stock Alerts

When an ingredient's quantity falls below its reorder threshold, a badge appears on the Kitchen menu in the sidebar. Check **Kitchen → Stock** regularly to see which items need restocking.

---

## Updating Quantities

When you receive a delivery or use stock:
1. Open the ingredient.
2. Update the **Current quantity** field.
3. Save.

> **Tip:** Connect ingredients to your prep recipes (in **Kitchen → Prep**) so quantities are adjusted automatically when production is recorded.

---

## Supplier Integration

You can link ingredients to supplier products and create **Purchase Orders** in **Kitchen → Suppliers**. When a purchase order is marked as received, the quantities are updated automatically.
