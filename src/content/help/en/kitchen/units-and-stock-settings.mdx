Open Settings → Stock, Units and Availability. Keep measurement units consistent with recipes and purchasing.

<span id="units" />

## Manage measurement units

Review unit names, dimensions and conversions before adding or editing a unit. Use compatible mass, volume or count units; a supplier pack size is not automatically a recipe quantity. Verify existing ingredient and recipe references before deletion.

<span id="policy" />

## Set stock and availability policy

Review when stock is reserved/consumed and how shortages affect public availability. Check the configured policy with a small development scenario before changing service behavior. Manual item visibility, timed availability and ingredient stock are separate restrictions and can combine.
