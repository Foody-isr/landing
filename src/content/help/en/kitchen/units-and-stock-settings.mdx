Open Settings → Stock, Units and Availability. Keep measurement units consistent with recipes and purchasing.

<span id="units" />

## Manage measurement units

Review unit names, dimensions and conversions before adding or editing a unit. Use compatible mass, volume or count units; a supplier pack size is not automatically a recipe quantity. Verify existing ingredient and recipe references before deletion.

<span id="policy" />

## Set stock and availability policy

Review when stock is reserved/consumed and how shortages affect public availability. Check the configured policy with a small development scenario before changing service behavior. Manual item visibility, timed availability and ingredient stock are separate restrictions and can combine. For an item tracked by a shared counter whose rule shows the remaining count, the cart accounts for available quantity even above the low-stock threshold. That threshold controls the warning, not the cart cap. See [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) to check the rule and manual overrides.

<span id="carte-series-stock" />

## Read quantities in the correct stock pool

Predefined item stocks now have a journal under Items & menus → Stock history and on each product. A rotating carte can prepare initial allowances per series and publish them without restoring already committed units. Previous remaining counts are not carried forward. Order changes and pre-production releases use the original frozen pool; a produced item remains consumed. Ingredient/preparation quantities and recipe costs remain separate. See [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) for initial imports, corrections and history filters.
