Admin → Kitchen → Purchases

<span id="supplier" />

## Maintain supplier products

Create or edit supplier contact details, then review linked stock products and purchasing units/prices. Save and check the product mapping before ordering.

<span id="needs" />

## Turn needs into a purchase order

Review the Needs view, choose the supplier and adjust proposed quantities. Create the purchase order and check its lines and total before sending.

<span id="send" />

## Review and send the order

Open the send action, review recipient/channel and the proposed message, then confirm the intended send. Check its result separately from saving the purchase order. For email, fill Delivery date and start before sending; End of delivery window is optional and must be later. Generating or saving a draft does not send it. Verify the recipient and explicitly confirm Send; if the outcome is uncertain, check status before repeating.

<span id="receive" />

## Receive or cancel deliberately

Use Receive for actual delivered quantities and reconcile discrepancies. Cancellation/deletion have different effects; read the confirmation and avoid crediting stock twice through another import.
