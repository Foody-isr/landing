Open the intended bill and verify its current amount before Pay. Available tenders depend on the restaurant and device configuration. The full-screen progress and automatic post-payment return described below require the matching POS version; an API update alone does not change the installed payment UI. To name a check from Continue/Send before collecting payment, follow [custom check creation](/en/help/orders/order-types#pos-tableless-dine-in).

<span id="verifone-admin-money-operations" />

## Refund or void a Verifone online payment

For eligible Verifone online payments in Admin, follow the complete [refund and VOID procedure](/en/help/payments/how-payments-work#verifone-admin-refund-void). Verified provider state determines the available action. An uncertain result must be checked without repeating it. This does not add a terminal refund action or cancel preparation.

After an approved payment, a closing error does not authorize another collection. Follow [the table-service closing check](/en/help/tables-floors/staff-and-assistance#paid-table-close-recovery).

<span id="verifone-readable-hosted-theme" />

## Keep the hosted payment form readable

When Foody enables a restaurant-branded Verifone hosted checkout, the payment page uses light surfaces and dark text, even if the restaurant website is dark. The published logo is kept and the brand/button accent is adapted when needed for readable contrast. This concerns online hosted payments, not POS terminals or native Apple Pay/Google Pay sheets. If an older page is unreadable, contact the Foody team to resynchronize its payment theme. The update applies to newly issued Checkout links, not the page already open. Check the current payment status before requesting another link; never repeat a debit because of a display problem.

<span id="weight-hold-margin" />

## Review the by-weight authorization ceiling

For online by-weight orders, open Settings → Payments & VAT with settings.edit. Set Weight hold buffer % from 0 to 100 and save; the default for new restaurants is 20. A failed save keeps the draft. Zero still authorizes the estimate. Only the weight-priced subtotal gets a margin: 100 weighted + 50 fixed at 20% authorizes 170; delivery has no extra margin. Checkout shows the estimate, temporary ceiling and Confirm and authorize, in the restaurant theme. A changed ceiling requires customer review and another confirmation before payment; unknown policy blocks authorization. Existing reviewed checkouts and holds keep their ceiling. Staff confirm measured weights before capture and progression; excess beyond the hold is flagged for a separate payment, never automatically charged. Cash/pay-later makes no hold promise. Requires a configured authorize/capture provider, with partial capture, release and expiry validated before production. This neither changes the catering deposit/balance policy nor enables new-card saving with a CST weighted hold.

<span id="cash" />

## Record cash

On an enrolled POS with an active PIN shift, first open More → Reports → Cash drawer balance and enter the physical opening float. Then choose cash on the intended order or financial bill, enter the amount received and check the change before confirming. Only record money actually received. A pending cash collection must be retried with its existing identity, not recreated as another sale. Split bills keep their own receipt and paid status. A Pending payment state can simply mean payment is expected before any attempt; it does not prove an operation is in progress. Existing payment actions retain their conditions and permissions. A card authorization or an actual operation with an uncertain result must instead be resolved through its existing flow, without creating a replacement collection. Unknown kitchen progress does not authorize payment.

<span id="cash-report-unconfirmed" />

## Retry an unconfirmed cash-drawer read

If Cash drawer balance or Cash movements cannot be read, do not infer that no drawer is open. Retry reads the state on the same device without a cash movement or Verifone deposit. New operations stay unavailable until the state is confirmed. Preserve pending operations with their identifiers and do not erase local data. Specific messages need compatible POS and API versions; this guide does not confirm installation. See [cash-access diagnostics](/en/help/payments/cash-access#cash-access-refusal) for refusals and safe recovery.

<span id="cash-drawer-report" />

## Count and close the cash drawer

On the same enrolled POS with an active PIN shift, open More → Reports → Cash drawer balance. Review the opening float, cash sales and refunds, paid in/out and expected cash. The optional description is copied to the closing note, without automatic saving. With payments.manage, open Paid in/out, enter a positive amount and mandatory reason, then choose Pay in cash or Pay out cash. A payout cannot exceed the expected balance. With orders.manage, choose Close cash session, count physical cash, enter that amount and confirm; any discrepancy is preserved. Dismissing the modal without confirming records nothing. Cash movements lists only the session loaded on this device, not past sessions. Resume any uncertain operation using its existing identity before another action. Cards are excluded: closing cash does not start a Verifone deposit, and a Verifone deposit does not close cash. Never use a cash adjustment to disguise a card refund.

To bring together the end-of-day steps, use [the combined closing flow](/en/help/payments/end-of-day).

<span id="card" />

## Collect a connected payment

On an ordinary counter or table check on the updated handheld terminal, Pay opens a black customer review with the selected check’s lines, modifiers and confirmed amount. Review it before Confirm and pay. The ⋯ menu offers cash; the language selector changes only this review, without changing the device language. Dismissing the review collects nothing. Tablets retain the full-screen Card/Cash tender choice. Choose Pay by card. Foody keeps a full-screen progress view during preparation, terminal connection, reported login/session/parameter stages, the terminal instructions, payment recording and the counter order's kitchen send. Only stages actually reported are shown; not every payment needs every stage. Card reading and PIN entry remain on the secure Verifone terminal. The displayed kitchen send confirms order submission, not physical paper output: only categories selected in the assigned kitchen profile print. The screen closes on refusal or cancellation without marking the bill paid; review the displayed error before retrying. Wait for Foody’s verified final status; browser return alone is insufficient. If the result is pending/uncertain, refresh and review the provider transaction with an authorized operator before any retry. Never ask the help assistant to inspect card details. Before collection, Foody checks the bill data again. A change to lines, amount, paid state or restaurant/account context stops the flow: reopen Pay and confirm the updated review. The same check protects cash confirmation. An interrupted review does not establish a charge; if the provider has already returned an uncertain result, use the reconciliation procedure below. A new item or kitchen batch added to the check during review or cash entry also requires fresh confirmation; it must not be ignored when collecting. This Victa checkout uses ILS; changing language does not convert currency.

If checkout directs you to a pending close, follow [recovery in Current drawer](/en/help/payments/end-of-day#end-of-day-recovery). For an interrupted card attempt, follow [the original payment verification steps](/en/help/payments/ashrait-terminal-inspection#ashrait-last-transaction-recovery).

<span id="terminal-deposit" />

## Close and deposit a Verifone terminal batch

At end of day, open [Current drawer and closing](/en/help/payments/end-of-day) on the main iPad. After confirming the count, use Transmit end of day for the Mini. Portables transmit in turn with FoodyPOS open. If a result needs review, verify the original transmission before sending again. After finalizing, print the available SHVA copy from Drawer history. On older software, follow the device’s deposit flow.

<span id="saved-qr-payment-scope" />

## Verify payment for one QR order

From Orders → Menu → Actions → Manage this order, offered actions affect the selected number only, never the combined value of the table’s QR orders. Collection is offered only for an eligible unpaid order in its workflow and with payments.manage; pending, partial or already-paid states do not permit charging the full total again. Verify number, state and amount before confirming. Changing restaurant or operator, or losing permission, invalidates the open action. A terminal approval arriving after that change remains to be reconciled: do not repeat the charge. View table is separate navigation that neither pays nor closes anything. Existing Verifone rules, journals and final terminal confirmation remain in place.

<span id="receipt-tip" />

## Review tips and receipt

Review any configured tipping steps before collection and confirm the total. On compatible versions, once a cash or card sale is recorded, Foody sends the receipt to this POS’s default printers. Payment confirmation closes automatically. [Configure receipt printers](/en/help/printers/profiles-and-routing#device-receipt-destinations). For another destination or sending, open [Transactions → New receipt](/en/help/payments/transactions#new-pos-receipt).

To print an open check, use Print in Check. On a compact terminal, open the ⋯ menu at the bottom of the check first.

<span id="invoice4u-order-lines" />

## Check an itemized Invoice4U invoice

This concerns online orders, not a new POS terminal integration. When Invoice4U issuance and an invoice document type are enabled by the Foody team for the merchant, ordinary immediate Verifone order payments send the saved order lines: items and quantities, selected variants/options, combo base and included components, order discount and delivery fee. Frozen prices already include VAT; it is not added again. The line total must equal the payment amount or payment initiation is blocked. The same detail is sent for a later saved-card sale. Legacy orders without catalogue lines and catering/table/capture operations keep their existing document behavior. An approved payment is not proof that Invoice4U issued a fiscal document: check the document in Verifone Central/Invoice4U and customer delivery. Do not create another payment to obtain a missing invoice.

<span id="counter-terminal-payment-before-kitchen" />

## Wait for counter card payment before kitchen preparation

For a new counter pickup order paid by card, choose Pay, then Pay by card. Wait for terminal approval and for Foody to record the payment before preparation and the kitchen ticket start. During collection the payment remains pending and is excluded from the live order board. After a refusal or cancellation, keep the same checkout and retry there; do not send it to the kitchen to bypass payment. If approval succeeded but synchronization failed, reconcile the existing payment before starting another charge. Orders deliberately sent for table service before collection keep their existing send-then-pay flow.

<span id="counter-completion" />

## Confirm a paid counter takeaway sale

After counter collection and confirmed kitchen submission, the POS briefly shows Payment recorded and returns automatically. The confirmation includes the amount and any known cash change. Open [Transactions → New receipt](/en/help/payments/transactions#new-pos-receipt) for a copy. The paid order remains active while it is being prepared: payment does not mark it ready or handed over. Orders and Transactions refresh when reopened. If payment is recorded but the kitchen send is unconfirmed, keep the existing bill and retry only its send; never charge again. Kitchen tickets and customer receipts are separate. Automatic kitchen output requires an enabled printer profile with In-person order tickets, the item's categories selected and the printer associated with the current POS installation. This includes manually entered takeaway sales, not just table service. Configure profiles in FoodyAdmin or More → Settings → Hardware → Printers → Profiles on the POS. Reinstalling or using another POS installation can require discovering the printer and assigning the existing profile again. A successful payment or a queued print job does not prove paper was printed. Check the printer's history and queued/uncertain jobs before reconnecting or reprinting; pending documents can print after reconnection. If a confirmed paid takeaway bill remains before kitchen dispatch, Send to kitchen resumes only that send, even with a customized preparation workflow. It never requests a tender or another charge. If the original tender is absent, confirmation uses a neutral Payment heading, not an invented card payment. Before reassigning a printer, review its pending-document warning and [routing](/en/help/printers/profiles-and-routing#assign).

<span id="mobile-terminal" />

## Find Pay on mobile

On an ordinary check on the updated terminal, open Check then Pay at the bottom. It stays unavailable until the workflow permits collection. Review the customer summary, lines and amount before Confirm and pay; use ⋯ for cash. Card and PIN entry stay on Verifone’s secure screen. The recorded payment confirmation closes automatically. Neither a tab change nor an animation confirms payment. Never repeat collection with an unknown result. Other check commands, including permitted prepayment printing, remain in the check’s ⋯ menu.

<span id="mobile-terminal-pages" />

## Read transactions and reports on mobile

Transactions uses the full width for its list; tap a row to open details and use Back to return to the list. Filters, pagination and access permissions remain those of the journal. On compact screens, details and the cash-drawer report use terminal-sized insets. The visual change starts no collection, refund or deposit.

## Connect and restore the Victa Mini on iPad

On iPad, connect the terminal once under More → Settings → Hardware → Payment terminals. The compatible POS then attempts to reconnect on opening and returning to the foreground, after restaurant access and permissions are restored. See [Victa Mini connection](/en/help/payments/victa-mini#connection) for secure setup, states and recovery locks. This flow starts no charge or deposit.

<span id="physical-card-destination" />

## Default card terminal

On iPad, Card uses the [default terminal set in Hardware](/en/help/payments/victa-mini#explicit-terminal-selection). For a pending attempt, resume the original payment.

<span id="unified-cash-workspace" />

## Use the unified cash workspace

With payment permissions and compatible versions, open More → Reports → Current drawer. The same workspace shows the cash drawer, recorded card collections by terminal and guided closing. Drawer history combines closed cash sessions and finalized Z reports. Older closing and deposit addresses open this workspace; older APIs retain their previous workflow. See [the unified cash workflow](/en/help/payments/end-of-day#unified-cash-workspace).

<span id="transaction-history" />

## Review completed payments

Open Transactions to review recorded cash, POS card and online payments. Follow [transaction history](/en/help/payments/transactions) for filters, payment origins, returns and unavailable original orders. This shared history does not retrieve all payments from Ashrait.

<span id="ashrait-last-transaction-inspection" />

## Inspect the last terminal transaction

In Hardware → Payment terminals, a manager can inspect the last transaction on the connected terminal. This read changes no payment. See [the procedure and its limits](/en/help/payments/ashrait-terminal-inspection). On a compatible Portable, the guide also explains how to print the customer copy when Ashrait returns it.

<span id="current-register-access" />

## Verify the register in use

On compatible versions, paying from the Portable uses its own enrollment even when attendance began on an iPad. Permissions and the closing check remain mandatory. If verification fails before the card prompt, check Drawer balance and this device’s authorization; do not end attendance to bypass the block.

<span id="ashrait-checkout-card-copy" />

## Request a card receipt copy

To request an Ashrait card copy, open More → Settings → Hardware → Payment terminals → Last terminal transaction. Compare its reference with the sale. When the terminal returned the document, print the customer or merchant copy on the Portable’s integrated printer or the Epson receipt printer selected on iPad. For the Foody sales receipt, open [Transactions → New receipt](/en/help/payments/transactions#new-pos-receipt).

<span id="ashrait-terminal-installment-selection" />

## Offer installment payments

With permission to manage payments, open More → Settings → Hardware → Payment terminals → Installment payments. Verify that the right terminal is active and enter the shekel range configured in VHQ. If the acquirer allows entering plans in Foody, enter its permitted maximum count as well. For a card payment within the range, you can ask the customer to choose installments on the terminal. When a maximum count is configured, Set installments in Foody lets you enter a permitted count and optionally a first payment amount. Confirm the plan before presenting the card. If the first payment field is blank, the terminal calculates the amounts. Foody records the full total after confirmation. Select Disable to remove the choice. The setting is local to this POS and terminal.
