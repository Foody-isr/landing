Open the intended bill and verify its current amount before Pay. Available tenders depend on the restaurant and device configuration.

<span id="weight-hold-margin" />

## Review the by-weight authorization ceiling

For online by-weight orders, open Settings → Payments & VAT with settings.edit. Set Weight hold buffer % from 0 to 100 and save; the default for new restaurants is 20. A failed save keeps the draft. Zero still authorizes the estimate. Only the weight-priced subtotal gets a margin: 100 weighted + 50 fixed at 20% authorizes 170; delivery has no extra margin. Checkout shows the estimate, temporary ceiling and Confirm and authorize, in the restaurant theme. A changed ceiling requires customer review and another confirmation before payment; unknown policy blocks authorization. Existing reviewed checkouts and holds keep their ceiling. Staff confirm measured weights before capture and progression; excess beyond the hold is flagged for a separate payment, never automatically charged. Cash/pay-later makes no hold promise. Requires a configured authorize/capture provider, with partial capture, release and expiry validated before production. This neither changes the catering deposit/balance policy nor enables new-card saving with a CST weighted hold.

<span id="cash" />

## Record cash

Choose cash, enter the received amount when requested and review change before confirming. Record only money actually received. On split bills select the correct financial bill so its receipt and paid status remain independent.

<span id="card" />

## Collect a connected payment

Choose an enabled connected tender and follow the terminal or hosted page. Wait for Foody’s verified final status; browser return alone is insufficient. If the result is pending/uncertain, refresh and review the provider transaction with an authorized operator before any retry. Never ask the help assistant to inspect card details.

<span id="receipt-tip" />

## Review tips and receipt

Review any supported tip/checkout prompt before collection and confirm the total. After success open the digital or configured printer receipt. A reprint does not collect another payment. Per-bill refund controls are not established for every advanced split flow; contact an authorized operator rather than assuming a refund button exists.

<span id="invoice4u-order-lines" />

## Check an itemized Invoice4U invoice

This concerns online orders, not a new POS terminal integration. When Invoice4U issuance and an invoice document type are enabled by the Foody team for the merchant, ordinary immediate Verifone order payments send the saved order lines: items and quantities, selected variants/options, combo base and included components, order discount and delivery fee. Frozen prices already include VAT; it is not added again. The line total must equal the payment amount or payment initiation is blocked. The same detail is sent for a later saved-card sale. Legacy orders without catalogue lines and catering/table/capture operations keep their existing document behavior. An approved payment is not proof that Invoice4U issued a fiscal document: check the document in Verifone Central/Invoice4U and customer delivery. Do not create another payment to obtain a missing invoice.

<span id="counter-terminal-payment-before-kitchen" />

## Wait for counter card payment before kitchen preparation

For a new counter pickup order paid by card, choose Pay, then Pay by card. Wait for terminal approval and for Foody to record the payment before preparation and the kitchen ticket start. During collection the payment remains pending and is excluded from the live order board. After a refusal or cancellation, keep the same checkout and retry there; do not send it to the kitchen to bypass payment. If approval succeeded but synchronization failed, reconcile the existing payment before starting another charge. Orders deliberately sent for table service before collection keep their existing send-then-pay flow.
