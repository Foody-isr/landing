On iPad, open More → Reports → Current drawer. Compatible POS and API versions show the restaurant’s card payments and support closing from the main register.

> **Release availability must be confirmed**
> This application exists in the source workspace. This guide does not confirm public distribution or availability in your installed release.

<span id="end-of-day-scope" />

## Check the closing scope

At the main register, the Z includes the counter drawer and the terminals included in the restaurant close. It records the cash count, Foody-recorded payments and SHVA confirmations separately. Depending on the equipment in use, the close can cover cash, cards or both. An open iPad drawer must be included. Web payments, GBX, attendance and table release follow their usual workflows. With older software, or before a main register is set, closing covers the drawer and terminals of the device in use.

<span id="end-of-day-run" />

## Count, transmit and archive

With payments.manage, choose Close drawer session on the main iPad. An open cash drawer also requires orders.manage. Review the terminals, count any open drawer, enter the actual amount, then choose Start closing. This freezes the count and pauses new card payments during closing. Use Transmit end of day on the iPad for each included Mini in the displayed order. Portables transmit in turn when FoodyPOS is open and connected. Wait for each terminal’s confirmation, then choose Finalize and create Z report. On a setup without a main register, confirm the current device’s transmissions in its own closing flow.

## Recover without transmitting twice

On the main iPad, open More → Reports → Current drawer. After a connection loss, choose **Resume recording**. Foody looks for the original confirmation. On a compatible Mini it also compares the SHVA reports retained by the terminal. For a Portable, open FoodyPOS on that device with an authorized personal session. Keep the original request while its result is uncertain.

With the compatible release and permission to manage payments and edit settings, a manager can choose **Cancel unsent closing** while all transmissions are pending. This releases the reserved drawer. After a transmission has started, choose **Resume on available terminals** and check the terminal connection. Foody retains the unresolved deposit and permits new sales on eligible terminals when the gateway reports readiness. Later sales belong to the next period. Finalize the Z after the confirmations arrive.

If you have the original paper report, choose **Verify a SHVA report** on that terminal and enter the transmission reference (אסמכתת שידור). Foody searches the ten most recent terminal reports and checks the SHVA number, time and totals before attaching it. Keep FoodyPOS open on a Portable. A late bank deposit can include sales in the next Foody period. If the terminal returns an empty gateway status, check the Ashrait version with Verifone.

On a replacement iPad enrolled at the restaurant, sign in as a manager, open Cash balance and choose **Take over this closing**. Review the closing time and confirm. This iPad becomes the main register. The original cash drawer, amounts and transmission requests stay with the same closing. For an already dispatched deposit, open the archived report review and enter its printed reference. Available terminals can resume taking payments while the deposit is being checked.

When the closing already says that the original transmission is under review and an identified terminal can resume payments, the manager has already resumed service. After a Mini restart, use Check Ashrait gateway on its Hardware page. A ready gateway allows a new sale on an eligible terminal. Keep the original deposit open for reconciliation. Use Verify a SHVA report with the transmission reference from the bank report to reconcile that deposit.

On a compatible main iPad, a manager can open the Mini’s unconfirmed transmission and choose **Find the report on the terminal**. Foody reads the ten archive slots and matches the bank terminal number and the time of that dispatch. If one report matches, read it, tap **Use this transmission reference**, then confirm so Foody can validate and attach the report. An empty, failed or ambiguous search leaves the transmission open for review. If you have the original bank report, you can enter its reference in **Verify a SHVA report**.

On the iPad managing the closing, open **Reports → Cash balance** and find the Mini. A manager with payment and settings permissions can select **Close this terminal and resume payments** for a pending batch. After confirmation Foody reads the Ashrait status. If closing is required and the batch has never been sent, Foody transmits it once and checks the Mini again. Resolve any interrupted card sale first. A confirmed empty batch may have no SHVA printout.

For an earlier **transmission to verify**, choose **Check and resume payments**. Foody reads the Mini’s gateway status. A request for parameters or settlement requires a full End of Day. When **Close the Mini’s current batch** appears, a manager can review and confirm that transmission. Payments can resume when Ashrait returns **OK** and Foody admits the terminal. The earlier deposit stays under review.

On the main iPad, check any interrupted card payment first. If Ashrait still requires an end of day, a manager with settings and payment permissions can choose **Close the Mini’s current batch**. Review the confirmation. The Mini sends all sales still in its batch, including earlier sales if they remain. Confirm once. Foody records a separate transmission and keeps the earlier one under review. Resume payments when Ashrait returns OK. If the connection drops, use **Resume recording** to check the result. You can view and print the new SHVA report when the Mini supplies it.

If the Mini displays **End of Day**, leave it powered on and connected until its home screen returns. In **Cash balance**, choose **Find the current batch report** for the latest attempt or **Find the original transmission report** for the earlier one. Both searches read the Mini’s reports. If no report matches, that transmission stays open for review. **Check and resume payments** lets you resume when Ashrait returns OK and the register allows payments on this Mini.

For a CST test Mini, if Verifone asks you to restart after a closing with no receipt, wait for the home screen. On the main iPad select **Check and resume payments**. If Ashrait still requires closing and Foody has authorized that specific retry, a manager can select **Retry the CST Mini closing**. The bank may already have received the previous batch. Foody looks for its report before the one authorized send. Use **Find the CST retry report** to review this attempt. Card payments resume when Ashrait returns OK and Foody allows payments on the Mini.

If the last CST retry failed and a diagnostic send was authorized for that specific attempt, open **Reports → Cash balance → Victa Mini** on the main iPad. Select **Run End of Day again on the CST Mini** and confirm once. Foody first checks the gateway and looks for the last attempt’s report. If closing is still required and the report is missing, the Mini sends its current batch. SHVA may already have received a previous batch. If closing fails, select **View Verifone response** to select and copy the technical message when available and the code received by the iPad. Some messages appear only on the Mini. After a correction, approval for the most recent attempt makes this button available for one more send. Find its result under **Mini close after correction**.

## Resolve pending cash operations

Before starting, recover any pending cash operation: follow the link to Cash balance for a drawer operation, or to Orders to find the affected collection. Its identity is retained while the result is uncertain; do not create a second operation to replace it. Once the drawer is reserved for closing, its report remains readable, but cash collections, movements and separate closing actions are blocked. The drawer and personal context are checked again at confirmation, before any drawer opening or collection confirmation. A current drawer check also precedes the cash tender dialog; if that read fails, do not collect cash through this dialog. With the required permissions, the reservation notice links to End of day. Only an explicit server-confirmed rejection releases a rejected request; a network error does not. Resume the existing closing instead of bypassing its reservation.

## Review several physical terminals

On iPad, verify the Mini profiles in Hardware. Open FoodyPOS on the restaurant’s enrolled Portables with an authorized session to verify their identities. The closing review lists them with the iPad’s verified terminals. A Portable also saved in the iPad profiles receives a single request, executed on that Portable. On the compatible release, terminals in different identified SHVA batches can transmit independently. Foody coordinates operations sharing one batch.

Before a new transmission, FoodyPOS reads the SHVA number stored on that terminal’s inventory entry.

For a new operation on a Mini connected by IP, FoodyPOS checks the SHVA number assigned to its physical serial in Backoffice. If there is no active assignment or SHVA number, ask Foody to check the fleet record. Use the original terminal and register to review an operation already started.

After a SHVA number is changed in Backoffice, new closings use the saved number. Earlier transmissions stay with their original batch. You can still look up their reports in cash history.

## Understand the card block and report period

The period ends when closing starts. After the first consolidated Z, the next period starts at that cutoff. When moving to the main register, card payments already archived in local Z reports are excluded from the current statement. Finalization reopens card payments. A manager can also resume sales on available terminals while a batch is being checked. Awaiting transmission identifies work still to be sent. A transmission marked for review needs its outcome confirmed before any retry.

<span id="end-of-day-recorded-cards-and-history" />

## View recorded card collections and previous reports

In Current drawer on iPad, Card payments for the restaurant shows the period, count and recorded amounts overall and by terminal. Amounts are shown by original currency. These Foody card collections exclude refunds. SHVA totals and financial payments are shown separately because some entries cover the same payments. In Drawer history on the main iPad, open a finalized Z to see the count, confirmations and available SHVA copies. An older archive may contain only the transmission confirmation.

<span id="unified-cash-workspace" />

## Track and close from Current drawer

With payments.manage, use Current drawer to open a drawer with its starting cash, record paid in and paid out, and review collections. Opening a drawer is a cash operation. Close drawer session first opens the count and transmission review. You can go back before starting the close. Drawer history lists the latest twenty finalized entries, including Z reports and separately closed cash sessions.

<span id="current-register-scope" />

## Close the current register

Before a main register is set, a close belongs to the enrolled device that started it. Finish any earlier open closing before setting the counter iPad. After setup, run closing and access its consolidated Z on this iPad. Terminal evidence stays attached to the original operation.

<span id="designate-main-register" />

## Set the main register

On the enrolled counter iPad, open More → Reports → Current drawer with an active personal session and payments.manage and settings.edit. Choose Set this iPad as the main register and confirm. Update the iPad and Portables before the first shared close. Open FoodyPOS with payment access on each Portable to register its identity.

If the old main iPad entry was forgotten during a closing, an authorized manager can choose **Take over this closing** on the restaurant’s enrolled iPad. Foody keeps the drawer and the transmission records for the closing in progress.

<span id="reprint-consolidated-shva" />

## Reprint a SHVA transmission report

On the main iPad, open Drawer history and the day’s Z. The Z consolidates debits, credits, operation counts and net amounts where the SHVA responses provide them. Under the terminal, choose Reprint SHVA report to use the iPad’s configured Epson receipt printer. An archived copy can be printed with the terminal switched off. This action prints the existing report. If the copy or totals are unavailable, review the confirmations and reports available for each terminal.

Under the selected terminal, choose **View SHVA report** to read the bank’s copy in Hebrew. Printing uses the same archived report. You can compare the transmission reference on screen and on paper.
