Admin → Orders → open the order → Actions → View invoice. This action appears when the order has a stored Sumit document reference. Some labels spell the provider Summit.

<span id="view-download" />

## Find the official document and its PDF

After confirmed Sumit payment, Foody stores the document number returned by Sumit. The Invoice panel retrieves the existing accounting document; View and Download do not issue another invoice or charge again. The exact document type depends on the Sumit configuration: inspect its heading, number and amount rather than treating every PDF as the same tax document.
1. Open the order → Actions → View invoice.
2. Check the document number. Choose View to open the PDF, or Download to save it.
3. Supplementary documents from confirmed balance payments are listed separately with their own amounts and View/Download actions. The original document remains separate. Manual partial-payment recording alone does not create a Sumit invoice; any receipt/reference for the earlier tender must be reconciled separately.

<span id="send-share" />

## Email or share the existing invoice

For the primary invoice, choose Send invoice. Check/edit the recipient email, then choose By email (via Summit) and wait for the sent confirmation. Alternatively use Share WhatsApp or Copy link. WhatsApp opens a prepared message in the app and still needs your send; it is separate from automatic order notifications. A copied invoice URL is a shareable document link: send it only to the intended recipient. Supplementary invoice rows currently offer View and Download; do not assume they have the primary invoice’s email action. Download the correct supplement to share it through an authorized channel.

<span id="missing-document" />

## If an invoice is missing or unavailable

First confirm that Sumit was the payment provider and payment really succeeded. A pending link, manual cash/card entry, or another provider does not guarantee a stored Sumit document. The invoice action is absent when there is no document reference. If the reference exists but loading/PDF fails, refresh and ask an authorized Foody operator to check the Sumit document; do not charge again or create a duplicate document to repair a display error. Check the original and supplementary document amounts separately. Invoice cancellation, credit notes and accounting corrections are not offered by this viewing panel; use the authorized provider/accounting procedure.
