## Review the by-weight authorization ceiling

For online by-weight orders, open Settings → Payments & VAT with settings.edit. Set Weight hold buffer % from 0 to 100 and save; the default for new restaurants is 20. A failed save keeps the draft. Zero still authorizes the estimate. Only the weight-priced subtotal gets a margin: 100 weighted + 50 fixed at 20% authorizes 170; delivery has no extra margin. Checkout shows the estimate, temporary ceiling and Confirm and authorize, in the restaurant theme. A changed ceiling requires customer review and another confirmation before payment; unknown policy blocks authorization. Existing reviewed checkouts and holds keep their ceiling. Staff confirm measured weights before capture and progression; excess beyond the hold is flagged for a separate payment, never automatically charged. Cash/pay-later makes no hold promise. Requires a configured authorize/capture provider, with partial capture, release and expiry validated before production. This neither changes the catering deposit/balance policy nor enables new-card saving with a CST weighted hold.

<span id="section-1" />

## Payment configuration

The payment provider is configured for each restaurant by the Foody team through the backoffice. Available payment methods depend on that configuration, the merchant contract and the order channel. Sumit and Verifone are integrated; availability must be confirmed for your installation. New PayPlus checkout is disabled.

<span id="section-2" />

## Check an online payment

1. Open the order in **Orders**.
2. Check its payment status and recorded method.
3. A return from a payment page is not by itself proof of payment. Foody verifies the payment with the provider before marking it paid.
4. For online orders requiring payment, confirmation makes the order available to the POS workflow.

<span id="section-3" />

## A failed or uncertain result

Do not assume that a timeout means no money was taken. Check the order and provider transaction before retrying a charge. Contact Foody support if they disagree or the outcome is unclear. Never include full card details, CVV or credentials in a support message.

Cash/manual recording, receipts, invoice issuance and refunds are separate operations. Their available controls depend on your setup and permissions. Do not record a card payment as successful solely because a customer shows a return page. Ask support for the procedure applicable to your provider before a refund or correction.

## Save a card during the first payment (CST)

On the CST test setup validated with Verifone, sign in or create your Foody customer account, explicitly select Save card, then confirm the order. The hosted Verifone page collects the card, CVV and Israeli ID and charges the full order amount while creating the reusable token. There is no separate 1 ILS registration payment and no automatic monthly charge. Foody opens confirmation only after verifying the transaction. On later orders, choose the saved card and explicitly confirm the new amount; the configured No-CVV contract requires neither CVV nor Israeli ID. A delayed result requires checking status, never repeating the debit. Previously issued registration links keep their old recovery flow. Weighted holds remain outside this new registration flow. Production availability requires separate merchant approval.

## Check an itemized Invoice4U invoice

When Invoice4U issuance and an invoice document type are enabled by the Foody team for the merchant, ordinary immediate Verifone order payments send the saved order lines: items and quantities, selected variants/options, combo base and included components, order discount and delivery fee. Frozen prices already include VAT; it is not added again. The line total must equal the payment amount or payment initiation is blocked. The same detail is sent for a later saved-card sale. Legacy orders without catalogue lines and catering/table/capture operations keep their existing document behavior. An approved payment is not proof that Invoice4U issued a fiscal document: check the document in Verifone Central/Invoice4U and customer delivery. Do not create another payment to obtain a missing invoice.
