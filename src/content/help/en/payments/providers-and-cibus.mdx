Open Settings → Payments or Cibus with the required permission. Provider availability is specific to the restaurant’s contract and deployed integration.

<span id="provider" />

## Arrange provider activation

Payment-provider credentials and activation are managed by the Foody team in the protected backoffice. Contact an authorized Foody operator to change them; the merchant Payments page does not connect a card processor. Use sandbox development to verify the supported transaction path. Never send credentials or card data to chat. SUMIT/Verifone availability depends on the contract; a provider name alone does not prove activation.

<span id="saved-methods" />

## Understand saved payment methods

For an eligible customer/provider, saving a method requires its explicit enrollment flow. Guest checkout remains possible without enrollment where offered. Removing Foody’s saved authorization does not remove unrelated provider subscriptions. A failed enrollment can be reported after a successful order payment without undoing that payment.

<span id="cibus" />

## Configure and use Cibus

In Cibus check whether the integration is enabled. With settings permission, replace the complete restaurant ID, POS ID and company code through the protected form; masked hints are not replacement credentials. Review the saved masked status. On POS select Cibus only when configured and inspect its result and remaining balance before another tender. A failed network response does not prove there was no charge.

<span id="policy-vat-tips" />

## Set payment policy, VAT and tips

In Settings → Payments review online-payment-only policy, default VAT and tips enablement. Only permitted, changed fields are saved. An unknown policy remains unknown until explicitly chosen; editing VAT must not silently choose it. Weight authorization buffer and fixed suggested-tip/rounding behavior are read-only or have no edit contract here. Verify saved values after success; retain edits on a failed save.

<span id="verifone-cst-card-saving" />

## Save a card during the first payment (CST)

On the CST test setup validated with Verifone, sign in or create your Foody customer account, explicitly select Save card, then confirm the order. The hosted Verifone page collects the card, CVV and Israeli ID and charges the full order amount while creating the reusable token. There is no separate 1 ILS registration payment and no automatic monthly charge. Foody opens confirmation only after verifying the transaction. On later orders, choose the saved card and explicitly confirm the new amount; the configured No-CVV contract requires neither CVV nor Israeli ID. A delayed result requires checking status, never repeating the debit. Previously issued registration links keep their old recovery flow. Weighted holds remain outside this new registration flow. Production availability requires separate merchant approval.

<span id="invoice4u-order-lines" />

## Check an itemized Invoice4U invoice

When Invoice4U issuance and an invoice document type are enabled by the Foody team for the merchant, ordinary immediate Verifone order payments send the saved order lines: items and quantities, selected variants/options, combo base and included components, order discount and delivery fee. Frozen prices already include VAT; it is not added again. The line total must equal the payment amount or payment initiation is blocked. The same detail is sent for a later saved-card sale. Legacy orders without catalogue lines and catering/table/capture operations keep their existing document behavior. An approved payment is not proof that Invoice4U issued a fiscal document: check the document in Verifone Central/Invoice4U and customer delivery. Do not create another payment to obtain a missing invoice.
