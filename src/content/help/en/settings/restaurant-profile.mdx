Open Settings for the selected restaurant. Confirm its name before saving changes.

<span id="weight-hold-margin" />

## Review the by-weight authorization ceiling

For online by-weight orders, open Settings → Payments & VAT with settings.edit. Set Weight hold buffer % from 0 to 100 and save; the default for new restaurants is 20. A failed save keeps the draft. Zero still authorizes the estimate. Only the weight-priced subtotal gets a margin: 100 weighted + 50 fixed at 20% authorizes 170; delivery has no extra margin. Checkout shows the estimate, temporary ceiling and Confirm and authorize, in the restaurant theme. A changed ceiling requires customer review and another confirmation before payment; unknown policy blocks authorization. Existing reviewed checkouts and holds keep their ceiling. Staff confirm measured weights before capture and progression; excess beyond the hold is flagged for a separate payment, never automatically charged. Cash/pay-later makes no hold promise. Requires a configured authorize/capture provider, with partial capture, release and expiry validated before production. This neither changes the catering deposit/balance policy nor enables new-card saving with a CST weighted hold.

<span id="identity" />

## Edit restaurant information

In general settings update the displayed name, description, contact information and address fields available to your role. In Branding update logo and brand assets. Save and verify the guest-facing result; Website may have its own published design overrides.

<span id="languages" />

## Set supported languages

In Languages choose the restaurant languages and default language offered by the form. Translate catalog names and public website content separately. Changing the interface language alone does not translate every item or customer message.

<span id="hours" />

## Maintain opening hours

In Opening hours edit each day and its time ranges, including closed days. Check the restaurant time zone, calendar exceptions and separate pickup/delivery schedules. Save, then verify the next offered fulfillment slot without placing a real order.

<span id="related-service-controls" />

## Access the new controls

The entry sign-in invitation is now a published website order-journey preference. Order without an account remains available; this setting does not replace mandatory phone verification or payment/delivery validation. Google/Geoapify consumption and limits are separately available in [Usage and quotas](/en/help/settings/api-usage-and-quotas).
