## Where to Find Workflow Settings

Workflow settings are available in two places that control the same configuration:
- **FoodyAdmin → Restaurant → Workflow Settings**
- **FoodyAdmin → Orders → Settings**

Changes apply immediately to FoodyPOS.

---

## Auto Send to Kitchen

**Default: off**

When **on**: orders are automatically sent to the kitchen queue the moment they are confirmed (no manual "Send to Kitchen" tap required).

When **off**: staff must tap **Send to Kitchen** in FoodyPOS to dispatch the order. This gives you a chance to review the order before it goes to the kitchen.

**Recommended for:** high-volume quick-service restaurants, or any workflow where manual dispatch adds unnecessary friction.

---

## Require Order Approval

**Default: off**

When **on**: each incoming order (especially from QR self-ordering) must be manually accepted by a staff member before it appears in the kitchen queue. Useful if you want to screen orders for accuracy.

When **off**: orders are auto-accepted and enter the workflow immediately.

---

## Tips

**Default: off**

When **on**: a tip prompt appears at checkout (both on FoodyPOS and on the QR payment screen). You can configure the suggested tip percentages.

---

## Rush Mode

**Default: off**

When **on**: estimated wait times are hidden from customers. Useful during peak service when wait times are unpredictable. Enable it manually when service gets busy, then disable when things calm down.

---

## Scheduled Orders

**Default: off**

When **on**: customers can place orders for a specific future date and time window (pickup or delivery). Scheduled orders appear in the **Scheduled Calendar** view on FoodyPOS.

To use this effectively:
1. Enable **Pickup** or **Delivery** order types as well.
2. Set the **Pickup Prep Time** so the system knows when to show the order to your kitchen.

---

## Pickup Prep Time

Defines how many minutes it takes to prepare a pickup or delivery order. This value is shown to the customer as part of their estimated ready time.

Example: if Pickup Prep Time is 20 minutes and a customer orders at 12:00, the app shows "Ready at 12:20".
