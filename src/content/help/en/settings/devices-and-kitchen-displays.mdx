Open Settings → Devices or Kitchen displays. Configure routing and permissions before using a device in service.

<span id="victa-identity-preservation" />

## Keep an enrolled Victa and its integrated printer linked

A Victa running Foody can report itself through the POS print agent as well as through its payment-terminal capability. With the corrected API, an older generic Android hardware report does not downgrade a terminal capability already registered for that active device. Its integrated printer keeps the same owned parent, identity, profiles and job history. This is not automatic discovery or payment certification, and it does not turn an ordinary tablet into a payment terminal. If the device or integrated printer becomes unavailable after an update, retain the error and have the device inventory and API version checked. Do not forget, recreate or move devices between restaurants as a workaround, and do not resend queued documents or payments merely to test connectivity. Forgotten devices remain forgotten; printer routing and uncertain-job recovery still require their own checks.

<span id="devices" />

## Configure devices

The Mini paired to this iPad appears in both **Restaurant devices** and **Payment terminals**. Both entries open the same device details and show the same name and connection status. **Connected** means FoodyPOS verified the terminal’s identity on that connection.

Find the correct device, review its identity, active state and selected POS profile. Do not reuse an activation code across restaurants. A staff role and a device profile have different responsibilities; check both if a screen or action is unavailable. After forgetting or replacing a device, check the current device's mode assignment explicitly. Cleanup of a forgotten assignment does not transfer the profile to a replacement; see [saving settings after a forgotten device](/en/help/settings/pos-behavior#forgotten-device-assignment) for compatible-version requirements and recovery.

<span id="stations" />

## Set kitchen stations

Create or edit the station and assign its allowed item categories and device access. Review priority and completion behavior. An item can route to its configured production station while keeping its customer-facing menu group unchanged.

<span id="service" />

## Work with kitchen tickets

Open the assigned KDS, inspect released tickets and advance or complete them using permitted controls. Held food is not a released ticket. Recall/priority actions must target the displayed ticket; do not re-send an order merely because its station is offline.

<span id="pair-android" />

## Pair an iPad or Android kitchen display

On the compatible pilot release, an owner or manager with permission to edit settings opens **Settings → Kitchen displays** in FoodyAdmin and enables dedicated kitchen tablets for the restaurant. Select the tablet in inventory, or register it with a recognisable name. Save a kitchen profile if the restaurant has none. Choose a default station and grant access to one, several or all stations in the restaurant, select whether the tablet can update tickets, then save its assignment. Generate an eight-character code. On the iPad or Android tablet, open **Dedicated kitchen display** on the FoodyPOS sign-in screen, enter the code within ten minutes and check the station name. A code activates one tablet. Issuing a replacement ends the previous identity.

<span id="station-selection" />

## Switch stations or set up a pass overview

A tablet opens one station at a time. An assigned list lets the cook switch among those stations from the station selector. All stations includes current and future profiles in this restaurant. A manager can change the assignment while the tablet stays paired. For a kitchen overview, create a pass profile with the required order sources, dining options and every category. An empty category or register selection includes future entries. Enable held courses to see upcoming items. Use view-only access on a display used for monitoring.

<span id="touch-and-recovery" />

## Complete by touch and recover after disconnection

Touch an item to mark that line, or the ticket header to complete the routed items at this station. Undo during the three-second delay to cancel the touch. The completed tab offers recall. At the pass, prep marks help coordinate the rest of the ticket. A held course or an advance preview of a scheduled pickup becomes actionable after kitchen release. Setting an alert time, priority or ticket completion affects kitchen work. Cash settlement and table occupancy use their respective FoodyPOS workflows. During a network outage the display shows the age of its last update and suspends actions. Reconnect, refresh and read the confirmed state before touching again. A normal app restart restores the association. For a lost or replaced device, check last contact in Admin, revoke its identity and pair the replacement.

<span id="forget-hardware" />

## Forget a device in FoodyPOS

On a compatible version of FoodyPOS, open **More → Settings → Hardware**. Select the device, choose **Forget device**, then confirm its name. For a standalone printer, open **Printers**, select it, and use the **…** menu. For a Verifone terminal, open **Payment terminals** and select the terminal. You need permission to manage all of the selected device’s functions.

The device’s Foody connections and assignments are removed. A POS that you remove must be authorized again to use its device access. Payments, closed cash reports and completed print jobs stay in history. Close any open cash drawer session before removing its POS. If you remove the main register, designate a replacement in Reports.

<span id="commercial-device-capacity" />

## Distinguish accounts and authorized devices

For versioned development offers, Staff → Devices shows physical POS capacity separately from employee accounts. Reauthorizing the same identified device does not consume another slot; a second device above the limit is rejected and the owner must add a licence in Billing. Revoked or expired credentials no longer count as active POS devices. Kitchen displays have their own contracted quantity; adding a new station is rejected at that limit. Historical contracts keep their rights.
