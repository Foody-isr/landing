Open Settings → Devices or Kitchen displays. Configure routing and permissions before using a device in service.

<span id="devices" />

## Configure devices

Find the correct device, review its identity, active state and selected POS profile. Do not reuse an activation code across restaurants. A staff role and a device profile have different responsibilities; check both if a screen or action is unavailable.

<span id="stations" />

## Set kitchen stations

Create or edit the station and assign its allowed item categories and device access. Review priority and completion behavior. An item can route to its configured production station while keeping its customer-facing menu group unchanged.

<span id="service" />

## Work with kitchen tickets

Open the assigned KDS, inspect released tickets and advance or complete them using permitted controls. Held food is not a released ticket. Recall/priority actions must target the displayed ticket; do not re-send an order merely because its station is offline.
