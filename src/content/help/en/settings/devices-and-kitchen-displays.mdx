Open Settings → Devices or Kitchen displays. Configure routing and permissions before using a device in service.

<span id="victa-identity-preservation" />

## Keep an enrolled Victa and its integrated printer linked

A Victa running Foody can report itself through the POS print agent as well as through its payment-terminal capability. With the corrected API, an older generic Android hardware report does not downgrade a terminal capability already registered for that active device. Its integrated printer keeps the same owned parent, identity, profiles and job history. This is not automatic discovery or payment certification, and it does not turn an ordinary tablet into a payment terminal. If the device or integrated printer becomes unavailable after an update, retain the error and have the device inventory and API version checked. Do not forget, recreate or move devices between restaurants as a workaround, and do not resend queued documents or payments merely to test connectivity. Forgotten devices remain forgotten; printer routing and uncertain-job recovery still require their own checks.

<span id="devices" />

## Configure devices

Find the correct device, review its identity, active state and selected POS profile. Do not reuse an activation code across restaurants. A staff role and a device profile have different responsibilities; check both if a screen or action is unavailable. After forgetting or replacing a device, check the current device's mode assignment explicitly. Cleanup of a forgotten assignment does not transfer the profile to a replacement; see [saving settings after a forgotten device](/en/help/settings/pos-behavior#forgotten-device-assignment) for compatible-version requirements and recovery.

<span id="stations" />

## Set kitchen stations

Create or edit the station and assign its allowed item categories and device access. Review priority and completion behavior. An item can route to its configured production station while keeping its customer-facing menu group unchanged.

<span id="service" />

## Work with kitchen tickets

Open the assigned KDS, inspect released tickets and advance or complete them using permitted controls. Held food is not a released ticket. Recall/priority actions must target the displayed ticket; do not re-send an order merely because its station is offline.
