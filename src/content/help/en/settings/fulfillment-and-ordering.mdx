Open Settings → Orders and Fulfillment. These rules affect customer eligibility, beyond the visual Website editor.

<span id="channels" />

## Choose ordering channels

Review enabled pickup, delivery and table modes and their minimums, fees and preparation settings. Change one rule at a time and inspect its customer-facing explanation. Public ordering can be paused independently of restaurant opening hours.

<span id="schedule" />

## Set scheduling limits

Review immediate versus scheduled ordering, lead time, booking horizon and time-slot settings. Batch preorder cutoffs and delivery-tour dates have separate configuration. Verify that an offered slot satisfies all applicable rules.

<span id="test-access" />

## Use temporary test access carefully

Temporary test access requires a specific public IP and expiry within seven days. Activation pauses public ordering. Tests create real orders and notifications; use development for test data. Expiry or revocation leaves ordering paused: resume explicitly when ready. Never publish an allowed IP list in a guide or message.

<span id="courier-departure-sync" />

## Follow an assigned delivery’s departure

When the assigned courier starts their route in Admin, unfinished accepted, preparing and ready delivery orders change together to Out for delivery. Staff screens receive the updated statuses through their usual order refresh. This confirms actual departure; assigning a courier or choosing a fulfillment mode alone does not start a route. Held kitchen courses and unsettled weight orders must be resolved first. If starting fails, the previous statuses remain. The route action does not reopen finished or cancelled orders.
