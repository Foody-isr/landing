Open More/Plus on the POS. Destinations are filtered by role and device capabilities.

<span id="navigation" />

## Find operational workspaces

Use Home shortcuts or More for Analytics, Kitchen, Staff and Settings. Orders contains live, scheduled calendar, production matrix and history views. A missing destination can reflect permission or activation, so check the account/profile instead of reinstalling immediately.

<span id="preferences" />

## Customize the device

In Settings choose Appearance and Shortcuts to adjust supported theme/layout and home actions. Hardware and Checkout have separate panels. Review the installed version in account/settings when reporting a problem; do not share a PIN or activation code.

<span id="connectivity" />

## Handle connectivity problems

Check Internet, the selected restaurant/device and explicit connection status. Refresh before repeating a save, kitchen send or payment. A cloud POS does not guarantee offline ordering, payment or printing; an unknown state must be reconciled before another action.
