Open Chain/Branches or the chain dashboard with an authorized account.

<span id="scope" />

## Choose the right branch

Select the chain and branch, then verify the active restaurant context before editing menu, stock, staff or payments. Shared chain presentation does not make restaurant records interchangeable.

<span id="publication" />

## Review public branch presence

Maintain branch information and available website/ordering presence, then check the chain selector and branch destination. Confirm active menus, hours, fulfillment and payment configuration independently for each restaurant before publication.
