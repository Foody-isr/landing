## Overview

When your restaurant owner adds you as a staff member, you will receive an invite link by email or from your manager directly. This guide walks you through logging in for the first time.

## Step 1 — Open FoodyPOS on Your Tablet

Launch the **FoodyPOS** app on your iPad or Android tablet. You will see the login screen with a PIN pad.

## Step 2 — Enter Your Staff PIN

Your manager will provide you with a 4-digit PIN. Enter it on the PIN pad to access the app.

> **Tip:** You can change your PIN later from **Settings → My Account → Change PIN**.

## Step 3 — Select Your Role

If you work in multiple stations (e.g., cashier and server), select the appropriate role for this session.

## Step 4 — Start Your Shift

Once logged in, you will land on the floor plan view. Tap any table to begin taking orders.

## Need Help?

Contact your manager or visit the [FoodyPOS Help Center](/help) for more guides.
