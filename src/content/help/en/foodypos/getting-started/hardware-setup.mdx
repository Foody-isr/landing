## Supported Hardware

FoodyPOS works with the following hardware:

- **Receipt printers**: Epson TM-T20, TM-T88 series (Bluetooth or Wi-Fi)
- **Cash drawers**: RJ12 connected to Epson printers
- **Card readers**: PayPlus or Summit integrated terminals

## Connecting a Printer

1. Power on your Epson printer.
2. On your tablet, go to **Settings → Printers**.
3. Tap **Add Printer** and select the connection type (Bluetooth or Wi-Fi).
4. Select your printer from the list and tap **Connect**.
5. Print a test receipt to confirm the connection.

## Connecting a Cash Drawer

The cash drawer connects via the RJ12 port on the back of your Epson printer. No additional setup is required — the drawer will open automatically when a cash payment is processed.

## Card Readers

Card terminal setup is handled by your payment provider (PayPlus or Summit). Contact your manager if the terminal is not configured.
