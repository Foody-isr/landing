## What Are Staff PINs?

Each staff member logs into FoodyPOS with a 4-digit PIN instead of a password. This makes shift changes fast and keeps the app secure.

## Adding a New PIN

1. Go to **Settings → Staff** (manager access required).
2. Tap **Add Staff Member**.
3. Enter the staff member's name and a 4-digit PIN.
4. Assign a role: **Server**, **Cashier**, or **Kitchen**.
5. Tap **Save**.

## Changing a PIN

1. Go to **Settings → Staff**.
2. Tap the staff member's name.
3. Tap **Change PIN** and enter the new 4-digit PIN.
4. Tap **Save**.

## Removing a Staff Member

1. Go to **Settings → Staff**.
2. Tap the staff member's name.
3. Tap **Remove** and confirm.

The staff member will no longer be able to log in with their PIN.

> **Note:** Removing a staff member from the POS does not affect their account in FoodyAdmin.
