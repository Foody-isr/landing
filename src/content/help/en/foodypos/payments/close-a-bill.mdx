## Closing a Bill

1. Open the table from the floor plan.
2. Tap **Charge** (bottom right of the cart).
3. Select the payment method:
   - **Card** — The card terminal will prompt the customer to tap or insert their card.
   - **Cash** — Enter the amount tendered; FoodyPOS will calculate the change.
   - **QR** — A payment QR code is displayed for the customer to scan.
4. Once payment is confirmed, the receipt prints automatically.

## Splitting a Bill

To split the bill between guests:

1. On the payment screen, tap **Split**.
2. Choose **Split Evenly** (by number of guests) or **Split by Item** (each guest pays for their items).
3. Process each part separately.

## Printing a Duplicate Receipt

After the bill is closed, tap **Reprint Receipt** from the order history to print again.

## Partial Payments

If a customer pays part of the bill in cash and the rest by card:

1. Tap **Charge → Cash** and enter the cash amount.
2. The remaining balance will be shown.
3. Tap **Charge → Card** to process the remainder.
