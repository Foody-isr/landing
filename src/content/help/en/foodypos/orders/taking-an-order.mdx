## Adding Items to an Order

1. Open a table from the floor plan.
2. Browse the menu categories on the left panel.
3. Tap an item to add it to the order. It appears in the cart on the right.
4. To add the same item multiple times, tap it again or use the **+** button on the cart line.

## Applying Modifiers

Some items have modifiers (add-ons or removals):

1. Tap an item in the cart to open the modifier panel.
2. Select the desired options (e.g., "No onions", "Extra cheese +₪5").
3. Tap **Confirm** to apply the modifiers.

The item in the cart will show a summary of the selected options and any price adjustments.

## Adding a Note to an Item

To add a special instruction:

1. Long-press an item in the cart.
2. Type your note in the **Special Instructions** field.
3. Tap **Save**.

## Sending to Kitchen

Once the order is ready to be prepared:

1. Tap **Send to Kitchen** at the bottom of the cart.
2. A kitchen ticket is printed automatically (if a kitchen printer is configured).
3. The table status changes to orange (in kitchen).

> **Note:** Items sent to the kitchen cannot be removed without manager approval.
