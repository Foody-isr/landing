## What is the Kitchen Display?

The Kitchen Display System (KDS) shows all active kitchen tickets in real time. It replaces paper tickets and helps your kitchen team stay organized during busy service.

## Ticket Lifecycle

1. **New** — A ticket appears when a waiter taps "Send to Kitchen".
2. **In Progress** — Tap the ticket to mark it as being prepared.
3. **Ready** — Tap **Mark Ready** when the food is plated. The waiter receives a notification on their device.
4. **Completed** — The ticket is archived but can be recalled.

## Recalling a Ticket

If you accidentally mark a ticket as ready:

1. Tap **Recall** in the top-right corner.
2. Select the ticket from the recent list.
3. The ticket reappears in the active queue.

## Filtering by Station

If your kitchen has multiple stations (grill, cold, pastry), tap the **Station** filter at the top of the KDS to show only tickets relevant to your station.
