In the service editor open customer flow and pricing. Use the simulator before publishing.

<span id="steps" />

## Configure customer steps

Maintain step order, required questions and single-choice options. Configure scheduling, guest count, event details and selections supported by the service. Check translated labels and every branch of the flow; changing a question can affect pricing conditions.

<span id="rules" />

## Maintain pricing rules

Choose the item, base/fallback rate and conditional rules such as guest count, weekday, slot or a selected answer. Avoid overlapping specific rules. In the simulator choose a representative context and inspect the resolved rate and any conflict warning before saving.
