Admin → Staff → Roles and permissions

## Availability and access

Requires role-management permission; restaurant scope always applies.

<span id="create" />

## Create a role

Open the role library, create a named role and select the required permissions. Save before assigning employees.

<span id="assign" />

## Assign and verify

Assign the role from the employee profile. Verify the permitted pages and actions with that employee; menu visibility alone is not proof of authorization. The Verifone Terminal deposits screen and its batch-changing actions require payments.manage; payments.view is not enough.

<span id="change" />

## Change or retire a role

Review assigned employees before changing permissions or deleting a role. Respect protected system/owner roles and check resulting access after saving.

<span id="related-service-controls" />

## Access the new controls

Settings → Usage and quotas displays this restaurant’s Google/Geoapify allowances and usage with settings view/edit permission. Fallback credits and deliveries submitted for optimization are distinct from request counts. Foody adjusts limits without resetting counters. See [limits and renewal](/en/help/settings/api-usage-and-quotas).

<span id="staff-activation-pos-code" />

## POS code at staff activation

Staff activation checks the permissions assigned in this restaurant, including custom roles: orders.manage or payments.manage requires a private POS code. Read-only couriers and kitchen-only roles do not need one. Existing unused invitation links apply the same current rule. Changing a role label, courier address or route destination does not grant POS permissions. See [activation and recovery](/en/help/getting-started/first-login#activate).

<span id="end-of-day-permissions" />

## Authorize end-of-day closing

End of day requires payments.manage on an already enrolled POS with an active personal PIN session. Including a cash drawer also requires orders.manage. Permissions are checked again on each write; opening a screen or reading a capability grants none. Transmission and finalization remain bound to the original actor and relevant POS. Changing role or signing into another device does not transfer an operation. See [the steps and recovery](/en/help/payments/end-of-day).
