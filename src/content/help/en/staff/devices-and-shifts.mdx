Admin → Staff → POS access / Shift reports

<span id="pending-cash-sign-out" />

## Finish the report before signing out

Sign out in navigation and End shift in More use the same check, scoped to the active POS and restaurant. With orders.manage or payments.manage, Foody checks the report before offering to end the shift. An open drawer, even with no sale or movement, or an unconfirmed local cash operation shows Cash drawer report pending. Cancel changes nothing. Finish report opens More → Reports → Cash drawer balance; it does not close the drawer, use expected cash as a physical count, or sign you out. Resume uncertain operations using their existing identity. To close, an operator with orders.manage physically counts the cash present and confirms closure. Then request sign-out again and confirm End shift to return to PIN while keeping the device enrolled. Closing cash alone does not automatically end your staff shift. Without report permissions, the server still checks the drawer: if open, ask an authorized manager; the message does not grant report access. If verification fails, the session stays open: check the connection and Retry without clearing local data. The server also rechecks after confirmation in case a drawer opened in the meantime. The Verifone card deposit stays separate and explicit: neither this message nor sign-out starts it. Revoked or expired access can require a security sign-out; this neither counts nor closes cash and does not erase recovery journals.

<span id="pos-reports-navigation" />

## Distinguish reports, cash and card deposits

On a tablet, More → Reports opens Cash drawer balance when your role permits, otherwise permitted Sales or Terminal deposits. The left rail contains only accessible sections. Choosing Cash movements replaces the content within this workspace without stacking another Reports page. Cash movements shows the session loaded on this device with date, type, amount and reason; it is not a history of other sessions. On a phone, Reports remains a destination list. Opening or refreshing a report closes nothing. Closing physical cash and the terminal’s Close and deposit remain separate actions even when both belong to the same end-of-day procedure.

<span id="pair" />

## Activate a POS device

Review the restaurant device list and follow the device setup pairing flow. Confirm the device belongs to the intended restaurant before authorizing access.

<span id="pin" />

## Use a personal POS code

After activation, use the configured employee POS code. End your service or switch employee using the POS controls instead of sharing a manager session.

<span id="report" />

## Review shift reports

Choose the report period and employee/device filters, then inspect recorded start/end times. A missing end time must be investigated rather than assumed closed.

<span id="finish-with-cash-drawer" />

## Finish a shift with an open cash drawer

If End shift says the cash drawer is open, open More → Reports → Cash drawer balance on the same enrolled POS. Resolve any pending cash operation, count the physical cash and close that device's cash session before trying End shift again. The shift is not ended by a Verifone card-batch deposit, and ending a shift does not itself transmit a card batch. If the count or payment result is uncertain, reconcile it with an authorized manager instead of forcing a sign-out or taking a second payment.

<span id="mobile-terminal-pages" />

## Read the cash-drawer report on a handheld

More → Reports → Cash drawer uses a vertical page on a handheld terminal. Amounts, sessions, permissions and confirmations retain their existing behavior. Colors and insets do not turn a report into a drawer closure or a card deposit.
