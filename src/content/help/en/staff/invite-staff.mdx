## Roles in Foody

Each staff member is assigned a role that determines what they can access.

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Full access: menu, orders, staff, billing, all settings |
| **Manager** | Full access except billing and owner-only settings |
| **Staff** | FoodyPOS app (orders, tables) — limited FoodyAdmin access |

---

## Inviting a Team Member

Staff management is done in **FoodyAdmin**.

1. Go to **Staff** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Invite Staff Member**.
3. Fill in:
   - First and last name
   - Email address
   - Role (Owner, Manager, or Staff)
   - A temporary password
4. Click **Invite**.

The staff member receives an email with their login link. They can log in to **FoodyAdmin** (if Manager/Owner) or **FoodyPOS** (all roles) using these credentials.

> **Note:** There is no PIN-based login. Every staff member logs in with their email and password.

---

## Changing a Role

1. Go to **Staff**.
2. Click the staff member's name.
3. Change the role from the dropdown.
4. Click **Save**.

Only **Owners** can change roles.

---

## Removing a Staff Member

1. Go to **Staff**.
2. Click the staff member's name.
3. Click **Remove** and confirm.

The staff member loses access immediately on all devices.

---

## Managing Roles and Permissions

For more granular control over what each role can do, go to **Roles** in the sidebar. You can adjust which sections and actions are accessible per role.
