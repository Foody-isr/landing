Open Settings → Stories and the supported social connection controls.

<span id="connection" />

## Connect and select content

Use the offered authorization flow, select permitted content and check its public visibility. External account permissions and media availability can expire. Reconnect through the protected flow rather than sharing an access token.

<span id="display" />

## Verify the stories page

Open the restaurant’s public Stories page on mobile and check media, order and links. A social media connection does not automatically publish unrelated Website draft changes. Remove unavailable content using the offered controls.
