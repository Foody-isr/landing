## The Dashboard Overview

The FoodyAdmin dashboard gives you a live view of your restaurant's performance. Open it from the **Dashboard** section in the sidebar.

## Key Metrics

| Metric | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| **Revenue Today** | Total sales for the current calendar day |
| **Orders Today** | Number of orders processed today |
| **Top Sellers** | Your 5 best-selling items by revenue |
| **Recent Orders** | Last 10 orders with status |

## Changing the Date Range

Click the **Date** selector at the top of the Analytics section to filter data by:
- Today
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Custom range

## Exporting Data

In **Analytics → Overview**, click **Export** to download a CSV of your sales data for the selected date range.
