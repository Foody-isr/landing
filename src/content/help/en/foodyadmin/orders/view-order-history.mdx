## Accessing Order History

Go to **Orders → All Orders** in the sidebar to see a complete list of orders, including past orders.

## Filtering Orders

Use the filter bar at the top to narrow results by:

- **Status**: Pending, Accepted, In Kitchen, Ready, Served, Paid
- **Date range**: Today, last 7 days, last 30 days, or custom
- **Order type**: Dine-in, Pickup, Delivery, QR
- **Table**: Filter by specific table

## Reading an Order

Each order row shows:
- Order ID
- Time
- Table or order type
- Customer name (if provided)
- Total amount
- Status badge

Click an order to view the full breakdown: items, modifiers, applied discounts, payment method.

## Exporting Orders

Click **Export CSV** in the top-right to download the current filtered view as a spreadsheet for accounting or reporting.
