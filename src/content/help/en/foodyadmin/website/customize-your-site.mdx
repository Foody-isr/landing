## What is the Foody Website?

Every restaurant on Foody gets a branded ordering website where customers can browse the menu and place orders by scanning a QR code. You can fully customize the look and feel from FoodyAdmin.

## Accessing the Website Builder

Go to **Website** in the sidebar. You will see a live preview of your site on the right and the settings panel on the left.

## What You Can Customize

- **Logo** — Upload your restaurant logo (PNG or SVG, recommended 200×200 px)
- **Primary color** — Sets buttons, highlights, and accents
- **Hero image** — The banner image at the top of your ordering page
- **Restaurant name & tagline** — Shown below your logo
- **Social links** — Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp
- **Sections visibility** — Show or hide sections like "About Us" and "Promotions"

## Saving Changes

Changes in the website builder are previewed in real time. Click **Save** to publish the changes to your live site.

## Your Site URL

Your ordering website is available at:
`https://[your-restaurant-slug].foody-pos.co.il`

You can find and share this URL from the **Website** page. Use it to generate QR codes for your tables.
