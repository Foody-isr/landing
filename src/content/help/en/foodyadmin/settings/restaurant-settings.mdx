## Restaurant Information

Go to **Settings → Restaurant Info** to update:

- **Name** — your restaurant's display name
- **Address** — shown on receipts and the ordering site
- **Phone number** — for customer contact
- **Description** — a short bio shown on your QR menu

Click **Save** after making changes.

## Operational Settings

Go to **Settings → Operational Settings** to configure:

### Order Approval
- **Auto-accept** — Orders are automatically confirmed (recommended for QR ordering)
- **Manual approve** — Each order must be approved by a staff member before it appears in the kitchen

### Service Modes
Toggle on/off the service types your restaurant supports:
- Dine-in
- Pickup
- Delivery

### Rush Mode
Enable **Rush Mode** during busy periods to hide estimated wait times and streamline the order flow.

### Tip Settings
Configure whether to offer tips on the payment screen and set the suggested tip percentages.
