## Creating a New Item

1. Go to **Menu → Items**.
2. Click **New Item**.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Name** — what the customer sees
   - **Category** — which section of the menu it belongs to
   - **Price** — in ₪ (ILS)
4. Optionally add:
   - **Description** — a short description shown to customers
   - **Photo** — upload an image (JPG or PNG, max 5 MB)
5. Click **Save**.

## Setting Item Availability

Toggle **Available** off to temporarily hide the item without deleting it. This is useful when an ingredient runs out during service.

## Adding Modifiers

Modifiers let customers customize items (e.g., "No onions", "Extra cheese +₪5").

1. On the item edit page, scroll to **Modifiers**.
2. Click **Add Modifier Group** (e.g., "Cooking Level", "Add-ons").
3. Add modifier options with optional price adjustments.
4. Set whether the modifier is **required** (customer must choose) or **optional**.
5. Click **Save**.

> **Tip:** Modifier groups can be shared across multiple items. Create them once in **Menu → Modifiers** and link them to any item.
