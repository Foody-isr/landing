## What Are Categories?

Categories group your menu items so customers can browse easily. Examples: "Starters", "Pizzas", "Drinks", "Desserts".

## Creating a Category

1. Go to **Menu → Categories**.
2. Click **Add Category**.
3. Enter the category name (shown to customers).
4. Optionally add a description.
5. Set the **Display Order** to control where it appears in the menu.
6. Click **Save**.

## Reordering Categories

Drag and drop categories in the list to change their display order. Changes are saved automatically.

## Hiding a Category

To temporarily hide a category (e.g., seasonal items):

1. Click the category name.
2. Toggle **Visible** to off.
3. Click **Save**.

Hidden categories are not shown to customers on the QR menu or FoodyPOS, but they remain in your admin for future use.
