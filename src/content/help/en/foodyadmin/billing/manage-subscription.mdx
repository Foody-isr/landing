## Your Current Plan

Go to **Billing** in the sidebar to see:
- Your active plan (Starter, Premium, or Enterprise)
- Subscription status (Active, Trial, Past Due)
- Next billing date
- Monthly order usage

## Available Plans

| Plan | Price | Best For |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Starter** | ₪299/month | Small cafes, quick service |
| **Premium** | ₪799/month | Full-service restaurants |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Multi-location chains |

## Upgrading Your Plan

1. Go to **Billing**.
2. Click **Change Plan**.
3. Select your new plan.
4. Confirm the change.

Upgrades take effect immediately. You will be charged the pro-rated difference for the remainder of the current billing period.

## Viewing Invoices

Under **Billing → Payment History**, you can see and download all past invoices as PDFs.

## Cancelling

To cancel your subscription, contact us at support@foody-pos.co.il. Your account will remain active until the end of the current billing period.
