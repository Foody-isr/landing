## Roles in Foody

| Role | Access |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Full access to all settings, billing, and staff |
| **Manager** | All features except billing and owner settings |
| **Staff** | POS access only (via staff PIN) |

## Inviting a New Team Member

1. Go to **Staff** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Invite Staff Member**.
3. Fill in:
   - First and last name
   - Email address
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Role
   - Temporary password
4. Click **Send Invite**.

The staff member will receive an email with their login credentials for FoodyAdmin (if they are a manager or owner) and can set their own password.

## Changing a Role

1. Go to **Staff**.
2. Click the staff member's name.
3. Select a new role from the dropdown.
4. Click **Save**.

> **Note:** Only owners can change staff roles.

## Removing a Staff Member

1. Go to **Staff**.
2. Click the staff member's name.
3. Click **Remove** and confirm.

The staff member will lose access immediately.
