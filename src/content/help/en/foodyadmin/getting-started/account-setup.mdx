## Overview

FoodyAdmin is the web dashboard where you manage everything about your restaurant — menu, staff, orders, analytics, and billing. This guide walks you through the initial setup.

## Step 1 — Accept Your Invite

When Foody creates your account, you receive an invite email with a setup link. Click the link and set your password to activate your account.

## Step 2 — Complete Your Restaurant Profile

1. Log in at [admin.foody-pos.co.il](https://admin.foody-pos.co.il).
2. Go to **Settings → Restaurant Info**.
3. Fill in:
   - Restaurant name
   - Address
   - Phone number
   - Description (shown to customers on your QR menu)
4. Click **Save**.

## Step 3 — Set Your Service Mode

In **Settings → Operational Settings**, choose how orders flow through your restaurant:

- **Dine-in** — Table ordering via FoodyPOS or QR codes
- **Pickup** — Customers order online and collect at the counter
- **Delivery** — You handle delivery yourself

## Step 4 — Invite Your Staff

Go to **Staff** and click **Invite Staff Member** to add your team. See [Managing Staff](/help/foodyadmin/staff/invite-staff) for details.
