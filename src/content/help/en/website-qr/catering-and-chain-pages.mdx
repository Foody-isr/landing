Select the catering or chain order-entry page in Website. The service catalog and branch activation are managed separately.

<span id="catering-copy" />

## Customize catering presentation

Edit the page heading, introduction, service subtitles and available layout options. An empty override inherits the original service text; use reset to restore inheritance. Only active services can be presented. Confirm the service’s separate availability and quote settings.

<span id="chain-entry" />

## Customize branch selection

Edit branch-selector copy in French, Hebrew and English and the available appearance options. Check that the chain page links to the intended active branch and ordering mode. Publishing a design does not grant a branch new permissions, a menu or payment credentials.
