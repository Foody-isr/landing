In Website open the order journey screens. Their appearance is separate from payment-provider configuration.

<span id="checkout" />

## Preview checkout and confirmation

Select checkout then confirmation, adjust available colors and text, and inspect mobile readability. Required customer, fulfillment and payment fields come from restaurant policy. A preview does not place an order or verify a live payment connection.

<span id="receipt" />

## Set receipt content and actions

On the receipt screen set localized title/message and compact or comfortable layout. Toggle logo, address, phone, status and customer information, then print, share, order history and new-order actions. Preview each language; publish to make the settings public. These controls do not alter the recorded transaction or fiscal document.
