In Website open the order journey screens. Their appearance is separate from payment-provider configuration.

<span id="checkout" />

## Preview checkout and confirmation

Select checkout then confirmation, adjust available colors and text, and inspect mobile readability. Required customer, fulfillment and payment fields come from restaurant policy. A preview does not place an order or verify a live payment connection. When the checkout calendar is shown, use the month menu to jump to another month, pick an available day and choose its time. If the order page requires a service choice first, cart and checkout retain the selected service. Return to the menu and use Back to order options to switch. Adding from a Web Story or continuing shopping from the cart opens the menu directly. If you have yet to choose, select pickup or delivery on the menu or in the cart before payment.

<span id="contact-fields" />

## Choose the fields customers fill in

With access to edit the site, open Website → Order journey → Contact and payment. Choose Delivery or Pickup in Preview order type. In Form fields, use the switch to show or hide a field. Open its row and choose Required or Optional. For delivery, show Floor, Apartment / unit, Building code or Delivery notes as needed. If a field is missing from an older form, select it under Add a built-in field so its answer is recorded with the delivery details. The phone remains required. If address suggestions are enabled, the customer must select a delivery address. Use Check fields in the live preview to test an empty required field without placing an order. Wait for the draft to save, then Publish and reopen the public checkout to verify. If the draft has not been published, customers still see the previous settings.

<span id="receipt" />

## Set receipt content and actions

On the receipt screen set localized title/message and compact or comfortable layout. Toggle logo, address, phone, status and customer information, then print, share, order history and new-order actions. Preview each language; publish to make the settings public. These controls do not alter the recorded transaction or fiscal document. Payment labels distinguish partial payment from an unpaid order; a recorded handoff remains separate from payment.

<span id="link-preview" />

## Check receipt and confirmation link previews

Check that the restaurant has a name and logo configured, then share a valid receipt or order-confirmation link with its intended recipient. The link preview uses that restaurant’s name and logo, independently of the website’s general share image. Without a logo, the preview shows a card with the restaurant’s name. It does not include customer details, order lines or amounts. The linked page can still contain private order information: do not publish the link publicly. If the wrong identity appears, check that the link belongs to the intended restaurant and that its configured logo is accessible. An expired or invalid receipt cannot provide a restaurant preview.

<span id="optional-entry-sign-in" />

## Choose whether entry offers sign-in

With Choose the order mode before the menu enabled, customers go directly from their service choice to a separate pickup or delivery screen. The sign-in invitation applies to the other entry flow. In Website → Order journey, set Show the sign-in invitation on arrival. Off: customers go directly to pickup or delivery details. On: Order without an account is immediately visible, with optional sign-in alongside it. Check the preview, save the draft and publish the site to apply the setting. Saving a draft does not change the public site. Preview does not sign in a real account. This preference does not remove checkout sign-in or mandatory phone verification. Existing configurations without this field retain the invitation; explicitly turn it off and publish for direct ordering. Service allowances are separate under Settings → Usage and quotas. When the invitation is off, a discreet Sign in link remains available in the pickup or delivery dialog. It opens authentication only when selected; dismissing authentication retains entered details and allows ordering without an account.
