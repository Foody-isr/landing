## Open the website editor

In FoodyAdmin, open your restaurant’s **Website** workspace. Choose a page or section and edit its content, images or appearance. The preview shows the current draft.

<span id="section-2" />

## Saving is different from publishing

Changes are saved as a draft. An autosaved draft does not replace the site your customers see.

1. Edit the required page or section.
2. Wait for the draft to finish saving and check the preview.
3. Use **Publish** to make the reviewed draft public.
4. Wait for a successful result, then verify the public link on the intended device.

If publishing fails, the preview alone does not prove the public site changed. Keep your draft and resolve the reported error. **Discard** restores the published state and removes unpublished edits; use it only when you intend to abandon the draft.

<span id="campaign-compatibility" />

## Check availability of campaign presentations

Pink Coffee, the Campaign hero/card presentation and Display headings require a compatible public Website version before Admin enables their controls. If a control is unavailable or publication reports an incompatible public renderer, keep the draft, ask Foody to update the matching Website version, then reload the editor and review the preview again. Existing presentations retain their normal editing and publication flow. A saved draft or gallery preview is not a production release. Configure the new options in [design and branding](/en/help/website-qr/design-and-branding#pink-coffee-template) and [sections and media](/en/help/website-qr/sections-and-media#campaign-hero), then publish only after checking desktop, mobile, links and item choices. New carousel layout, menu icon, grid width and individual product background controls also require their compatible release. They can remain disabled while an existing Campaign hero remains editable.

<span id="section-3" />

## Menu and ordering content

Website appearance does not replace menu configuration. Items must belong to a menu group with the right channel and hours. Check the ordering page and the relevant pickup, delivery or table context after publishing.

[Menus and groups](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups)

<span id="mobile-fulfillment-review" />

## Review mobile order entry

When the invitation is enabled, on mobile and desktop, verify that Order without an account is immediately visible and usable, with Sign in as an option. No address field or confirmation should appear before choosing. Check opening/closing sign-in and guest access: address entry for Delivery, collection details without a delivery address for Pickup. Back must retain the draft; switching services or reopening the dialog must not repeat the guest choice. Also check a signed-in customer’s validated address card and Edit. In a 360 px visual viewport with at least four results, four whole 44 px suggestions must remain visible. The title, tabs, Back and input keep their positions when the keyboard opens or closes. Check 320 px screens, desktop and French, Hebrew and English. Sign in opens Google and the email/password form. Facebook and Apple appear only when configured by the service. Standalone email-code sign-in is no longer offered. Account creation, address verification and password-recovery codes remain separate. Check dismissal and resumption without losing the draft on mobile and desktop; four whole suggestions with the keyboard open retain the same geometry. Sign-in choices must not reintroduce moving tabs or input.

<span id="related-service-controls" />

## Access the new controls

The entry sign-in invitation is controlled in Website → Order journey → Show the sign-in invitation on arrival. Off goes directly to pickup/delivery details; when on, Order without an account remains immediately visible. Save the draft and publish to apply this choice. See [the journey setting](/en/help/website-qr/checkout-and-receipt#optional-entry-sign-in). When the invitation is off, a discreet Sign in link remains available in the pickup or delivery dialog. It opens authentication only when selected; dismissing authentication retains entered details and allows ordering without an account.

<span id="menu-and-category-text-colors" />

## Set menu and category text colors separately

In Site design → Colors, select the style assigned to Item list, then open Customize menu colors → Category menu. Menu text controls the names and underline of menu tabs, such as BASSARI and HALAVI. Category text controls unselected group links, search and its icons. The selected group keeps its active text setting. The two colors are independent: changing or resetting one field to automatic does not change the other. Without a chosen menu color, tabs use the style’s paragraph color when readable against the bar, otherwise a suitable light or dark text color. Check both levels in preview, reload to confirm draft saving, then publish and review desktop and mobile. If the wrong color appears, check the style assigned to Item list; the Wolt preset uses its dedicated palette. In Item list, Category menu style applies the same Plain text, Pill, Rounded or Underlined choice on desktop and mobile. Menu tabs remain separate and underlined.
