## Open the website editor

In FoodyAdmin, open your restaurant’s **Website** workspace. Choose a page or section and edit its content, images or appearance. The preview shows the current draft.

<span id="section-2" />

## Saving is different from publishing

Changes are saved as a draft. An autosaved draft does not replace the site your customers see.

1. Edit the required page or section.
2. Wait for the draft to finish saving and check the preview.
3. Use **Publish** to make the reviewed draft public.
4. Wait for a successful result, then verify the public link on the intended device.

If publishing fails, the preview alone does not prove the public site changed. Keep your draft and resolve the reported error. **Discard** restores the published state and removes unpublished edits; use it only when you intend to abandon the draft.

<span id="section-3" />

## Menu and ordering content

Website appearance does not replace menu configuration. Items must belong to a menu group with the right channel and hours. Check the ordering page and the relevant pickup, delivery or table context after publishing.

[Menus and groups](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups)

<span id="mobile-fulfillment-review" />

## Review mobile order entry

Before publishing a site release, open its order page on a small phone. Check Pickup and Delivery, then enter an address with the keyboard open: the field, scrolling suggestions, top close button and confirmation must remain reachable. Select a complete address, check that the keyboard dismisses and delivery and time checks finish before confirmation. Optional account access appears after the ordering choices. Check French, Hebrew and English using the restaurant colors. Publish the updated procedure with the site correction.
