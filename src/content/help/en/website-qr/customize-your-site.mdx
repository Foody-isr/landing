## What Is the Foody Ordering Website?

Every restaurant on Foody gets a branded ordering website. Customers visit it by scanning a QR code (at the table or on a takeaway flyer) or through a direct link. They can browse your menu and place orders without downloading an app.

Your site URL is: `https://[your-slug].foody-pos.co.il`

---

## Opening the Website Builder

In FoodyAdmin, go to **Website** in the sidebar. The left panel has settings; the right panel shows a live preview that updates as you make changes.

---

## What You Can Customize

- **Logo** — Upload your restaurant logo (PNG or SVG, recommended 200×200 px)
- **Primary color** — Sets buttons, highlights, and accents
- **Hero image** — The banner at the top of the ordering page
- **Restaurant name & tagline** — Shown below your logo
- **Social links** — Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp
- **Section visibility** — Show or hide sections such as "About Us"

---

## Publishing Changes

Changes in the website builder preview in real time. Click **Save** to publish them live. Customers will see the updates immediately (no deploy needed).

---

## QR Codes

### For the whole restaurant (pickup/delivery)
Your general ordering link can be turned into a QR code from the **Website** page.

### Per table (dine-in)
Each table in your floor plan has a unique QR code.

1. Go to **Restaurant → Floor Plans**.
2. Open a floor plan.
3. Click a table.
4. Click **Download QR Code** (PNG or PDF).
5. Print and place it on the physical table.

When a customer scans this QR code, the ordering page opens pre-set to their table so their order goes directly to the right place.
