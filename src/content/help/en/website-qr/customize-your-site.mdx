## Open the website editor

In FoodyAdmin, open your restaurant’s **Website** workspace. Choose a page or section and edit its content, images or appearance. The preview shows the current draft.

<span id="section-2" />

## Saving is different from publishing

Changes are saved as a draft. An autosaved draft does not replace the site your customers see.

1. Edit the required page or section.
2. Wait for the draft to finish saving and check the preview.
3. Use **Publish** to make the reviewed draft public.
4. Wait for a successful result, then verify the public link on the intended device.

If publishing fails, the preview alone does not prove the public site changed. Keep your draft and resolve the reported error. **Discard** restores the published state and removes unpublished edits; use it only when you intend to abandon the draft.

<span id="section-3" />

## Menu and ordering content

Website appearance does not replace menu configuration. Items must belong to a menu group with the right channel and hours. Check the ordering page and the relevant pickup, delivery or table context after publishing.

## Review mobile order entry

On mobile and desktop, review the initial Sign in and Continue as a guest choices as text actions without an additional yellow button. No address field or confirmation should appear before choosing. Check opening/closing sign-in and guest access: address entry for Delivery, collection details without a delivery address for Pickup. Back must retain the draft; switching services or reopening the dialog must not repeat the guest choice. Also check a signed-in customer’s validated address card and Edit. In a 360 px visual viewport with at least four results, four whole 44 px suggestions must remain visible. The title, tabs, Back and input keep their positions when the keyboard opens or closes. Check 320 px screens, desktop and French, Hebrew and English. Sign in opens Google and the email/password form. Facebook and Apple appear only when configured by the service. Standalone email-code sign-in is no longer offered. Account creation, address verification and password-recovery codes remain separate. Check dismissal and resumption without losing the draft on mobile and desktop; four whole suggestions with the keyboard open retain the same geometry. Sign-in choices must not reintroduce moving tabs or input.
