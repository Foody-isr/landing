Admin → Account → Billing, when offered

<span id="read" />

## Read the current subscription

Check the recorded plan, subscription status and relevant trial/renewal/grace date. Confirm commercial terms with Foody; technical plan labels can differ from current offers.

<span id="change" />

## Review a plan change

An authorized owner reviews and confirms an offered plan change. Wait for the subscription reread; if uncertain, verify the recorded plan before submitting again.

<span id="events" />

## Review billing events

Inspect event type, date, amount and currency. Contact support for an unexplained failed payment or blocked subscription; an order payment is separate from the software subscription.
