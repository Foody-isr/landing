Admin → Account → Billing, when offered

<span id="read" />

## Read the current subscription

Check the recorded plan, subscription status and relevant trial/renewal/grace date. Confirm commercial terms with Foody; technical plan labels can differ from current offers.

In the test billing portal, open **Subscription** for the accepted plan, billing frequency, next charge including VAT and its date. When automatic renewal is off, the card shows the end of the paid period. Open **Subscription details** to read the accepted terms, or **Manage renewal** to review the period end and confirm a stop. A new offer awaiting acceptance has its own summary.

<span id="change" />

## Review a plan change

An authorized owner reviews and confirms an offered plan change. Wait for the subscription reread; if uncertain, verify the recorded plan before submitting again.

<span id="events" />

## Review billing events

Inspect event type, date, amount and currency. Contact support for an unexplained failed payment or blocked subscription; an order payment is separate from the software subscription.

<span id="versioned-sandbox-additions" />

## Add modules or licences in sandbox

For a new restaurant enrolled in versioned development offers, **Billing** shows active modules and separate account and physical POS quotas. During an active paid period, the owner can open **Add modules or licences**, choose an additional module, a plan composed of modules not already purchased, or licence quantities, and review the offer. A quotation-only row requires a Foody offer. Check the amount due now for the remaining period, the new renewal amount and resulting quantities. Preparing an offer grants no rights. Accept its terms, complete the sandbox payment and verify the status. Added rights appear after server confirmation; the already-paid period keeps its end date. A free addition still requires explicit acceptance. The original contracted rights are retained; historical contracts keep their existing flow. A missing feature displays offers that include it; contact Foody if none is listed. Employees ask the owner to manage the subscription.

For a new subscription to the reviewed catalogue, Restaurant and Online each cost ILS 590/month excluding VAT, Kitchen 890, Restaurant + Online 990 and Integral 1,790 per site. Selecting exactly the duo or Integral modules automatically applies a cheaper published matching plan without reducing included rights. Restaurant and the duo include 2 active devices; Integral includes 3 devices, 1 chef and 1 KDS. Each additional POS device costs ILS 149/month excluding VAT; personal POS accounts are not charged per employee. An authorized device consumes capacity until its authorization is revoked; staff sign-out does not release it. Existing contracts and additions to them are not automatically repriced as Integral. Request an offer to change plans.

Standard monthly software has no minimum term. Annual prepayment covers 12 months for the price of 11 on software modules and plans: ILS 590/month becomes ILS 6,490/year excluding VAT, saving ILS 590. Additional licences, hardware, options and payment services do not receive this discount. Accepted offers, saved invitations and negotiated prices retain their agreed terms.

## Choose annual renewal during a Foody subscription test

If Foody has enabled a private CST offer for your restaurant, open **Settings → Account → Billing** as its owner. With **Annual**, the separate authorization shows the amount for each 12-month renewal. Leave it unchecked for a single prepaid year. After a confirmed recurring checkout, check the next amount and date in Billing or stop automatic renewal there.

If the offer comes from a finalized quotation, check the quotation number, the acceptance deadline and the total including VAT. Billing shows the frequency agreed in the quotation. One-time equipment and usage fees are handled separately. When checkout opens, enter the CST test card on Verifone. If Billing shows an incomplete test payment, refresh the status before trying again. A confirmed payment shows the period covered.

## Find a test subscription invoice

Open **Billing → Invoices**. Each row shows a payment, document status and amount including VAT. Open **Payment details** for the service period, net amount and VAT. Choose **Filters** to search by month, invoice number or document status. Select a month to download its available PDFs as a ZIP. An **Available** invoice can be viewed or downloaded. Enable the test inbox under **Email delivery** to receive confirmations and documents. When offered, **Resend by email** sends the archived PDF. Earlier payments remain visible with their archive status. Pilot documents are for testing.

At the first payment with automatic renewal, Verifone shows the selected plan price including VAT. The monthly option pays for one month and the annual option pays for a full year. This payment authorizes future charges of the same amount at the agreed interval. To stop future charges, open **Billing** and turn off automatic renewal. In the Invoice4U pilot the sale requests an invoice. Its PDF becomes available after Foody verifies the original. Enable email delivery before paying to include the authorized test inbox.

## Choose a plan when your trial ends

In development, sign in as the restaurant owner and open **Settings → Account → Billing → Subscription**. Without a private offer, owners, including design partners, can choose a published plan or individual modules. A dated trial shows its end date. Select the plan and licences, then **Review subscription** to see the monthly and annual totals. Use **Change my selection** to return to the catalogue before payment. Choose the frequency, review VAT and renewal, then confirm the Verifone test checkout. A private offer published by Foody presents the agreed terms directly. **Invoices** remains available before subscribing and after a sandbox billing reset.
