Open Restaurant → Table QR, then Customize or Print.

<span id="design" />

## Customize the QR card

Select a card layout, text, logo/image and colors, then adjust the available typography roles: font, weight, case, size, letter spacing and color. Check Hebrew glyph support and contrast. QR readability takes priority over decoration.

<span id="print" />

## Print and verify table destinations

Choose the intended tables and print/export options. Scan a sample from the actual printed size and verify restaurant/table before placing it in service. Replaced or expired signed links require refreshed cards; do not manually edit encoded IDs/signatures.
