## What Is a Floor Plan?

A floor plan is a visual layout of your dining area. It shows which tables exist, where they are, and lets FoodyPOS staff tap a table to open or manage an order for it.

Floor plans are only used in **Table Mode**. If you're running **Counter Mode**, you don't need a floor plan.

---

## Creating a Floor Plan

Floor plans are created and edited in **FoodyAdmin**.

1. Go to **Restaurant → Floor Plans**.
2. Click **New Floor Plan**.
3. Give it a name (e.g., "Main Room", "Terrace", "Bar").
4. On the canvas, add tables:
   - Click **Add Table** to place a new table.
   - Give each table a name or number (e.g., T1, T2, Window, Bar).
   - Drag tables to position them on the canvas.
5. Click **Save**.

You can create **multiple floor plans** for different areas of your restaurant (indoor, outdoor, bar counter). All floor plans appear as tabs in FoodyPOS.

---

## Using Floor Plans on FoodyPOS

1. Open FoodyPOS.
2. Tap the **Tables** tab.
3. Your floor plans appear as tabs at the top.
4. Tap any available table to open a new order for it.
5. Occupied tables are highlighted to show they have an active session.

---

## Table Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Available (empty) | No active order. Ready for guests |
| Occupied | An order is open for this table |

---

## Editing and Reordering Floor Plans

In FoodyAdmin → Restaurant → Floor Plans:
- Click a floor plan name to edit the canvas layout.
- Drag floor plans in the list to change the tab order in FoodyPOS.
- Click **Delete** to remove a floor plan (only if there are no active orders on it).

---

## QR Codes Per Table

Each table in a floor plan has a unique QR code. Customers scan it to open your ordering page pre-selected to that table.

To access QR codes: open a floor plan in FoodyAdmin → click a table → **Download QR Code**.
