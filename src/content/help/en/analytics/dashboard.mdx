## Accessing the Dashboard

In FoodyAdmin, click **Dashboard** in the sidebar. It shows a live overview of today's performance.

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | What it shows |
|--------|--------------|
| **Revenue Today** | Total sales for the current calendar day (all paid orders) |
| **Orders Today** | Number of orders processed today |
| **Top 5 Sellers** | Your best-selling menu items by revenue |
| **Recent Orders** | Last 10 orders with status and amount |

---

## Analytics Section

For deeper reporting, go to **Analytics** in the sidebar.

### Sales Overview
- Revenue and order count over a date range
- Comparison to previous period

### Top Sellers
- Items ranked by revenue and quantity sold
- Filter by category or date range

### Customer Analytics
- Repeat customers, order frequency, average spend

---

## Changing the Date Range

Use the **date picker** at the top of the Analytics section to select:
- Today
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Custom range

---

## Exporting Data

Click **Export CSV** (available in the Analytics Overview) to download your sales data as a spreadsheet. This is useful for accounting, tax reporting, or sharing with your accountant.
