Open the AI business panel in Admin with an authorized restaurant account. This panel is distinct from the public help assistant.

<span id="question" />

## Ask about business data

Choose the intended restaurant and ask a precise question about current/weekly sales, top items, stock, recent orders, customers, staff or menu. Review the answer against the relevant source screen. AI interpretation is not proof of payment reconciliation or a substitute for accounting.

<span id="limits" />

## Understand the panel’s scope

Available business queries use the signed-in restaurant context. The public support assistant receives only published guides and conversation. Do not paste business/customer exports, passwords or card details into public support. If a business answer is unsupported, use the source module or contact an authorized manager.
