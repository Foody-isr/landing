## What Is It?

The **Orders** screen is one live list of every order — whether it came from the guest web
app, a QR table, or the POS. From here you track status, accept orders, and send them to
the kitchen.

<span id="section-2" />

### How It Works (Simply)

- POS **Orders → All** reads saved orders across all sources and payment states. Active, Scheduled and Completed remain separate operational views.
- Admin **Orders → All** uses the same restaurant-owned records; its selected dates and filters still apply.
- Payment and preparation are independent: a paid takeaway being prepared remains active.
- Use Active orders to advance fulfillment. All-order details on the POS are read-only.

<span id="section-3" />

## Order flow

1. **Placed** — a paid web/QR order, or a POS order, arrives.
2. **Accepted** — you accept it (or it auto-accepts, depending on your workflow settings).
3. **In kitchen** — sent to preparation; a kitchen ticket can print automatically.
4. **Ready / Completed** — handed to the customer.

> Tip: workflow behavior (auto-accept, auto-send-to-kitchen, auto-print) is configured in
> **Settings**. See [Workflow settings](/en/help/settings/workflow-settings).

<span id="section-4" />

## Good to know

- A prepaid-channel order awaiting payment remains consultable in All. Visibility is not payment approval and does not send food to the kitchen or print a ticket.
- Unsaved local carts are not shared orders. Restaurant access and permissions still apply.
- If a dish sold out while the customer was ordering, checkout blocks it — see [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability).

## Track every saved order across POS and Admin

Open POS → Orders → All, or Admin → Orders → All for the same restaurant. Counter, Admin-entered, website, QR and connected-channel orders share the existing order API; source and dine-in/pickup/delivery are independent filters. The POS All list has no default date, status, source, fulfillment-type or payment filter and loads more records as you scroll. Open a row for read-only items, totals, preparation and payment status. Switching back into All reloads it; pull down on a populated list to refresh, and retry a visible loading error instead of treating it as an empty history. Confirmed live order updates also refresh the retained list. Payment-pending web orders may require this read refresh because they do not emit a kitchen-arrival event. In Admin, widen the period and reset filters when searching for older orders. Do not recreate, recharge or reprint an order merely because it is absent from a filtered view.

<span id="paid-counter-orders" />

## Find a counter sale after payment

A paid takeaway sale sent to the kitchen belongs in Orders → Active while food is being prepared, not Completed merely because payment succeeded. Open the correct takeaway filter and clear other filters if needed. Transactions records the collection separately and refreshes on entry. Payment recorded briefly confirms the collection, then returns automatically to the floor plan. Clearing the current POS bill does not delete the saved order; Orders → All remains available for cross-channel tracking. If paper is missing, verify the profile, current POS/printer association and printer history before reprinting or collecting any additional payment. See [counter checkout](/en/help/payments/pos-checkout#counter-completion). The Client tab uses the order's saved name/phone, not a guessed customer from another bill. Optional contact collection is configured [per fulfillment method](/en/help/settings/pos-behavior#fulfillment-customer). Additional kitchen batches retain coherent contact details without sending a new order-created confirmation for each course; status notifications remain separate.

## Collect a balance and open Sumit invoices

Open the order payment summary. A Partially paid order with a positive Balance due offers Generate payment link, as does a paid order increased after editing. This collection action requires order-management permission and is hidden on cancelled orders. Generating or sharing does not settle the balance. See [payment links and balances](/en/help/payments/payment-links-and-balance) for the full initial/partial-payment steps. If a Sumit document is recorded, Actions → View invoice opens its existing PDF and any supplementary documents; see [Sumit invoices](/en/help/payments/sumit-invoices) for download, email and sharing.
