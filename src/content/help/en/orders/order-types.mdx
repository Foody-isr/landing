<span id="pos-cash-by-order-type" />

## Collect cash for a POS order type

Dine-in and counter pickup orders can be collected in cash only on an enrolled POS with an open cash drawer session. Open More → Reports → Cash drawer balance before taking the first cash payment, then settle the intended order or financial bill. A delivery or online pay-later order is not a recorded cash sale merely because cash is its intended tender; record only cash actually received. Card terminal deposits remain separate.

<span id="weight-hold-margin" />

## Review the by-weight authorization ceiling

For online by-weight orders, open Settings → Payments & VAT with settings.edit. Set Weight hold buffer % from 0 to 100 and save; the default for new restaurants is 20. A failed save keeps the draft. Zero still authorizes the estimate. Only the weight-priced subtotal gets a margin: 100 weighted + 50 fixed at 20% authorizes 170; delivery has no extra margin. Checkout shows the estimate, temporary ceiling and Confirm and authorize, in the restaurant theme. A changed ceiling requires customer review and another confirmation before payment; unknown policy blocks authorization. Existing reviewed checkouts and holds keep their ceiling. Staff confirm measured weights before capture and progression; excess beyond the hold is flagged for a separate payment, never automatically charged. Cash/pay-later makes no hold promise. Requires a configured authorize/capture provider, with partial capture, release and expiry validated before production. This neither changes the catering deposit/balance policy nor enables new-card saving with a CST weighted hold.

<span id="dine-in" />

## Dine-in and tables

Select dine-in and the intended table/session when offered. Check seats and guest count separately; order type does not assign a physical table by itself.

<span id="pickup" />

## Pickup

Enable pickup in the restaurant settings, then check the offered date/time and preparation delay. The customer collects at the configured location.

<span id="delivery" />

## Delivery

Require a usable delivery address and check zone, fee and fulfillment window. A delivery day can be saved before its final time window is confirmed.

<span id="scheduled" />

## Scheduled and batch orders

Review ordering windows, cutoff and fulfillment dates. With a batch calendar, use the restaurant-defined cycle instead of inventing a free date.

<span id="whatsapp-stage-attachment" />

## Choose a stage attachment

In Admin, open Settings → Restaurant → Order workflow and the required pickup or delivery stage. With Notify customer enabled, choose the message type, an approved Meta photo/PDF model and its attachment: a fixed file, or that order’s receipt for a PDF. Choose the sending time and save the workflow. Manual stage entry or its configured event uses this binding. Selecting a file changes neither progression, payment rules nor delivery eligibility. Correct an incompatible or missing model/file before relying on delivery. The model library and creation controls are in Settings → Communications → WhatsApp.

<span id="courier-departure-sync" />

## Follow an assigned delivery’s departure

When the assigned courier starts their route in Admin, unfinished accepted, preparing and ready delivery orders change together to Out for delivery. Staff screens receive the updated statuses through their usual order refresh. This confirms actual departure; assigning a courier or choosing a fulfillment mode alone does not start a route. Held kitchen courses and unsettled weight orders must be resolved first. If starting fails, the previous statuses remain. The route action does not reopen finished or cancelled orders.

<span id="internal-combo-substitution" />

## Make a one-order combo exception

For a one-order combo exception, an authorized Admin user opens the combo step and selects Choose another item. The replacement is included without a surcharge and is saved on that order only. Fulfillment, scheduling and delivery rules remain unchanged. Review the selected dish and portion in the order details. Guest and POS combo pickers retain the configured composition; arrange an exception through the internal Admin order editor. See the combo guide for quantities, eligibility and recovery.

<span id="counter-terminal-payment-before-kitchen" />

## Wait for counter card payment before kitchen preparation

For a new counter pickup order paid by card, choose Pay, then Pay by card. Wait for terminal approval and for Foody to record the payment before preparation and the kitchen ticket start. During collection the payment remains pending and is excluded from the live order board. After a refusal or cancellation, keep the same checkout and retry there; do not send it to the kitchen to bypass payment. If approval succeeded but synchronization failed, reconcile the existing payment before starting another charge. Orders deliberately sent for table service before collection keep their existing send-then-pay flow.

<span id="carte-series-stock" />

## Use the original series stock pool

Predefined item stocks now have a journal under Items & menus → Stock history and on each product. A rotating carte can prepare initial allowances per series and publish them without restoring already committed units. Previous remaining counts are not carried forward. Order changes and pre-production releases use the original frozen pool; a produced item remains consumed. Ingredient/preparation quantities and recipe costs remain separate. See [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) for initial imports, corrections and history filters.

<span id="staff-contact-and-courier-defaults" />

## Staff contact details and courier destination

Staff contact details are managed in Admin → Staff → employee → Edit contact details. The restaurant-scoped name and phone are used for courier assignment and upcoming delivery reminders; the sign-in email remains read-only. Couriers can save a default final destination or link it to their home address. New routes propose that destination and allow an explicit override or removal. It is the courier’s end-of-route destination, separate from the customer’s delivery address. Existing populated routes and completed delivery snapshots keep their saved data. See [staff profiles](/en/help/staff/invite-staff#edit-staff-contacts) for saving, permissions and recovery.

<span id="delivery-test-departure-recovery" />

## Recover a delivery after a test departure

Starting courier mode marks assigned orders Out for delivery. If this was a test departure, a user with order-management permission can open Orders → Deliveries, select the date and choose Undo departure while no arrival, delivery or skipped stop has been recorded. Out for delivery orders return to In preparation; the route returns to draft and can be unassigned and reallocated. This is a delivery correction, not a new order, refund or reprint. Payments and previously sent messages are preserved. See [the dispatch procedure](/en/help/delivery/dispatch-and-routes#undo-departure).

<span id="conditional-item-choices" />

## Check conditional item choices

An item configured with repeated positions requires one separate choice per position according to the selected format. For example, a two-character Number Cake accepts 11, AB or A7. The format price replaces the base price; modifiers add only their configured surcharges. Reducing the position count removes newly hidden choices. Positions remain distinct in the cart, orders and kitchen ticket; review them before confirming. These customizations do not change delivery, availability, recipe, discount or cancellation rules. Configuration requires compatible versions and menu editing permission. See [Modifiers and selection rules](/en/help/menu/modifiers#conditional-positions) for setup and recovery after a rejected selection.
