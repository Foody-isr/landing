Admin → Settings → Orders / POS → order entry

<span id="dine-in" />

## Dine-in and tables

Select dine-in and the intended table/session when offered. Check seats and guest count separately; order type does not assign a physical table by itself.

<span id="pickup" />

## Pickup

Enable pickup in the restaurant settings, then check the offered date/time and preparation delay. The customer collects at the configured location.

<span id="delivery" />

## Delivery

Require a usable delivery address and check zone, fee and fulfillment window. A delivery day can be saved before its final time window is confirmed.

<span id="scheduled" />

## Scheduled and batch orders

Review ordering windows, cutoff and fulfillment dates. With a batch calendar, use the restaurant-defined cycle instead of inventing a free date.

<span id="courier-departure-sync" />

## Follow an assigned delivery’s departure

When the assigned courier starts their route in Admin, unfinished accepted, preparing and ready delivery orders change together to Out for delivery. Staff screens receive the updated statuses through their usual order refresh. This confirms actual departure; assigning a courier or choosing a fulfillment mode alone does not start a route. Held kitchen courses and unsettled weight orders must be resolved first. If starting fails, the previous statuses remain. The route action does not reopen finished or cancelled orders.
