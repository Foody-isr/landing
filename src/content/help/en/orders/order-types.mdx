## Overview

Foody supports four order types. You choose which ones your restaurant accepts from **FoodyAdmin → Restaurant → Workflow Settings**.

---

## Dine-In

Customers sit at a table and order through a staff member on **FoodyPOS**, or by scanning a **QR code** at the table.

- Requires **Table Mode** to be active (see [Service Mode](/help/settings/service-mode)).
- Orders are tied to a table number.
- The order stays open until the bill is closed.

**To enable:** Table Mode is on by default. You can enable QR dine-in from Workflow Settings if you want customers to self-order.

---

## Pickup

Customers order ahead (online or at the counter) and collect at the restaurant. No table assignment.

- Used in both **Counter Mode** (staff takes the order in FoodyPOS) and online via the QR/web ordering page.
- A pickup time can optionally be shown to the customer.
- Configure **Pickup Prep Time** (in minutes) in Workflow Settings so customers know when their order will be ready.

**To enable:** Go to **Restaurant → Workflow Settings** and toggle **Pickup** on.

---

## Delivery

Your restaurant handles its own delivery (or via **Spoke** integration).

- Delivery orders come in through the QR ordering page or a manual entry on FoodyPOS.
- You can configure a minimum order amount for delivery.
- **Spoke integration**: if you use Spoke for delivery, connect your API key in **Settings → Spoke Delivery**.

**To enable:** Go to **Restaurant → Workflow Settings** and toggle **Delivery** on.

---

## Scheduled Orders

Customers choose a specific date and time window for their pickup or delivery order.

- Scheduled orders appear in the **Scheduled Calendar** view on FoodyPOS.
- Each order has a pickup window (e.g., 13:00–13:30).
- Staff can view the day's scheduled orders and prepare accordingly.

**To enable:** Go to **Restaurant → Workflow Settings** and toggle **Scheduled Orders** on.

---

## Which Order Types Should I Enable?

| Restaurant type | Recommended |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Full-service restaurant (dining room) | Dine-in only, or dine-in + QR |
| Café or quick service | Counter Mode + Pickup |
| Restaurant with takeaway | Dine-in + Pickup |
| Delivery operation | Pickup + Delivery + Scheduled |
| Ghost kitchen | Pickup + Delivery + Scheduled |

You can enable multiple order types at once.
