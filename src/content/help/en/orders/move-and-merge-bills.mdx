Open the table bill or a saved item’s Options. Whole-bill movement and selected-item transfer are different actions.

## Availability and access

Advanced actions appear only when the installed POS and API advertise the required capability and your role permits them. Refresh a changed bill before retrying; never create another charge to resolve an uncertain result.

<span id="move-payment-boundary" />

## Move a bill without duplicating cash

Moving or merging a bill changes its table allocation, not the physical cash already counted in a drawer. If a payment is claimed or its result is uncertain, resolve that exact payment before moving the bill. Do not use a move, merge, or payment-method correction to turn a card result into a cash sale; cash collection is recorded only when money is received on an enrolled POS with an open drawer session.

<span id="move" />

## Move a bill

Choose Move, select an authorized destination and read the confirmation before saving. Reopen the destination to verify the bill and covers. Moving a financial bill does not resend food to the kitchen. A payment in progress can block movement. This saved-bill operation is distinct from changing a draft's fulfillment method: the latter never merges into a financial check. Selecting a table with one refuses and cancels the pending draft transfer without changing its source; see [changing a draft's service](/en/help/orders/order-types#pos-change-draft).

<span id="merge" />

## Merge occupied table bills

Choose the supported merge action and an authorized unpaid target, then review both totals/covers. The visible merged bill retains the original pricing policies and food history. Review its new balance before taking a single payment. Paid or actively claimed bills cannot be merged.

<span id="transfer" />

## Transfer saved items

Open the saved item → Options → Move, choose an existing authorized table bill and confirm. A regular quantity line moves its quoted units; isolate one portion with Split first if needed. Covers stay with their bills; moved items begin at Table at the destination. Unsent drafts and named non-table tickets are outside this saved-table path. Kitchen history and stock are not duplicated.

<span id="terminal-check-selection" />

## Select the check on the terminal

On the updated handheld terminal, open Checks with the bottom receipt icon, then use the radio selector for All checks or the intended floor. A free table asks for covers when configured; an occupied table or named check opens its existing context. Verify the displayed table and bill before Actions → Move or an eligible transfer action. The view selector and floor panning neither move nor merge a bill: only confirmation of the actual business action can do so. Dismissing the selector without choosing changes no destination.
