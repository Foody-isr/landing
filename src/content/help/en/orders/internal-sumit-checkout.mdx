Admin → Orders → New order → Checkout

<span id="sumit-internal-prerequisites" />

## Check availability and prepare the basket

These two choices appear only when the restaurant’s active provider is configured Sumit. Creating an order requires orders.manage; entering a card also requires payments.manage. In New order, add items from a carte or the library and select Continue. The ticket becomes Complete order: choose pickup or delivery and the service date, expand discounts if needed, then choose the payment action. The total must be positive. Sumit does not support weighted items requiring authorization. Record a received payment and Pay later remain separate choices; neither is selected by default. For delivery, Delivery fee appears in Fulfillment before the payment choice. Enter zero or a positive amount with at most two decimal places; 0 provides free delivery. This amount remains included in the total when you then choose a payment link, card, received payment or pay later. For a link, leave the field blank to calculate fees from the customer’s address. An entered amount stays fixed by the restaurant; delivery coverage, minimum and availability are still checked. Returning to items preserves the amount during this creation; after reloading a local draft, review and re-enter fees.

<span id="sumit-customer-basket-link" />

## Send the customer a Foody basket link

Choose Payment link, then Create payment link. The confirmation screen shows Awaiting payment; use Copy link. The admin does not have to fill in the customer’s name, phone or address. Copy the private Foody link and share it only with the intended customer. The recipient sees the prepared items, chosen options, total, discount, service date and delivery fee; they enter their own name and phone, optional email, and the delivery address when needed. Items, discounts and service date are prepared by the restaurant. For delivery, enter the address and choose Calculate delivery and total. Foody checks the restaurant’s delivery zone, service availability and minimum, keeps the fee fixed by the restaurant, or calculates it from the address when no amount was fixed, then recalculates applicable discounts, and displays the final total. Review it, then choose Pay securely with Sumit. A changed fee requires another review; the client cannot set their own fee. Contact the restaurant for an unavailable service date. Pickup proceeds directly to Sumit’s hosted form. The link expires 24 hours after creation; the background cancellation process releases an expired unpaid basket’s stock reservations. A paid order offers View order and cannot start another payment. For an address in a zone with delivery weekdays, the retained slot must also fit the restaurant’s delivery hours and slots. An incompatible slot blocks continuation; ask staff to correct the date or address before resuming. This check does not start a new preparation delay for an already prepared basket. For an ordinary order, address-based fees are the default and the fixed-fee input opens only through Set a fixed delivery fee. Leaving automatic pricing selected lets the recipient’s address determine the tariff.

<span id="sumit-shared-storefront-cart" />

## Use the restaurant’s existing cart and theme

The private link opens the same cart page as the restaurant website, with its logo, colors, fonts and published order-page settings. Review the basket, then choose Continue to payment to use the existing checkout form. The restaurant’s published fields and labels are reused; custom answers and notes are saved on this order. Prepared items, prices, discount and service date stay fixed. A personal basket already in the browser is kept separately. This link does not load an account, saved address or saved card. For delivery, calculate and review the final amount; changing the address requires a new calculation. Once payment has started, resuming retains the bound contact details without collecting them again. Previously created Foody links also enter this journey.

<span id="sumit-admin-hosted-card" />

## Enter the card from Admin

Choose Charge a card in Complete order, fill in the customer name and phone and, for delivery, the address. Review the service date and final total, then select Open secure payment once. This creates the order and opens Sumit’s hosted form inside Admin. Enter the card only there; Foody has no card-number or CVV field in this flow. Open the same payment in a new tab is available if the embedded form cannot be used. The order stays pending while the payment is processed; Payment confirmed appears only after the API records Sumit confirmation. Closing the form or a successful page load does not mark the order paid. To resume later, select the saved order in Orders and use Open secure Sumit payment. Resuming opens the same hosted payment, without recreating the order.

<span id="sumit-checkout-recovery" />

## Recover an interrupted checkout

The prepared basket is immutable while pending. To change its items, discount or fulfillment quote, cancel it and prepare a replacement after checking the payment state. Customer details bind on the first payment-opening action; Resume the same payment retains them. If the result is uncertain, check this saved order and Sumit before creating or collecting another payment. A provider or persistence failure may keep the checkout blocked for review. An invalid, expired or closed link, or a provider change away from Sumit, requires contacting the restaurant. These flows do not save a reusable card, send a payment link automatically or confirm kitchen dispatch before payment.

<span id="independent-lifecycle-confirmations" />

## Review independent confirmations

The summary distinguishes recorded collections and refunds, net received and the remaining balance. An unknown value is not zero; a refund does not automatically recreate debt. The summary authorizes no new charge and existing financial flows remain required. See [recorded amounts](/en/help/orders/payment-preparation-and-handoff#recorded-money-summary).

## Move between checkout steps

With the compatible Admin version, add items and choose Continue. Checkout contains Handover and customer, then Payment and confirmation; Event / Catering adds an Event step between them. In Handover and customer, choose pickup or delivery, its date and window, then enter customer details and an address when needed. For an eligible Sumit restaurant, select The customer will complete their details through the Foody link when the recipient should provide those answers: missing contact and event answers then do not block link creation. In Event, enter guests, date and time; other fields are under Additional information. Date, address and contact reuse buttons copy values only after your click; review them after changes. Information to request from the customer groups requirements and permission to change the event date and time. In Payment and confirmation, explicitly choose payment, review any deposit/authorization terms, discount, items and total, then use the final action. Navigating between steps never confirms receipt of money.

Previous and visited step titles let you go back. Continue validates required fields before advancing; selecting a previously visited later step repeats that validation. Entered details, payment choice, fees and discounts remain in memory on return, including Back to items. An applied percentage discount is recalculated for the current items. Changing the received-payment method or amount requires acknowledging receipt again. Reloading a saved local draft does not restore financial exceptions: review them again.

When a step has errors, all affected fields have a red outline and a specific message. The panel focuses the first field; the correction list opens other fields, including collapsed sections or earlier steps. Correct the fields and choose Continue. A time window needs both start and end, with end strictly after start; 12:00–12:00 is rejected. Event time can equal the handover end. A window ending after the event starts shows a warning to review, without imposing an installation delay. Switching from a link to manual payment may require completing customer and event answers.

Production plan stays visible with its date and remaining conditions. On the final step, Add to production plan appears when explicit inclusion is useful and authorized; automatic inclusion is shown in the status. This request never confirms payment or bypasses server event-confirmation requirements. Failed creation preserves the form; check a saved order before retrying an uncertain result.
