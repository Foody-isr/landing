Open the saved table bill → Actions → Split. Choose by cover, item or equal parts where available.

## Availability and access

Advanced actions appear only when the installed POS and API advertise the required capability and your role permits them. Refresh a changed bill before retrying; never create another charge to resolve an uncertain result.

<span id="allocation" />

## Allocate covers or items

By cover creates bills for assigned guests with items and shares multi-guest items. Table items and unused covers remain on the original. By item lets you select and assign items; equal parts divides the selected amount. The displayed plan remains provisional until Save.

<span id="share" />

## Share selected items

Select complete items then Share item(s). Choose equal or custom parts and enter the count or amounts. Complete the exact selected total; review each bill, reductions, fees and covers before Save. Custom item amounts allocate food value and do not collect a payment.

<span id="combine" />

## Recombine saved fractions

Return every unpaid part of the same original unit to its original bill/ledger anchor, then use Regroup when offered. Paid, missing, actively claimed or mixed offered/charged parts cannot be regrouped. Regroup before subdividing a saved fraction again. Back/Discard leaves the saved plan unchanged.
