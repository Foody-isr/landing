Open the saved table bill → Actions → Split. Choose by cover, item or equal parts where available.

## Availability and access

Advanced actions appear only when the installed POS and API advertise the required capability and your role permits them. Refresh a changed bill before retrying; never create another charge to resolve an uncertain result.

<span id="split-cash-collection" />

## Take cash for a split bill

After saving the split, select the exact financial bill and verify its amount. Cash collection requires the same enrolled POS to have an open cash drawer session; the confirmed amount is recorded for that bill and drawer. A claimed cash payment with an unknown outcome must be resolved before the drawer can close. Do not merge or move a bill to work around a pending payment, and do not make a second cash sale for a timeout.

<span id="allocation" />

## Allocate covers or items

By cover creates bills for assigned guests with items and shares multi-guest items. Table items and unused covers remain on the original. By item lets you select and assign items; equal parts divides the selected amount. The displayed plan remains provisional until Save.

<span id="share" />

## Share selected items

Select complete items then Share item(s). Choose equal or custom parts and enter the count or amounts. Complete the exact selected total; review each bill, reductions, fees and covers before Save. Custom item amounts allocate food value and do not collect a payment.

<span id="combine" />

## Recombine saved fractions

Return every unpaid part of the same original unit to its original bill/ledger anchor, then use Regroup when offered. Paid, missing, actively claimed or mixed offered/charged parts cannot be regrouped. Regroup before subdividing a saved fraction again. Back/Discard leaves the saved plan unchanged.

<span id="variant-position-details" />

## Check variant details before allocating

An item’s allocation details retain its variant choices by position. Check the characters and their order before allocating; they add no surcharge to the already recorded amount. Sharing rules and production quantities remain unchanged.

<span id="independent-lifecycle-confirmations" />

## Review independent confirmations

In a saved order’s actions, review preparation and handoff separately from payment. Handoff review selects the actual physical units; financial splitting does not multiply them. Before closure, review the exact check or financial portion and its confirmed balance. A closed scope refuses new commercial changes; another open batch is not closed by that action. See [tracking and confirmations](/en/help/orders/payment-preparation-and-handoff).
