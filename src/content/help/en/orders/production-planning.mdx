## Select the service date

Select the intended service/date and inspect included orders. Compare quantities with the same date basis used by Orders and the dashboard.

## Review item and portion quantities

Review aggregated items, variants, portions and notes before assigning preparation. Recheck the plan after a saved order change.

## Prepare customer packing

Use the customer/order breakdown to organize packing and pickup/delivery handover. Confirm completed preparation with the offered controls; preparation does not confirm payment.
