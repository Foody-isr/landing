Admin → Orders → Production / POS → Scheduled calendar

<span id="scope" />

## Select the service date

Select the intended service/date and inspect included orders. Compare quantities with the same date basis used by Orders and the dashboard. Accepted scheduled cash pickup/delivery orders are included regardless of customer trust. When a card guarantee is required, wait for confirmed bank authorization: an awaiting or unknown guarantee is excluded by default, including for a trusted customer. The existing explicit staff override remains separate. Authorization does not confirm cash receipt: check payment separately. Disabling future cash payments does not remove already accepted orders.

<span id="quantities" />

## Review item and portion quantities

Review aggregated items, variants, portions and notes before assigning preparation. Recheck the plan after a saved order change.

<span id="packing" />

## Prepare customer packing

Use the customer/order breakdown to organize packing and pickup/delivery handover. Confirm completed preparation with the offered controls; preparation does not confirm payment.
