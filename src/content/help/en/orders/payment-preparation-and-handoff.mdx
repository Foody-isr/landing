## Distinguish the check, payment, preparation and handoff

With compatible API, POS, Admin and guest-web versions, review the commercial check, its payment, preparation batches and handoff separately. Paid does not mean ready or served; a refund does not prove that work already performed was cancelled. Ready does not mean handed over. Unestablished information remains unknown: do not infer it from payment or apply current settings to an old order. Successive sends on one check remain separate batches. QR customers at the same table retain individual order IDs and payments even when the POS groups the display. Financial splitting does not produce additional food. Existing table-release settings still apply; one customer paying does not settle the others’ orders.
