Admin → Settings → Order taking → Preorders

<span id="policy" />

## Choose the intake policy

Select whether preordering is disabled, offered alongside other orders, or required using the offered policy controls. Check enabled pickup/delivery services separately.

<span id="slots" />

## Configure date/time slots

Choose the slot calendar and set offered lead time, horizon and fulfillment windows. Test boundary dates in the preview rather than assuming all calendar dates are available.

<span id="batch" />

## Configure a batch cycle

Set ordering opening, cutoff and fulfillment days/windows for the cycle. Review pickup and delivery windows separately and inspect the generated cycle preview.

<span id="prepayment" />

## Check payment and product exceptions

Review the prepayment policy and product-specific exceptions. Save, reopen settings and test the published guest path without making a live payment as a test.
