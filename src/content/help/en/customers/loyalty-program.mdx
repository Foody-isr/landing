In Foody Admin, select the restaurant and open **Customers → Loyalty program**. The overview shows the saved status, how customers earn, and the reward they unlock.

## Availability and access

Use a restaurant account with permission to view settings. Changing the program requires settings edit permission for that restaurant.

<span id="overview" />

## Read the overview

Read the saved reward and when it is earned, for example a free coffee after five visits. Check the minimum purchase, reward validity and enrollment status. **Wallet Studio** opens the card design. **Open the customer loyalty page** opens the restaurant’s storefront. For a promotional code such as LUNCH10, open **Customers → Discounts**.

<span id="configure" />

## Prepare the program

Select **Create program** or **Edit program**. Under **Program rules**, choose visits or points, a minimum eligible purchase and the threshold for a fixed discount or a free product. Under **Enrollment texts**, select each language to enter the name and terms in English, French and Hebrew and enter a terms version. To save a paused program, leave **Program active** off, save and confirm.

<span id="purchase-earning" />

## Choose how a purchase earns points

Under **Combine visits and spend**, add a fixed award per paid visit to a spend-based program or an award per spend step to a visit-based program. A single eligible purchase can earn both. Open **Ways to earn**. Add an item or category from the restaurant catalogue and enter points per unit. An item rule takes precedence over its category. Select the option to count only the selected products for a card such as “buy eight coffees, get one free”. Otherwise, these points add to the visit or spend award. The minimum purchase applies to paid merchandise. Complimentary and voided items and extras do not count as units. Foody combines a member’s portions before rounding. Refunds adjust the award. For a promotion, select the double-point weekdays and, optionally, the start and end dates. Dates use the restaurant time zone. Save and confirm.

<span id="member-activities" />

## Reward member activities

In **Ways to earn**, enable the activities you want and enter the award for each. A member earns the welcome bonus once when accepting the terms. On the loyalty page a signed-in member can answer a one-time satisfaction survey from 1 to 5 and copy a referral link. A friend enrolls through that link. The referrer earns the bonus after the friend’s first qualifying paid purchase. If all qualifying purchases are refunded, the bonus is reversed. Members can enter their birth month and day. Each year the restaurant can grant points or issue the program reward directly. February 29 birthdays are celebrated on February 28 in non-leap years. The same account shows balances and rewards. The restaurant can read the average rating in the overview.

<span id="activate" />

## Activate or pause enrollment

Turn on **Program active**, save and confirm. On the restaurant’s ordering site, customers can open Loyalty from order history and explicitly accept the terms to join. To pause new enrollment and earning, turn the switch off, save and confirm. Existing members can still view their balance and earned rewards. Apple Wallet and Google Wallet availability is managed separately.

<span id="wallet-appearance" />

## Customize the Wallet card

Open **Wallet Studio**. Choose a logo and a banner in PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 10 MB per file. Open **Frame banner** and choose **Entire image** to keep every edge visible or **Fill the frame** to crop. Adjust the zoom and drag in the preview, or use the horizontal and vertical sliders. Apple and Google share the settings until you enable **Use a separate Google frame** in the Google preview. On Apple, place the counter **Below the banner** or **On the banner**. New uploads retain their original file in the private draft. For an older banner, upload the original again if you need its full resolution. Turn off **Show restaurant name** if the logo already includes the name. Google keeps the program title. **Save draft** lets you generate an Apple test card. Select **Publish card design** to apply the saved artwork to customer cards. The card displays **Powered by Foody** near the QR code and includes a Foody link in its details.

<span id="open-loyalty-card" />

## Open and add your personal loyalty card

Use **Loyalty card** in the navigation of the compatible restaurant website. Sign in or create an account, then accept the program terms and join. When an add-to-Wallet button is available, select it from your phone and confirm in Wallet. Sign in with the same account to use your earned rewards in an eligible checkout.

<span id="member-purchase" />

## Earn on the restaurant’s ordering site

After joining, sign in to the same account before placing an order. Eligible orders count after payment is confirmed, under the restaurant’s rules. Open **Loyalty** to see your progress and any earned rewards.

<span id="generate-wallet-from-website" />

## Use the website design on the Wallet card

In **Customers → Loyalty program → Wallet Studio**, click **Generate with AI**. Foody uses your published website colors, logo and one photo for the banner. It tries the homepage hero first, then the cover or a photo from a visible section. When the site has no color, Foody can extract one from the logo or photo. The crop adapts to each Wallet. Check the Apple and Google previews. You can undo the suggestion immediately, change the colors or reframe the banner. An unavailable element keeps its current value. **Save draft** keeps the design for the restaurant. **Publish design** applies it to customer cards.

<span id="preview-card-layouts" />

## Compare layouts with the restaurant’s banner

In **Wallet Studio**, choose a palette or adjust the two colors. After adding a banner, try **Full product**, **Panorama** or **Balance on image**. Each layout uses your image. You can fine-tune the Apple and Google frames afterwards. Move **Sample balance** to check readability in both previews. The slider changes only the simulation. Save the draft, check a test card, then publish the design when ready.

<span id="compose-wallet-manually" />

## Design a card manually

In **Wallet Studio → Card layout**, choose **Table journal** for a photo with a gingham band or **Coffee poster** for a cutout in front of a wave. Upload your logo and photo, or choose **Use website logo** and **Use website photo** to select those assets from the published site. Open **Artwork title** to edit the text, typeface, size, position and angle. **Pattern and background** sets the colors, pattern and divider position. **Photo placement** lets you move the image, trim transparent margins and place it in front of the title. Under **Logo and member information**, choose the balance alone or with its target, then the remaining progress or the program benefit. Compare the **Poster** and **Classic card** Apple previews and open **Google Wallet**. iPhone uses the poster format from iOS 27. Use the sample balance to simulate progress. Select **Save draft**, test the Apple pass, then **Publish design** to release it to customer cards.

<span id="visit-stamp-card" />

## Create a visit stamp card

In **Customers → Loyalty program**, choose visits and set the number needed for a reward. Open **Wallet Studio** and enable **Visit stamp card**. Under **Unfilled stamp**, choose a coffee cup, croissant, burger, pizza slice, sushi, falafel pita, salad, ice cream, cookie, cocktail or star. Each earned visit shows a check mark. Choose the disc and ink colors, then set three to six stamps per row. You can use the restaurant’s photo behind the stamps. Move **Sample balance** to 7 to check a ten-visit card in both previews. Save the draft to download an Apple test card with that sample balance. **Publish design** applies it to member cards. The tenth eligible visit issues the reward and starts the next cycle on the same card. With a large goal, the two rows display the current group of visits and the counter shows overall progress.

<span id="distribute-and-scan" />

## Share the enrollment QR and scan the member’s card

In **Wallet Studio → Customer card**, open **Enrollment QR code** and download the SVG for your counter. Scanning it opens the restaurant website. The customer signs in, accepts the program terms and adds their personal card to an available Wallet. Eligible paid online and delivery orders linked to that account count toward the same card. At the register, open loyalty on the bill before payment. On iPhone, iPad or Android, select **Scan with the camera**, or use a 2D reader. Present one member QR at a time and verify the card before attaching it to the bill. Member lookup displays the balance. An eligible payment credits the visit.

<span id="welcome-reward" />

## Offer a first-visit coupon

Under **Ways to earn → Joining the program**, choose **Give the program reward directly** to issue the configured discount or free item at first enrollment. Signed-in members see it in their rewards and can redeem it on their first eligible order. Signing in again or using another device opens the same card and coupon.

<span id="member-notification-preferences" />

## Choose notifications

A newly available reward can trigger a notification from an installed Wallet when updates and notifications are allowed. To receive restaurant messages, open **Loyalty → Restaurant messages** on the website, select **Receive messages on this device** and grant the browser permission. On iPhone, open the website from its Home Screen icon. Opt in on each device you use. **Turn off messages for this restaurant** withdraws this restaurant subscription on all of the member’s devices and preserves the card and balance.

<span id="member-campaigns" />

## Send a message or set up a reminder

In **Customers → Loyalty program → Member engagement**, an authorized manager can write a one-time message up to 180 characters. Choose all subscribers, those who joined in the last 30 days, those with at least five paid visits, or those without a visit in 30 days. For an automatic reminder, choose 7–180 days of inactivity, a visit milestone or a reward expiring in 1–30 days. To request a Google review once per member, enter the restaurant’s review link. Requests can start at 6 pm local restaurant time on the day of a paid visit. Activate the rule and select **Stop** to stop further sends. The history distinguishes provider-accepted, pending, failed and unconfirmed sends.

<span id="member-engagement-reports" />

## Track return visits and export members

In **Member engagement**, read today’s paid visits, members with at least two visits in 30 days, average time to the second visit and available or redeemed rewards. **Draft a reminder** selects subscribers without a visit in 30 days. Review the message before scheduling it. A manager with access to the program and customer management can select **Export members (CSV)**. The file shows membership, balance, visits and website notification subscription. Contact details come from orders at this restaurant and can be blank.

## If the operation fails

If a save has an unknown outcome, use **Resume original request**. If Foody rejects an outdated edit, reload the current program and review it before saving again. After the first enrollment, the earning mode and accepted terms are locked.
