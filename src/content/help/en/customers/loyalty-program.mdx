In the compatible Foody Admin, select the restaurant and open **Loyalty program** in the sidebar.

## Availability and access

Use a restaurant account with permission to view settings. Changing the program requires settings edit permission for that restaurant.

<span id="configure" />

## Prepare the program

Choose **Points** or **Visits**, the reward threshold and minimum eligible purchase. Choose a fixed discount or a free product. Add the name and terms in English, French and Hebrew and give the terms a version. Save and confirm with **Program active** off to keep it paused.

<span id="activate" />

## Activate or pause enrollment

Turn on **Program active**, save and confirm. On the restaurant’s ordering site, customers can open Loyalty from order history and explicitly accept the terms to join. To pause new enrollment and earning, turn the switch off, save and confirm. Existing members can still view their balance and earned rewards. Apple Wallet and Google Wallet availability is managed separately.

## If the operation fails

If a save has an unknown outcome, use **Resume original request**. If Foody rejects an outdated edit, reload the current program and review it before saving again. After the first enrollment, the earning mode and accepted terms are locked.
