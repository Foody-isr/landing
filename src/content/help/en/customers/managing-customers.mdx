Admin → Clients

## Availability and access

Editing and merging require customer-management permission.

<span id="find" />

## Find a customer

Search the customer list and inspect name, phone, address, order count and last order. Check the intended person before editing.

<span id="edit" />

## Edit delivery details

Open the customer profile, edit the name, address and delivery details, then save. To change a phone, use Correct in the customer list. Recheck the selected order address separately for already-created orders.

<span id="trusted" />

## Authorize cash ordering

Use the trusted/cash-allowed setting only for the intended customer. This controls cash ordering eligibility, not evidence of an actual payment.

<span id="merge" />

## Merge duplicates

Review selected customer profiles and phone numbers before confirming a merge. Check the resulting combined profile and avoid merging different people.

<span id="phone-corrections" />

## Correct one or several phone numbers

With customers.manage, open Clients and use Correct on one row, or select customers and choose Actions → Correct phones. Recognizable formats are prefilled; correct other numbers manually. Review the current number and saved format, then Save. Israeli numbers become +972 without the national trunk zero; for another country enter + and its country code. Letters, extensions, incomplete numbers and invalid prefixes prevent saving. The batch is atomic: a number belonging to another customer or conflicting cash records blocks every correction. Existing order contacts and customer keys are corrected together for future WhatsApp sends; names, addresses, merges and cash eligibility remain intact. Login accounts and other restaurants are unchanged. Errors retain input; if only refreshing fails after a successful save, Retry reloads without repeating the write. Format validation does not verify ownership or WhatsApp registration.
