Admin → Clients

## Availability and access

Editing and merging require customer-management permission.

<span id="find" />

## Find a customer

Search the customer list and inspect name, phone, address, order count and last order. Check the intended person before editing.

<span id="edit" />

## Edit delivery details

Open the customer profile, correct the phone/address and delivery details, then save. Recheck the selected order address separately for already-created orders.

<span id="trusted" />

## Authorize cash ordering

Use the trusted/cash-allowed setting only for the intended customer. This controls cash ordering eligibility, not evidence of an actual payment.

<span id="merge" />

## Merge duplicates

Review selected customer profiles and phone numbers before confirming a merge. Check the resulting combined profile and avoid merging different people.
