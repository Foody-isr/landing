This guide walks you through everything you need to do — in order — to go from a fresh Foody account to a live, working restaurant.

---

## Step 1 — Accept Your Invite & Set Your Password

Foody creates your restaurant account for you. You will receive an **invite email** with a setup link.

1. Click the link in the email.
2. You will be taken to **FoodyAdmin** (the web dashboard).
3. Set your password and confirm.
4. You are now logged in as the restaurant owner.

> **Did not receive the email?** Check your spam folder. If you still can't find it, contact your Foody representative — they can resend the invite link or share it with you directly.

---

## Step 2 — Complete Your Restaurant Profile

In **FoodyAdmin**, go to **Settings → Restaurant Info** and fill in:

- **Restaurant name** — shown on receipts and your QR ordering page
- **Address** — used on receipts
- **Phone number** — for customer contact
- **Description** — a short bio shown on your QR menu

Click **Save** when done.

---

## Step 3 — Choose Your Service Mode

This is the most important configuration decision. Go to **Restaurant → Workflow Settings** and set your **Service Mode**:

| Mode | When to use |
|------|------------|
| **Table Mode** | You have a dining room with assigned tables. Orders are tied to a table number. |
| **Counter Mode** | No table assignment — customers order at the counter or pick up (cafes, quick service). |

You can also enable or disable specific **order types** here:
- **Pickup** — customers order ahead and collect at the counter
- **Delivery** — you handle your own delivery (or via Spoke)
- **Scheduled orders** — customers book an order for a future time slot

> You can change the service mode at any time — it takes effect immediately on FoodyPOS.

---

## Step 4 — Build Your Menu

Go to **Menu** in the sidebar.

### 4a — Create categories
1. Go to **Menu → Categories** → click **Add Category**.
2. Examples: "Starters", "Mains", "Drinks", "Desserts".
3. Set the display order — lower numbers appear first.

### 4b — Add menu items
1. Go to **Menu → Items** → click **New Item**.
2. Fill in: name, price, category, description (optional), photo (optional).
3. Toggle **Available** to control visibility.

### 4c — Create modifiers (optional)
Modifiers let customers customize items (e.g., "No onions", "Extra cheese +₪5").

1. Go to **Menu → Modifiers** → click **Add Modifier Group**.
2. Give it a name (e.g., "Add-ons", "Cooking Level").
3. Add options with optional price adjustments.
4. Mark whether the modifier is **required** (must choose) or **optional**.
5. Link the modifier group to items on each item's edit page.

> **Tip:** Modifier groups are reusable — create one "Salad dressing" group and link it to every salad item.

---

## Step 5 — Set Up Your Floor Plan (Table Mode only)

If you chose **Table Mode**, go to **Restaurant → Floor Plans**.

1. Click **New Floor Plan** and give it a name (e.g., "Main Room", "Terrace").
2. On the canvas, add tables — drag them into position.
3. Name each table (e.g., T1, T2, Window, Bar).
4. Save the floor plan.

You can have multiple floor plans for different areas (indoor, outdoor, bar).

---

## Step 6 — Configure Workflow Settings

Back in **Restaurant → Workflow Settings**, configure how orders flow through your kitchen:

- **Auto Send to Kitchen** — when ON, orders go directly to the kitchen queue the moment they are confirmed. When OFF, a staff member must manually send them.
- **Require Order Approval** — when ON, each incoming order (especially from QR) must be manually accepted before appearing in the kitchen.
- **Tips** — enable to show a tip prompt at checkout.
- **Rush Mode** — enable during busy service to hide estimated wait times.
- **Pickup Prep Time** — set how many minutes it takes to prepare a pickup order (shown to the customer).

---

## Step 7 — Download and Log In to FoodyPOS

FoodyPOS is the iPad/Android app your staff uses at the point of sale.

1. Download **FoodyPOS** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the app and enter your restaurant's credentials (email + password).
3. The app will sync your menu, floor plan, and workflow settings automatically.

From this point, your staff can start taking orders.

---

## Step 8 — Generate QR Codes (optional)

If you want customers to order from their phone (QR dine-in or pickup):

1. In **FoodyAdmin**, go to **Website**.
2. Customize your ordering page (logo, colors, description).
3. Go to **Restaurant → Floor Plans** and open a floor plan.
4. Each table has a QR code you can print or download.
5. Place the QR codes on your tables.

Customers scan the QR → see your branded menu → place their order → it appears directly in FoodyPOS and the kitchen.

---

## Step 9 — Invite Your Team

Go to **Staff** → **Invite Staff Member**.

Fill in name, email, and role:
- **Owner** — full access including billing
- **Manager** — everything except billing and owner settings
- **Staff** — assigned to FoodyPOS app usage (limited admin access)

The invited person receives an email to set their password and log in.

---

## You're Ready

Once Steps 1–7 are complete, your restaurant is live.

**Checklist:**
- [ ] Password set, profile filled in
- [ ] Service mode chosen (table or counter)
- [ ] Menu has at least one category and item
- [ ] Floor plan created (table mode only)
- [ ] Workflow settings configured
- [ ] FoodyPOS installed and logged in
- [ ] QR codes printed (if using QR ordering)
- [ ] Team invited
