Admin sign-in and account menu

<span id="activate" />

## Activate an invitation

Open the received invitation, check the restaurant and finish account setup. Ask the manager to resend an expired invitation.

<span id="restaurant" />

## Select the restaurant

Sign in with your own account, then choose an authorized restaurant. Check its name before editing or exporting data.

<span id="recover" />

## Recover access

Follow the account recovery instructions or contact your manager. Never share a password, activation link or POS code in the assistant.
