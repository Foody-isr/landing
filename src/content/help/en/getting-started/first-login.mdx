## Receiving Your Invite

Foody creates your restaurant account on your behalf. You will receive an invite email from **noreply@foody-pos.co.il** with a link to set your password.

> **Note:** The email is sent to the address you provided to your Foody representative. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

## Setting Your Password

1. Click the **Set up your account** button in the email.
2. You are taken to **FoodyAdmin** (the web dashboard).
3. Enter a secure password and confirm it.
4. Click **Save & Log In**.

You are now logged in as the restaurant owner.

## Logging In to FoodyAdmin

FoodyAdmin is the browser-based dashboard at **admin.foody-pos.co.il**.

1. Open the URL in any browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
2. Enter your email address and password.
3. If your account is associated with multiple restaurants, you will be asked to select one.

## Logging In to FoodyPOS

FoodyPOS is the iPad/Android tablet app.

1. Download **FoodyPOS** from the App Store or Google Play.
2. Open the app.
3. Enter your email address and password (same credentials as FoodyAdmin).
4. The app syncs your menu, floor plan, and settings automatically.

> **Each staff member has their own account.** Invite your team from **FoodyAdmin → Staff** so they can log in independently.

## Logging Out

- **FoodyAdmin**: Click your name in the top-right corner → **Log Out**.
- **FoodyPOS**: Go to **Settings → Account → Log Out**.

## Forgot Your Password?

On the FoodyAdmin login page, click **Forgot password?** and enter your email. You will receive a reset link.
