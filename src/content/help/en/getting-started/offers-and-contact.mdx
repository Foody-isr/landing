Visit foody-pos.co.il, choose French, Hebrew or English, then Products, Pricing, Demo or Contact.

<span id="offers" />

## Compare public offers

Open Pricing for three independent modules per site: Foody Restaurant ILS 590/month excluding VAT, Foody Online 590 and Foody Kitchen 890. Restaurant includes 2 active POS devices and the web interface needed for table QR ordering. Employees have personal accounts and rotate on these devices. Online works independently for remote ordering, with no required POS or printer. Kitchen includes 1 chef and 1 KDS. Restaurant + Online costs 990 with 2 devices; Foody Integral combines all three at 1,790 with 3 devices, 1 chef and 1 KDS. Each additional POS device costs ILS 149/month excluding VAT. A payment-only terminal is not a POS device. Check setup, equipment, payments and options separately; software bundles do not include them. For maps and tours, open Foody Delivery and its quotas. Connecting an existing POS requires a compatibility check and quotation.

<span id="billing-frequency" />

## Compare monthly and annual payment

On the Pricing page that includes this selector, choose Monthly or Annual above the modules. All three modules and combined plans switch together; their features stay the same. Monthly shows the amount billed each month and No commitment. This choice has no minimum term and does not commit you to a year. Annual shows the full amount payable upfront for 12 months, plus the saving compared with 12 monthly payments: one month free, approximately 8.33%. Annual payment is a separate choice, paid upfront for 12 months. Cancellation terms are set out in the quote and contract. Annual pricing applies to the selected module or bundle; additional licences, options, hardware, setup, training and payment services remain separate and do not receive this discount. Prices exclude VAT and are per site. The selector neither subscribes you nor starts a payment. Contact Foody for a quote with the payment schedule; the terms of an already accepted contract still apply.

<span id="contact" />

## Request a demo or support

Use Demo/Contact with the requested business contact details and wait for the success confirmation. The Help center is public at /fr/help, /he/help and /en/help. Its assistant answers published procedures and cannot access or change a restaurant account. Do not send secrets or card data.

<span id="published-catalogue" />

## Read the published catalogue

In the version connected to the commercial catalogue, Pricing displays modules, public plans, their features and included quantities. Prices are in shekels excluding VAT. Account and physical-device licences are separate. Quotation means an amount must be agreed with Foody. Displayed rates belong to the environment being viewed and can take one minute to update after publication. An unavailable catalogue displays a contact message. Catalogue edits do not rewrite an accepted offer; viewing the page starts no payment. Maps Plus remains in preparation.
