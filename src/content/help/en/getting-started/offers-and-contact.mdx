Visit foody-pos.co.il, choose French, Hebrew or English, then Products, Pricing, Demo or Contact.

<span id="offers" />

## Compare public offers

Open Pricing to review Foody Restaurant, Foody Orders and Foody Kitchen. You can buy each module on its own or combine several. Restaurant includes the first employee’s POS licence. Count one extra licence for each additional employee who takes orders or accepts payments. For maps and routes, open Foody Delivery, then View allowances and the Maps Plus extension. Review the one-time setup fees, then the hardware and payment prices. Request a quote for the equipment, options and integrations your setup requires.

<span id="contact" />

## Request a demo or support

Use Demo/Contact with the requested business contact details and wait for the success confirmation. The Help center is public at /fr/help, /he/help and /en/help. Its assistant answers published procedures and cannot access or change a restaurant account. Do not send secrets or card data.
