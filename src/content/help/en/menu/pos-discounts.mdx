Admin → Articles and menus → Staff discounts

<span id="definition" />

## Create a named discount

Create a name and choose fixed amount, fixed percentage, variable amount or variable percentage. Enter a value for fixed definitions and save the active state.

<span id="apply" />

## Apply with permission

Select an active staff discount on the POS and enter the value when variable. Review eligible check/item scope and final balance before confirming.

<span id="distinction" />

## Distinguish online codes

This staff library is separate from guest promotional codes. Do not assume that a staff discount is redeemable online or available to every employee.
