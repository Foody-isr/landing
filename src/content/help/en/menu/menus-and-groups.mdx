Items & menus → Menus: open a menu, then create or edit its groups.

<span id="section-1" />

## What Is It?

Foody separates **what you sell** from **how you show it** — the same idea Square uses, so the
catalogue works for any business.

- **Items** — your global catalogue of dishes/products (created once).
- **Categories** — internal labels for *you* (reporting, kitchen routing). **Customers never see categories.**
- **Menus (cartes)** — a customer-facing menu, e.g. "Breakfast", "Lunch", "Dinner".
- **Groups** — the visual sections inside a menu, e.g. "Salads", "Drinks". **This is what customers see.**

> **The rule that trips everyone up:** an item must be placed in a **group** to appear to
> customers. Being in a category is *not* enough — categories are internal only.

<span id="section-2" />

## The structure

```
Item            (global catalogue — "Caesar Salad")
  └─ belongs to one Category   (internal: "Salads")  ← customers don't see this

Menu / carte    ("Lunch")
  └─ Group      ("Salads")  ← customers DO see this
       └─ contains Items     (the same item can sit in several groups/menus)
```

A group like "Lunch specials" can pull items from several categories. The same item can appear
in several groups and menus.

<span id="section-3" />

## Prepare, save and publish a carte

Open Items & menus → Menus and select the carte. For a rotating carte, choose its series in the header. Add lets you choose items for a group or create a group; Add menu group is also available below the list. A group’s chevron collapses or expands its table without changing the carte. Its “…” menu contains Edit group and Choose items. The “…” menu beside the carte title contains Carte settings and Preview. Group, channel and hours edits stay local. Once edits or a saved draft exist, the bottom bar offers Save draft and Publish. Save stores a private draft; Preview opens a private list that cannot take orders. Publish asks for confirmation and applies the carte and prepared stocks together. Saving alone never changes the live website or POS. Edit POS layout opens the separate tile editor; unsaved changes are guarded before navigating there.

<span id="section-4" />

## Where it sells: online vs POS

Each menu and group has **channel** switches:

- **Web enabled** — shows in the online / QR ordering app for guests.
- **POS enabled** — shows on the staff POS.

So you can run, say, a "Dinner" menu that's web-only, or hide a group from guests but keep it
on the POS. Turn a group off for one channel and it simply doesn't appear there.

<span id="section-5" />

## When it sells: availability hours

A menu (and each group) can **follow your opening hours** or use **custom hours** — e.g. a
"Breakfast" group that only shows 07:00–11:00. Outside its hours, customers don't see it.

<span id="section-6" />

## How this connects to availability

Channels and hours determine whether a group can be shown. Item availability can then make it orderable, sold out or hidden, depending on its rule and manual status. A sold-out item may remain visible with a badge; membership in a group does not guarantee it is orderable. [Availability](../kitchen/availability).

<span id="section-7" />

## Check the publication

Create and save the shared library items first. Add them to the carte draft, check the selected series, web/POS channels, hours and group hiding, then preview and publish. The current series stays unchanged while preparing a future series. A future series without a publication is not served once carte publication is activated. Product names, prices, recipes and active status remain shared library edits; availability switches marked immediate apply immediately and are journaled.

<span id="draft-leave-and-conflict" />

## Leave safely and resolve a conflict

If you leave or change series with unsaved edits, choose Save draft and leave, Discard, or Continue editing. Discard in this dialog drops unsaved edits only; a previously saved draft remains. Discard draft on the carte removes the saved draft after confirmation and keeps the published carte live. If saving or publishing fails, stay in the editor and review the error. Your edits remain available. A stale revision, changed stock setting, invalid quantity or allowance below existing commitments prevents publication. Reload saved draft explicitly to recover the latest server version; it discards unsaved local edits.

<span id="series-sale-stock" />

## Prepare stock for a new series

With menu editing permission, open the rotating carte, select a series and open the “…” menu beside the title → Prepare stocks for this series. Enter INITIAL allowances including quantities already ordered: available = initial minus committed. Use shared units, compatible base measures (g/ml), counts per size, or no predefined limit. Copy previous initial quantities copies allowances, never previous remaining quantities or an old sold-out override. Quantities are shared by the same restaurant item across cartes for that series. Save draft keeps preparation private; Publish applies it atomically without restoring reservations made while editing. The first activation imports current available stock and existing scheduled orders, preserving current remaining quantities. Later series never inherit the old global counter. No predefined limit may still be constrained by a recipe, rule or manual status.

<span id="table-actions" />

## Use the table and item actions

With menu editing permission, select one or more items in a group. The selection bar lets you move them to another group or remove them from this group. A row’s “…” menu also offers Move up, Move down and Remove from group. Dragging reorders rows and groups. These actions edit the draft without deleting shared library items. An item name opens its shared item editor; Select under Modifiers also opens the shared editor; use its Personalizations section to change them. Returning from the editor brings you back to the carte. Leaving unsaved changes offers save, discard or continue editing. Columns show the location, configured channels, modifiers and price; remaining stock appears below the name. Configured channels do not guarantee ordering: hours, active status and availability still apply. On mobile, secondary columns are hidden while name, stock, price and the “…” menu remain accessible.

<span id="contextual-availability" />

## Change availability and read history

In a rotating carte, select the series, then open the item’s “…” menu → Change availability. Choose Automatic (immediate), Always available (immediate) or Sold out (immediate), then Apply. Selecting alone changes nothing. Apply acts immediately on this series independently of the carte draft and is recorded in history. On error the dialog stays open; review the message before retrying. Cancel closes without applying the selection. The same “…” menu opens Stock history for that item. For all items, use the “…” menu beside the carte title → Item stocks, or the matching navigation entry.
