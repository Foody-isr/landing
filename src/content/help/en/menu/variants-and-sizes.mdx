## Understand price and portion

A variant describes a size or version of an item. Its price is an absolute amount replacing the base price; modifiers are additional amounts. Portion describes serving size. Ingredient quantities are entered separately under Recipe and cost, per serving or variant.

<span id="section-2" />

<span id="section-4" />

## Add and save variants

Open the item from Items & menus → Item library and scroll to Price and variants. Edit groups and rows directly in this section. Add a set if needed, enter its title and use Add variant for each size. Fill Name, Price, Portion and Status, then Save in the item header. Do not look for the old Details tab or a Done button to commit all variants.

## Availability, order and combo-only rows

Status offers Available and Unavailable. Arrows reorder rows; the bin removes a row and Remove detaches the group from the item. Combo-only marks rows not sold on their own. Check order and prices before Save. Past orders retain their own sale snapshots.

<span id="section-8" />

## Reuse a saved option set

Click Saved option sets on the relevant group, search and select a set in the picker. Typing a matching group title alone does not apply a set. Review imported rows, prices, portions and status, then save the item. Check other affected items before changing a shared library.

## Connect sizes to the recipe

Under Recipe and cost, enable different quantities per size and fill the variant columns. Example: 200 g meat for Regular and 400 g for Large, with Same quantity for an identical bun. For soup, explicitly enter 250 ml and 500 ml in the columns; Portion text alone does not supply those quantities. Check missing-quantity warnings.

<span id="section-14" />

## Check after saving

Reopen the item to check saved rows, then inspect the enabled POS or web channel. If saving fails, retain the input and retry before closing. [Create an item](./creating-a-menu-item) · [Recipe quantities](./ingredients-and-portions).

<span id="carte-series-stock" />

## Track size stock by series

Carte membership, groups, order, channels and hours now use a private draft: Save draft, Preview, then explicit Publish. Shared product details remain in the item library. On a publication-managed carte, choose item membership from the carte draft instead of the product group picker. Initial quantities for a rotating series are prepared separately from live recipe/ingredient stock. See [Menus and groups](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups) and [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) for preparation, history and recovery.

<span id="related-service-controls" />

## Access the new controls

Carte availability is now directly clickable beneath an item name. When the sale allowance is exhausted, enter the remaining quantity; committed orders are preserved. Review [the availability procedure](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups#contextual-availability) before changing sale stock.
