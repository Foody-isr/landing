## Open the recipe in the continuous editor

Open Items & menus → Item library, select a saved item and scroll to Recipe and cost. This is a section of the page, not a separate Recipe or Cost tab. The recipe links raw ingredients or preparations to quantities used for one sale.

<span id="section-2" />

<span id="section-3" />

<span id="section-17" />

## Add a raw ingredient or preparation

Use Add ingredient above the table. Choose a raw stock ingredient or preparation and its unit. Search for an existing record before creating one. After adding, check its row, quantity and save confirmation. A preparation needs a yield and cost for a reliable calculation.

<span id="section-6" />

## Enter quantity for one serving

Without different quantities per size, enter each ingredient quantity for one serving in a compatible unit. Example: 200 g beef, one bun and 20 g sauce. A missing value is not an automatically calculated quantity. Resolve validation errors before continuing.

## Set quantities per size

For an item with variants, enable different quantities per size. The table shows a column per variant. Enter consumption in each cell. Use Same quantity for an ingredient shared identically by every size. Example: 200 g beef for Regular and 400 g for Large, with one bun in both. The serving portion shown under Price and variants does not replace these entries.

<span id="section-11" />

<span id="section-12" />

## Prefill and check cells

Prefill quantities by size uses a base quantity and your multipliers to fill empty cells. Each cell stays editable. Check every size: an ingredient with no quantity for a variant is not deducted for that variant until corrected. Do not look for an automatic Scale to item size mode instead of entering these cells.

<span id="section-13" />

<span id="section-18" />

## Check units, cost and modifiers

Use units compatible with stock and configure custom recipe-unit conversions on the stock record when needed. The table derives ingredient cost excluding VAT and per-variant totals from entered quantities. Investigate a missing or zero cost on the stock/preparation record instead of treating the recipe as free. Configure add-ons through modifier groups; their price does not fill base recipe quantities.

<span id="section-19" />

## Save and recover from an error

Ingredient edits save immediately; instructions and notes save with the item. An error retains input: retry before leaving and verify the result. If an ingredient record was created but linking it failed, find that record and complete the link without creating a duplicate. [Stock](../kitchen/stock-management) · [Variants](./variants-and-sizes).

<span id="carte-series-stock" />

## Recipe capacity and series sale allowances

Carte membership, groups, order, channels and hours now use a private draft: Save draft, Preview, then explicit Publish. Shared product details remain in the item library. On a publication-managed carte, choose item membership from the carte draft instead of the product group picker. Initial quantities for a rotating series are prepared separately from live recipe/ingredient stock. See [Menus and groups](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups) and [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) for preparation, history and recovery.

<span id="related-service-controls" />

## Access the new controls

Carte availability is now directly clickable beneath an item name. When the sale allowance is exhausted, enter the remaining quantity; committed orders are preserved. Review [the availability procedure](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups#contextual-availability) before changing sale stock.
