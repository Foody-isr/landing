Menu → Items → Create item opens the continuous full-screen editor.

<span id="section-1" />

<span id="section-2" />

## Check access and open the editor

Check the active restaurant and your menu editing permission. Open Menu → Items and click Create item. This is one continuous full-screen form: scroll through its sections. It does not use the old three-tab Details, Recipe and Cost modal. Save stays available in the header.

<span id="section-3" />

<span id="section-4" />

<span id="section-5" />

## Item information and photo

Under Item information, choose the type, enter its name and description, and use Add photo to choose an image file. Selecting a file does not yet save the item. Pasting an image URL is not a field in this form. Open an existing photo preview to use its replacement action. Translation editing is available for saved items.

<span id="section-6" />

<span id="section-8" />

## Classification and distribution

Use the Distribution side area for the internal category, Active status and menu groups. Selected groups are assigned after the item is created. Categories organize the catalogue and reporting; groups structure the customer menu. An active item without a group is not automatically visible.

<span id="section-7" />

<span id="section-9" />

## Price and variants

Price and variants contains selling price and sizes. A simple item requires a positive selling price. A by-weight item requires its per-kilogram price and the requested estimated weight. Variant prices are absolute and replace the base price; modifier prices are additional amounts. Portion describes serving size; it does not automatically fill recipe ingredient quantities.

<span id="section-10" />

## Personalizations and other sections

For a standard item, Personalizations holds modifiers and customer notes; checkout customization controls selection behavior on the POS. Customer information contains customer-facing facts. Stock & availability and Recipe and cost follow on the same page. AI assistant context is private guidance for the ordering assistant, separate from support. A combo uses Composition instead of the standard item recipe sections.

<span id="section-11" />

## Save, then complete the recipe

Click Save. Before the first save, Recipe and cost and Stock & availability offer to save and configure because these settings require a created item. Reopen it and scroll to Recipe and cost. Ingredient edits save immediately; recipe instructions and notes save with the item. Wait for confirmation and retry an error without creating another item.

<span id="section-12" />

<span id="section-13" />

<span id="section-14" />

## Verify visibility and resolve a missing item

Check group membership, menu/group channels, hours and availability, then inspect the relevant website or POS. If Save is disabled, check permission, name and required price. If creation is only partially complete, keep the editor and retry the remaining steps rather than recreating the item.
