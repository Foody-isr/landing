Admin → Items & menus → Modifier Sets

<span id="group" />

## Create a reusable group

Create a named option/modifier group and add its choices. Set required/optional selection and allowed minimum/maximum counts, then save.

<span id="pricing" />

## Set price adjustments

Enter modifier price deltas relative to the item price. Variant prices are absolute replacements; do not use the two interchangeably. Options is a separate library entry; see [Variants and sizes](./variants-and-sizes) for size sets and their absolute prices.

<span id="link" />

## Link groups to items

Open the item editor, attach the reusable groups and order the choices. Verify defaults, selection limits and the final cart price on each enabled channel.

<span id="notes" />

## Configure guest notes

Where a choice offers a customer-note prompt, configure its label/requirement and test the guest form. Guest-only prompts do not require staff to complete the same fields.

<span id="carte-series-stock" />

## Shared choices and carte publication

Carte membership, groups, order, channels and hours now use a private draft: Save draft, Preview, then explicit Publish. Shared product details remain in the item library. On a publication-managed carte, choose item membership from the carte draft instead of the product group picker. Initial quantities for a rotating series are prepared separately from live recipe/ingredient stock. See [Menus and groups](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups) and [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) for preparation, history and recovery.

<span id="related-service-controls" />

## Access the new controls

Carte availability is now directly clickable beneath an item name. When the sale allowance is exhausted, enter the remaining quantity; committed orders are preserved. Review [the availability procedure](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups#contextual-availability) before changing sale stock.

<span id="library-availability" />

## Change availability from Items

The Items library Availability column also opens the small panel shared with Cartes. Choose a status or remaining stock and Apply; simply opening it changes nothing. The current series is displayed when applicable. See [Categories and items](/en/help/menu/categories-and-items#library-availability) for choices, per-size quantities, cancellation and error recovery. Full item settings retain their own Save action.

<span id="conditional-positions" />

## Condition actual modifier groups

Conditions remain available for actual modifiers, such as offering an extra only for a particular format. In the item, under Customizations, attach the group and open Conditions and display. Under Show this group according to, choose an independent priced format or an independent single-choice modifier group. Set its position count per choice: 0 or an unlisted choice hides the group, 1 shows it once, and 2–12 creates required single-choice positions. Save within this panel; these rules apply only to this item. Prices remain deltas added to the item or format price. Sources cannot themselves be conditional; remove dependent conditions before detaching one. For Number Cake characters use option groups under [Variants and sizes](./variants-and-sizes#conditional-positions), preserving their role as variant dimensions without surcharges. Existing modifier groups are not converted automatically.
