Item editor → combo configuration / POS combo entry

<span id="steps" />

## Define the formula steps

Open the combo item in Admin and go to Composition. Select New step and give it a name. Use Item list to choose specific items and eligible sizes, or Menu group to follow a group published on a carte. For a menu group, choose a shared catalogue size to impose it, or select All sizes and open Limits per size. Set the minimum and maximum picks, check the choices in the step and save the item. Use the step arrows to adjust the order.

<span id="shared-catalogue-sizes" />

## Reuse a catalogue size across items

In Admin, open Options and choose an option set. Expand Catalogue sizes. Choose New size, enter a name such as 250 g, and save. In each matching option row, select the shared size. In the combo, open a menu-group step and select the catalogue size. To rename it everywhere, select the existing size under Catalogue sizes, edit its name and save. Open the combo preview to check the choices. Different selling options can use the same size and keep their own prices and stock.

<span id="unavailable-choices" />

## Check a step with no available choices

Open the affected step in Composition. For a menu group, check that the correct carte and group have been published, that the items belong to the current series and that they have stock in the required size. If the required size has changed, choose the current size and check the preview before saving. In an item list, check the selected items and sizes and whether a choice can be offered outside its carte. Open the combo on the ordering channel to confirm that the required step can be completed.

<span id="price" />

## Review formula pricing

Review base/formula prices and choice supplements in the pricing preview. Verify the resulting basket for several allowed combinations instead of assuming every choice has the same price.

<span id="order" />

## Take a combo order

Select the combo and complete every required step. On a compact terminal with limited available width, Cancel Combo and Previous Step occupy the first row; Next Step or Add to order spans the row below. Previous Step appears after the first step. Next Step remains unavailable while the current choice is incomplete; Add requires every step to be satisfied. Scroll the grid to reach further choices. Review components, note and price before adding; an eligible existing-line edit is confirmed with Done. Cancel discards provisional choices without adding the combo. Tablets and sufficiently wide areas retain a single row of buttons. This adaptation changes neither selection limits, surcharges nor permissions. Check kitchen presentation separately on the intended channel.

<span id="internal-combo-substitution" />

## Make a one-order combo exception

With orders.manage permission, open New order or edit a saved order in Admin and open its combo composition. In the relevant step, select Choose another item. Remove one selected unit first if the step is full, search the restaurant article library and choose the replacement and its size. Exception · included identifies the choice. Active fixed-price individual items are eligible, including items outside the active carte; inactive, sold-out and weight-priced items and other combos cannot be used. Required step quantities, per-item limits and configured portions still apply. Save the combo, review the ticket and submit once. For example, replace the beef in a two-main Mamie formula with fish while retaining chicken. The replacement and its size add no surcharge; other normal configured choices keep their existing surcharges. The formula base price and saved definition do not change, and editing an existing order preserves its original base-price snapshot. The choice survives draft restoration and reopening the order editor. Guests cannot request this exception. If rejected, refresh availability and choose an active item in an allowed size; a failed saved-order replacement retains the previous composition.

<span id="website-composition" />

## Build a combo on the restaurant website

Open a combo in the website menu. Its sheet uses the item-detail color style, site fonts, corners and button shape. Choose the quantity when offered, then Build your combo. The catalogue keeps its presentation throughout composition; only eligible, available items for the current step can be selected. Tap an item, then choose a variant if requested. The counter shows your picks; the − button removes a pick. Previous step lets you review a choice, and View details opens the sheet without clearing the composition. Complete the steps and add the combo to the cart. Cancel leaves composition without adding a combo. A fixed combo lists its contents and adds directly from its sheet. If a choice is disabled, check its step membership and availability. For incorrect colors, check the item-detail style assigned in the order page and publish the reviewed settings.
