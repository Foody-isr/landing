## Where to Manage Your Menu

Menu editing is done in **FoodyAdmin** (the web dashboard). FoodyPOS displays the menu for ordering but cannot edit it.

---

## Creating a Category

Categories organize your menu for customers (e.g., "Starters", "Mains", "Drinks").

1. In FoodyAdmin, go to **Menu → Categories**.
2. Click **Add Category**.
3. Enter the category **name** (shown to customers).
4. Optionally add a **description**.
5. Set the **display order**: lower numbers appear first.
6. Click **Save**.

To **hide a category temporarily** (e.g., seasonal menu): edit the category and toggle **Visible** off.

To **reorder categories**: drag them in the list.

---

## Adding a Menu Item

1. Go to **Menu → Items** → click **New Item**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name**: required
   - **Category**: which section it belongs to
   - **Price**: in ₪
3. Optionally add:
   - **Description**: a short text shown to customers
   - **Photo**: JPG or PNG, max 5 MB
4. Click **Save**.

### Hiding an Item

Toggle **Available** to off to hide the item without deleting it. Useful when an ingredient runs out mid-service.

---

## Modifiers

Modifiers let customers customize items. See [Setting Up Modifiers](/help/menu/modifiers) for a full guide.

---

## Combos

A combo is a bundle of items sold together at a fixed price (e.g., "Burger + Fries + Drink , ₪65").

1. Go to **Menu → Combos** → click **New Combo**.
2. Add the items that make up the combo.
3. Set the combo price.
4. Save.

Combos appear on FoodyPOS and the QR ordering page just like regular items.

---

## Weekly Menu Rotation

If your menu changes by day of week, use **Menu → Rotation** to schedule which items are available on which days. Items outside their rotation window are automatically hidden.
