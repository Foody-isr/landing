Items & menus → Item library: catalogue library, filters and actions.

<span id="section-1" />

## Use the Items library

Items is a table-based library with search, filters and actions. There are no Total Items or Out of Stock KPI cards above this list. Categories classify the internal catalogue and reporting; they are not the customer menu sections.

<span id="filters" />

## Search, filter and choose columns

Search by name. Category and Status filters combine; Status offers Active and Inactive, with active items selected initially. All Filters opens the filter panel. Calculated availability is separate from active/inactive status. The header column picker controls Images, Category, Availability and Price. Click Name or Price to sort. The list is paginated.

<span id="edit" />

## Create, edit and apply bulk actions

With menu editing permission, click Create item to open the full-screen editor, or click an existing row to edit it. Actions includes imports and refresh. After selecting rows, it also offers category, option and modifier assignment, availability overrides and confirmed deletion. Check the selected items and restaurant before a bulk action.

<span id="section-2" />

## Show an item to customers

Carte membership, groups, order, channels and hours now use a private draft: Save draft, Preview, then explicit Publish. Shared product details remain in the item library. On a publication-managed carte, choose item membership from the carte draft instead of the product group picker. Initial quantities for a rotating series are prepared separately from live recipe/ingredient stock. See [Menus and groups](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups) and [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) for preparation, history and recovery.
