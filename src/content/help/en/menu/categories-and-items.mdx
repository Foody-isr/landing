Items & menus → Item library: catalogue library, filters and actions.

## Use the Items library

Items is a table-based library with search, filters and actions. There are no Total Items or Out of Stock KPI cards above this list. Categories classify the internal catalogue and reporting; they are not the customer menu sections.

<span id="filters" />

## Search, filter and choose columns

Search by name. Category and Status filters combine; Status offers Active and Inactive, with active items selected initially. All Filters opens the filter panel. Calculated availability is separate from active/inactive status. The header column picker controls Images, Category, Availability and Price. Click Name or Price to sort. The list is paginated.

<span id="edit" />

## Create, edit and apply bulk actions

With menu editing permission, click Create item to open the full-screen editor, or click an existing row to edit it. Actions includes imports and refresh. After selecting rows, it also offers category, option and modifier assignment, availability overrides and confirmed deletion. Check the selected items and restaurant before a bulk action.

## Show an item to customers

Carte membership, groups, order, channels and hours now use a private draft: Save draft, Preview, then explicit Publish. Shared product details remain in the item library. On a publication-managed carte, choose item membership from the carte draft instead of the product group picker. Initial quantities for a rotating series are prepared separately from live recipe/ingredient stock. See [Menus and groups](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups) and [Availability](/en/help/kitchen/availability) for preparation, history and recovery.

## Access the new controls

Carte availability is now directly clickable beneath an item name. When the sale allowance is exhausted, enter the remaining quantity; committed orders are preserved. Review [the availability procedure](/en/help/menu/menus-and-groups#contextual-availability) before changing sale stock.

<span id="library-availability" />

## Change availability from Items

In Items, with menu editing permission, click an active item’s badge in the Availability column. The same small panel as in Cartes opens; opening it neither changes availability nor navigates to the item editor. Choose Available without limit, Unavailable, Predefined stock or Automatic (existing rules), then Apply. Predefined stock requires a positive remaining quantity, in units, base g/ml for measured stock, or per size. An empty size field keeps that size unlimited. Existing orders stay reserved. For a restaurant using series, the panel displays the current series resolved by the server and changes only its stock; prepare another series in its carte. Outside series, it changes the item’s stock and saves all size counters together. Changes take effect immediately after Apply, independently of carte drafts. Cancel, Escape or dismissing the panel discards unapplied choices. Fresh stock loads on opening; if loading fails, Reload stocks is available and applying is blocked. A save error keeps entered values: reload stock, review the quantity and explicitly Apply again. Inactive items and users without editing permission have a read-only status.

## Check conditional item choices

An item configured with repeated positions requires one separate choice per position according to the selected format. For example, a two-character Number Cake accepts 11, AB or A7. The format price replaces the base price; modifiers add only their configured surcharges. Reducing the position count removes newly hidden choices. Positions remain distinct in the cart, orders and kitchen ticket; review them before confirming. These customizations do not change delivery, availability, recipe, discount or cancellation rules. Configuration requires compatible versions and menu editing permission. See [Variants and sizes](/en/help/menu/variants-and-sizes#conditional-positions) for setup and recovery after a rejected selection.
