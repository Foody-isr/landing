Organize related groups in a published menu and use them on the ordering page and the POS.

<span id="group-hierarchy" />

## Create and publish a subgroup

Open a menu in Items & menus → Menus. Use the parent group’s actions and choose **Add a subgroup**, then name it and add its items. To nest an existing group, open **Edit group** and select its **Parent group**. Expand a parent to see its subgroups and use the move controls to reorder groups within the same level. Use **Preview** to review the arrangement, then save and publish the draft. To move a subgroup back to the top level, choose **None** as its parent and publish the change.

<span id="pos-group-layout" />

## Arrange subgroup tiles on the POS

The default POS grid opens at the top level. Tap a group to see its direct items and subgroup tiles, then tap Back to go up one level. For a custom layout, open **POS display** from the menu in Admin. Select a group tile and open the group, then arrange its tiles or use **Add a tile**. Save the layout and check it on the POS. A level with saved tiles uses those tiles. If an expected subgroup is missing there, add its tile and verify that the group is published and visible on the POS.

<span id="group-and-pos-images" />

## Upload a group or POS tile image

With permission to edit the menu, open **Items & menus → Menus**, then choose **… → Edit group**. Under **Group image URL**, click **Upload an image** or **Change image**. Uploading fills the field. You can also paste a URL. Save the draft to keep the image and publish the menu to apply it to the group.

For a tile background, open **POS display**, select the tile and choose **Background → Image**. Upload a file, use a suggested image or paste a URL, then click **Save**. JPEG, PNG, GIF and WebP files up to 5 MB are supported. If the upload fails, the field keeps its previous URL and you can retry with a supported file.
