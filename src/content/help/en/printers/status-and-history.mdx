Open the configured printer’s detail, then Pending document printing → View history.

<span id="status" />

## Check connectivity

Check explicit online/offline/unknown status, power, paper and network. Unknown is not Ready. Star CloudPRNT, compatible Epson/gateway and native Victa paths depend on the actual configured model/transport; USB, Bluetooth or AirPrint are not universally supported.

<span id="history" />

## Read the last 24 hours

View the paginated last-24-hour history and inspect job kind/state/times. It is read-only and excludes ticket/customer content. Refresh does not claim or reprint jobs. For uncertain printing, reconcile the queued job before requesting another ticket to avoid duplicates.

<span id="whatsapp-pdf" />

## Distinguish WhatsApp PDFs from print jobs

A receipt PDF attached to a WhatsApp automation is a digital order summary. It uses saved items and totals but creates no print job, requires no online printer and does not appear in printer history. Configure it in Communications → WhatsApp or Order workflow. Existing paper receipts, profiles, diagnostics and printing rules remain configured in the printing screens.

<span id="variant-position-labels" />

## Check printed variant positions

For an item with variant positions, check that the ticket lists each choice in order, for example Character 1: A then Character 2: 7. These choices describe the product and carry no addition/removal sign or surcharge. If a detail is missing, check the API and ordering client versions, then the item configuration under [Variants and sizes](../menu/variants-and-sizes#conditional-positions). Connection, routing and print recovery procedures remain the same.
