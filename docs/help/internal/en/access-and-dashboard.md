Internal Foody operators only. Open Backoffice in the intended environment and authenticate with an authorized account.

<span id="access" />

## Verify operator scope

Confirm environment and operator role before accessing restaurant data. Do not use a restaurant owner account as an implicit superadmin. Log out from shared devices and never copy credentials or customer records into the help catalogue.

<span id="dashboard" />

## Read platform totals

Review dashboard totals and navigate to the specific restaurant/user record for investigation. Treat aggregates as operational observations, not as customer testimonials or proof of individual payment settlement.
