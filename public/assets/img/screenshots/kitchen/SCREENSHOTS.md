# Kitchen help screenshots — capture checklist

Drop PNGs into this directory with the filenames below. Articles reference them as
`/assets/img/screenshots/kitchen/<filename>.png`. Until a PNG lands, the article shows a
broken-alt box; the text still reads fine.

**Target aspect**: ~1000 × 500 px. Zoom to 100%. Dark mode preferred. Mask any real prices
you don't want public.

## For `availability.mdx`

| # | Filename | What to pre-fill & capture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `availability-dish-panel.png` | Article editor → open a dish that **has a recipe** → **Recette** tab → the **Disponibilité** panel. Show the live readout ("Réalisable maintenant : N portions, limité par …"), the rule dropdown, and the Auto / Toujours dispo / Forcer épuisé buttons. |
| 2 | `availability-rules-library.png` | **Cuisine → Disponibilité** rules library showing the Standard / Made fresh daily / Always available cards, one marked **Par défaut**. (Capture after the dedupe fix ships so each rule appears once.) |

## For `preparations.mdx`

| # | Filename | What to pre-fill & capture |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | `prep-editor.png` | **Cuisine → Préparations** → open a prep (e.g. a tahini/sauce). Show its raw-ingredient recipe and the **yield per batch** field. |

## Tips

- Prefer dark mode (matches the product).
- Keep PNGs under ~200 KB (re-compress with pngquant if larger).
