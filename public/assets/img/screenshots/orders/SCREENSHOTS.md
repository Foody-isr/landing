# Orders help screenshots — capture checklist

Drop PNGs into this directory with the filenames below. Articles reference them as
`/assets/img/screenshots/orders/<filename>.png`. Until a PNG lands, the article shows a
broken-alt box; the text still reads fine.

**Target aspect**: ~1000 × 500 px. Zoom to 100%. Dark mode preferred. Mask real customer data.

## For `managing-orders.mdx`

| # | Filename | What to pre-fill & capture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `orders-list.png` | **Orders** screen with a few sample orders across statuses, the status tabs with counts visible, and the **Live** badge. Crop so the list + tabs are recognizable. |

## Tips

- Prefer dark mode (matches the product).
- Keep PNGs under ~200 KB.
